What is Doctor's ED Solution and how is it meant to work?

Doctor's ED Solution is a digital program, not a medicine. It is presented by Dr. Umer Khan, who describes himself on the sales page as an internal medicine specialist, and it combines three ideas: exercises, food and tracking. There is nothing to swallow and nothing to wear.

The program includes a Fast Start guide, exercise routines, a superfood plan with recipes, smoothie recipes and a tracking guide. One of the first exercises it recommends is training the PC muscle, part of the pelvic floor. The sales page also mentions the herb Tribulus terrestris as part of the food and supplement plan.

Several of these ideas have real support in the research. Pelvic floor exercises, healthy weight and activity have all been tested in randomized trials in men with erectile dysfunction, as we explain below.

Does the approach behind it work? What the research shows

We could not find a published trial of Doctor's ED Solution itself, so we looked at the research on the ideas it uses.

  • In a randomized trial of 55 men with erectile dysfunction, those who did pelvic floor exercises with biofeedback and lifestyle advice improved their erectile function score by 6.74 points more than men given lifestyle advice only after 3 months, and 22 of the 55 men (40%) reached normal function by the end of the study.[1]
  • In a randomized trial of 110 obese men aged 35 to 55, detailed advice on losing weight and exercising more led to bigger drops in body weight and inflammation markers than general advice, and the authors concluded that lifestyle change was associated with better sexual function in about a third of men.[2]
  • A 2026 meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials found that Tribulus terrestris improved erectile function scores more than placebo, with a similar rate of side effects.[3]

What this means for you: exercises, a healthier weight and an active lifestyle are some of the best-supported drug-free steps for erectile function, and this program organises them into a 21-day plan. Results vary, and ED can also be a sign of heart or hormone issues, so it is sensible to mention it to your doctor.[1]

How to get the most out of Doctor's ED Solution

  1. Read the Fast Start guide first so you know the whole plan before you begin.
  2. Learn the pelvic floor exercise properly. Stopping the flow of urine once is a way to find the muscle, but do not make it a regular habit while urinating.
  3. Be consistent for the 21 days, and consider repeating the program, as the page suggests. Trials of pelvic floor training measured results after 3 to 6 months.[1]
  4. Use the tracking guide to note changes in confidence, energy and erection quality.
  5. Work on the basics: weight, activity, sleep and stress all matter for erectile function.[2]

Is Doctor's ED Solution safe?

The exercise and food parts of the program are gentle for most healthy men, and the program is drug-free. Erectile problems can have medical causes, so a doctor's check is worthwhile.

  • Check with your doctor first if you have heart disease, diabetes, high blood pressure, or take nitrate or blood pressure medicine.
  • Check with your doctor before using any herb or supplement the program mentions, including Tribulus terrestris, because supplements can interact with medicines.
  • ED that starts suddenly, or comes with chest pain, shortness of breath, or pain in the penis, needs medical attention.
  • Do not stop prescribed medicine because of any program without talking to your doctor.
  • The program is not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, and nothing here replaces medical care.
