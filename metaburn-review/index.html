What is MetaBurn and how is it meant to work?

MetaBurn is a daily capsule that combines plant extracts, a soluble fiber and a gut bacterium. The official page markets it for support with metabolism, appetite, digestion and gut balance, and says it contains 11 ingredients; eight of them are named on the page.

Instead of relying on caffeine, MetaBurn takes a gut-and-metabolism approach. Konjac fiber is a soluble fiber that swells with water, berberine and cinnamon are widely studied for blood-sugar and metabolic support, and Akkermansia muciniphila is a gut bacterium that scientists have been studying for its link to metabolic health.

MetaBurn is not a diet on its own. It is meant to sit alongside balanced meals, sleep and movement.

Does MetaBurn work?

Several of MetaBurn's ingredients have genuine human research behind them, although the effects in studies are modest:

  • In a 3-month pilot trial with 40 overweight and obese volunteers, pasteurized Akkermansia was safe and well tolerated and improved insulin sensitivity and cholesterol compared with placebo.[2]
  • A systematic review and meta-analysis of placebo-controlled trials found that glucomannan (konjac fiber) was associated with about 1.3 kg more weight loss than placebo.[3]
  • A 2025 meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials has examined berberine across the components of metabolic syndrome.[4]

What this means for you: MetaBurn combines ingredients that science has looked at closely for metabolism and gut health. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a supportive extra and give it a few weeks of daily use alongside balanced meals and activity.

How to get the most out of MetaBurn

  1. Follow the label on your bottle. The official page does not give dosing directions.
  2. Take it consistently, for example with breakfast, so it becomes part of your routine.
  3. Drink plenty of water, since konjac fiber works best with fluids.
  4. Pair it with the basics: balanced meals, regular movement and good sleep. No capsule replaces these.
  5. Track simple things such as appetite, energy and how you feel, so you can decide before the 90 days are up.

Is MetaBurn safe? Side effects

MetaBurn is stimulant-free and its ingredients are generally well tolerated. The most common effects of fiber and berberine are mild and digestive, such as bloating or a change in bowel habits as your body adjusts.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take medicine for diabetes, blood pressure or cholesterol, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first. Berberine and cinnamon can affect blood sugar.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor if you have persistent stomach pain, unexplained weight changes or symptoms that worry you.

MetaBurn FAQ

Does MetaBurn work?

MetaBurn contains ingredients that have been studied for weight, appetite and metabolic health, such as konjac fiber, berberine and Akkermansia. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, effects in studies are modest, and results vary from person to person. It works best alongside balanced meals and regular activity.
