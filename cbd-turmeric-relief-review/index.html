What is CBD Turmeric RELIEF and how is it meant to work?

CBD Turmeric RELIEF is a plant-based liquid that combines two well-known botanicals: full-spectrum hemp extract containing CBD, and concentrated turmeric extract. You measure one dropperful and take it as part of your day. The sales page positions it for restful sleep and a calmer evening.

Do CBD and turmeric products like this work?

The research is still developing, but the early signs for CBD and sleep are encouraging, and turmeric is among the most studied botanicals:

  • In a case series of 72 adults with anxiety or poor sleep, sleep scores improved within the first month in about two thirds of patients taking CBD, although scores fluctuated over time. It was a chart review without a control group.[2]
  • A 2025 scoping review found that cannabinoids, particularly CBD, showed potential for easing anxiety and sleep disturbances, and called for better-designed trials and standardized dosing.[3]
  • A review of curcumin's effects on human health attributes most of its benefits to antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity, and notes that absorption is a challenge, which is why formulation matters.[4]

What this means for you: CBD and turmeric are both well-chosen, widely studied ingredients, and a clearly dosed liquid makes it easy to try them sensibly. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks and notice how you sleep and feel.
