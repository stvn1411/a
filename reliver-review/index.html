What is RELIVER and how is it meant to work?

RELIVER, sold on its own website as Reliver Pro, is a capsule formula built around the idea that the liver benefits from a steady supply of certain plant compounds and nutrients. It combines herbs that have long been used for digestion and liver wellness, such as milk thistle, dandelion root, artichoke and chanca piedra, with newer research favourites like berberine and N-acetyl-cysteine.

The maker positions it for people who want to support liver function, energy and a healthy waistline. We treat the weight and energy language as the maker's positioning. What we can verify is the ingredient list and what published research says about the key ingredients, and we cover both below.

The practical appeal is a clear, named formula, two capsules a day, a one-time payment and a generous refund policy.

Does RELIVER work?

The formula as a whole has not had its own clinical trial, but its two best-known ingredients have human research behind them:

  • A 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of 26 randomized trials of silymarin in fatty liver disease reported lower ALT and AST liver enzymes, while noting that larger confirmatory trials are needed.[1]
  • A 48-week placebo-controlled trial of silymarin in 99 people with NASH did not find a significantly larger drop in disease activity score than placebo, so the evidence for liver-enzyme changes is stronger than for liver disease outcomes.[1]
  • A three-centre trial of 184 people with fatty liver compared lifestyle changes alone, with pioglitazone and with berberine, measuring liver fat, blood sugar, lipids and enzymes.[2]

The studies listed at the bottom of the sales page cover topics such as digestion and weight loss in general; we based this review on the studies we read ourselves.

What this means for you: RELIVER combines ingredients with real human research, especially silymarin and berberine, with traditional liver-friendly herbs. It is best seen as a supportive extra beside a balanced diet, regular activity and moderate alcohol intake. The finished formula has not been trialled on its own, and results vary.

How to get the most out of RELIVER

  1. Take two capsules a day with a large glass of water, as the maker directs.
  2. Give it at least 60 days, which is the time the maker suggests.
  3. Build the basics first: plenty of vegetables, fewer sugary drinks and less alcohol, and regular movement.
  4. Keep your doctor in the loop, especially if you take regular medicine.
  5. Track how you feel and any routine blood tests your doctor orders.

Is RELIVER safe? Side effects

Herbal formulas are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and RELIVER contains no stimulants. Mild digestive effects such as loose stools or an upset stomach are the most commonly reported with ingredients like berberine and artichoke.

  • Berberine can lower blood sugar and interact with some medicines, so check with your doctor first if you take diabetes, blood pressure or blood-thinning medicine.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, have gallstones or bile-duct problems, or have a liver condition, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[3]
  • See a doctor promptly for yellowing of the skin or eyes, dark urine or persistent pain in the upper right abdomen.
