What is The Olive Reset and how is it meant to work?

The Olive Reset is a web app that opens in your browser on your phone or computer. You answer a two-minute quiz about your age, weight, activity and food preferences, and it builds a personal 28-day plan. The idea is that most diets fail because they depend on hunger and willpower, so the Reset uses a way of eating that is filling and flexible instead.

The method has three parts. The Reset Plate is half vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter whole grains or legumes, with olive oil on top, so you can portion by eye. Your personal deficit is a daily calorie target calculated from your body and activity, kept above a safe floor and capped at one to two pounds of loss a week. The daily walk starts at 15 minutes and builds up to the time you choose, up to 45 minutes.

Each week has one job: reset the plate and shopping in week one, build rhythm in week two, handle real life in week three and cook from memory in week four. After day 28 you keep the recipes, shopping lists and tracker, and the final lesson shows you a maintenance number.

Does the Mediterranean approach in The Olive Reset work?

The Mediterranean pattern is among the best-studied ways of eating for weight and heart health. Here is what the research behind the approach shows:

  • In the one-year PREDIMED-Plus trial of older adults with overweight or obesity and metabolic syndrome, 33.7 percent of the group on an energy-restricted Mediterranean diet with activity and support lost at least 5 percent of their body weight, compared with 11.9 percent of the control group.[1]
  • In the two-year DIRECT trial of 322 moderately obese adults, the Mediterranean diet group lost about 4.4 kg on average, compared with about 2.9 kg for the low-fat group and 4.7 kg for the low-carbohydrate group.[2]
  • The authors of the DIRECT trial concluded that Mediterranean and low-carbohydrate diets may be effective alternatives to low-fat diets, and that personal preference could guide the choice.[2] The Reset leans on that idea: a way of eating that nothing is forbidden in.

What this means for you: the eating pattern behind The Olive Reset has solid research behind it, and the plan sensibly caps weight loss at a steady 1 to 2 pounds a week. The studies tested the pattern in specific groups, not this app, so results will vary with your starting point and how consistently you follow it.

How to get the most out of The Olive Reset

  1. Take the quiz honestly. Your target, meals and walking plan all come from your numbers.
  2. Cook from the week 1 shopping list. The first week is about the plate and the list, nothing else.
  3. Use the swap button freely. A meal you like is a meal you will cook again.
  4. Watch the 7-day average. The tracker shows the trend, so one heavy day looks like a blip.
  5. Read Lesson 8. It shows your maintenance number so you can keep the plate and the walk after day 28.

Is The Olive Reset safe? Who should check first

The Olive Reset is built on ordinary food, with a calorie target kept above a minimum for your sex and a weight-loss rate capped at one to two pounds a week. The quiz will not build a deficit plan for a BMI under 18.5.

  • If you have diabetes, kidney, heart or other medical conditions, or take medicine that affects your diet, check with your doctor before starting.
  • The page lists pregnant or breastfeeding women and anyone under 18 as people the plan is not for.
  • Choose your allergies and dislikes in the quiz so those foods never enter your plan, and check labels as you normally would.
  • Stop and see a doctor if you feel faint, unusually tired or unwell while following the plan.
