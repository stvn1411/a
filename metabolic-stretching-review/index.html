What is Metabolic Stretching and how is it meant to work?

Metabolic Stretching is a digital workout program built around one idea: stretching does not have to mean holding still. Instead of long static holds, the routine strings together flowing, controlled movements so you are moving, breathing and lengthening your muscles at the same time.

The program was created by Brian Klepacki, a certified strength and conditioning specialist with a master's degree in exercise science who coaches for Critical Bench. It draws on calisthenics, dynamic stretching, mobility drills, fascia work, breathing, Pilates-style core moves and yoga, picking ten movements that are simple to learn.

There are two versions. The beginner workout takes about 15 minutes; the advanced workout takes about 30 minutes and uses the same moves at higher intensity. Everything is delivered digitally, so you can start the same day.

Does Metabolic Stretching work?

The research supports the building blocks of the program, and the habit it encourages:

  • A systematic review in Applied Physiology, Nutrition, and Metabolism concluded that stretching within a warm-up that includes further dynamic activity is recommended for reducing muscle injuries and increasing joint range of motion.[3]
  • A review of 17 studies on yoga found full sessions averaged about 3 METs, which counts as light to moderate activity, and that some flowing sequences reached moderate-to-vigorous intensity. That shows flowing movement can contribute to your daily activity.[2]
  • A review of exercise and weight loss notes that activity helps create the energy balance needed for weight loss, but that the minimum guideline levels are generally not enough for significant weight loss without also managing calories. Combining movement with sensible eating is what works.[1]

What this means for you: Metabolic Stretching is a sensible, low-impact way to move more and improve flexibility, and a short routine you actually enjoy is easier to repeat than a long one. Treat it as one part of a healthy routine alongside good eating habits and sleep. The program has not had its own clinical trial, so judge it by how you feel after a few weeks of regular use.

How to get the most out of Metabolic Stretching

  1. Start with the beginner workout, even if you are fit. Learn the moves first, then move up to the advanced version.
  2. Watch the coaching videos once before the follow-along sessions so your form is right from day one.
  3. Pick a regular time. A short morning or lunchtime slot is easier to repeat than an occasional long session.
  4. Go at your own pace. Move smoothly and stop if anything hurts. Stretching should feel like a pull, not pain.
  5. Pair it with good habits: balanced meals, enough protein and water, walking and a good night's sleep.

Is Metabolic Stretching safe? Side effects

It is a low-impact bodyweight routine with no equipment, which makes it a gentle choice for most healthy adults. As with any exercise, the most common problems are muscle soreness after the first few sessions or a strain if you push a stretch too far.

  • If you have an injury, a joint or back problem, a heart condition or are pregnant, check with your doctor before starting. The program itself advises this too.
  • Choose the beginner workout first and scale up gradually.
  • Stop and seek advice if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath.
