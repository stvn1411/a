What is Quietum Plus and how is it meant to work?

Quietum Plus is a daily capsule built around the idea that healthy hearing is about more than the ears alone: sound is also processed by the nerves and the brain. The formula therefore combines nutrients and plants that are traditionally used, or studied, for nerve health, circulation, stress balance and mood.

Its ingredient list is broad. It includes adaptogenic herbs such as ashwagandha and maca, warming and antioxidant plants such as ginger and piperine, traditional botanicals such as dong quai, damiana and sarsaparilla, and nutrients that many people already know: vitamin A, B vitamins and zinc, plus the amino acids L-tyrosine and L-arginine.

The sales page describes the formula as a proprietary blend and presents it as supporting ear and brain wellness. It is a dietary supplement, not a medicine, and the best way to think about it is as a daily support alongside good sleep, sensible noise protection and regular check-ups.

Does Quietum Plus work?

Research on the individual ingredients is a mixed picture, and it helps to be clear about what is and is not known:

  • Ashwagandha has some of the best human data in the formula, with randomized trials reporting lower stress scores compared with placebo.[5]
  • Low vitamin B12 has been linked with poorer hearing in a systematic review, which makes adequate B vitamin intake a sensible part of ear and nerve health.[3]
  • Trials of single nutrients for ringing in the ears have been less encouraging: a systematic review found no clear benefit from zinc,[2] and a randomized trial of vitamin B12 in idiopathic tinnitus found no distinctive effect.[4]
  • L-tyrosine has been studied for focus under stress, with encouraging results in short, demanding situations.[6]

What this means for you: Quietum Plus brings together several well-known nutrients and herbs for general nerve, stress and brain support. Its finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as supportive daily nutrition rather than a treatment, and give it the 2 to 3 months the maker suggests alongside healthy habits.

Is Quietum Plus safe? Side effects

Quietum Plus is made from familiar nutrients and herbs and is described by the manufacturer as free of stimulants. As with any supplement with many plant ingredients, individual responses vary.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine (especially for blood pressure, thyroid or mood), or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting. The sales page gives the same advice.
  • Some ingredients, such as L-arginine and ashwagandha, can interact with certain medicines, so it is worth showing your doctor the label.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden hearing loss, ringing in one ear only, dizziness or ear pain; these need a professional check and are not something to manage with a supplement.
