What is ProNail Complex and how is it meant to work?

ProNail Complex is a mist spray rather than a supplement you swallow. You spray it on your feet after you step out of the shower, and the maker says the fine mist is absorbed quickly, so your toenails are not left feeling greasy. Putting on clean cotton socks afterwards is part of the recommended routine.

The formula combines carrier oils that moisturise (such as sweet almond, jojoba and flaxseed), aromatic oils that are traditionally used in foot care (tea tree, clove, lavender, lemongrass), vitamin E, aloe vera and undecylenic acid, an ingredient found in many over-the-counter foot products.

Our review looks at ProNail Complex as a foot-care and nail-care product. It is not a medicine, and it is not a replacement for a doctor's care if you have a nail infection.

Does ProNail Complex work? What the research says

ProNail Complex itself has not been through a published clinical trial, so we looked at the research on its key ingredients and on topical nail care:

  • Tea tree oil has been tested directly on toenails. In a six-month double-blind trial, 100% tea tree oil was comparable to a clotrimazole solution, with similar proportions of people improving.[2]
  • Applying products to nails is difficult because the nail plate is a hard barrier. Reviews of topical toenail treatments note that cure rates are lower than with oral medicines, and that longer, consistent use over 24 to 48 weeks gives better outcomes.[3]
  • Keeping nails thin, feet dry and socks breathable helps any topical product reach the nail, which is exactly the routine the maker recommends.

What this means for you: ProNail Complex is a sensible, well-priced foot-care spray with a published ingredient list and a research-supported oil in tea tree. Nails grow slowly, so give it months, and see a doctor if a nail looks infected, because a medical diagnosis and prescribed treatment are the reliable route in that case.

How to get the most out of ProNail Complex

  1. Spray after showering, when your feet are clean, following the maker's directions.
  2. Dry thoroughly first, including between toes, then put on clean cotton socks.
  3. Be consistent. Nails grow slowly, so judge over months, as the manufacturer advises.
  4. Keep nails short and thin, using clean, disinfected tools, so products can reach the nail surface.
  5. Rotate shoes and avoid walking barefoot in pools and gym showers.

Is ProNail Complex safe? Side effects

ProNail Complex is a topical product with natural oils, so the main thing to watch for is skin sensitivity. Essential oils such as tea tree, clove and camphor can irritate some people's skin.

  • Test on a small area of skin first, and stop if you notice redness, itching or irritation.
  • Avoid if you are allergic to nuts or to any ingredient listed, and keep the spray away from eyes and broken skin.
  • The official page advises anyone who is taking medication or has a medical condition to show the bottle to their doctor before use.
  • Dietary and personal-care products aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]
  • See a doctor for nails that are thick, discoloured, painful or crumbling, and especially if you have diabetes or poor circulation.
