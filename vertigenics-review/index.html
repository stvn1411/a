What is Vertigenics and how is it meant to work?

Vertigenics is a daily capsule marketed to support a healthy brain and balance system. The official page positions it as a natural, stimulant-free formula and highlights healthy brain function, balance, focus and concentration.

Its ten ingredients are mostly well-known antioxidant and metabolic-support nutrients. Alpha lipoic acid, green tea leaf and resveratrol are popular antioxidants, zinc and chromium are essential minerals, berberine and Korean ginseng are traditional botanicals, and milk thistle, cayenne and banana leaf round out the blend.

It's worth knowing that dizziness has many different causes, from the inner ear to blood pressure and medicines. Vertigenics is a supplement for everyday wellness, not a substitute for finding out why dizziness is happening.

Does Vertigenics work?

We looked carefully for published research and want to be straightforward about what it does and doesn't show:

  • There is no published trial of Vertigenics itself, and we found no trials of its ingredients for dizziness or vertigo.
  • The ingredients are well-known antioxidant and metabolic-support nutrients, which fits the product's positioning around general brain and body wellness.
  • For the most common true vertigo, benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV), the best-supported treatment is not a supplement: repositioning maneuvers such as the Epley maneuver have good evidence and are done by a clinician.[2]

What this means for you: Vertigenics is a sensible daily wellness addition, but it isn't a treatment for vertigo. If dizziness is new or recurring, see a doctor first, then use a supplement like this as general support alongside their advice.

How to get the most out of Vertigenics

  1. Take 2 capsules a day with a glass of water, at the same time each day.
  2. Be consistent. Daily nutritional support is judged over weeks, not days.
  3. Support balance in everyday ways: stay hydrated, get regular sleep, move gently each day and stand up slowly from lying or sitting.
  4. Keep a simple note of when any dizziness happens and what you were doing; it helps a doctor find the cause.
  5. Use the bonus guides if you choose the 6-bottle package.

Is Vertigenics safe? Side effects

The ingredients are common nutrients and botanicals with no stimulants, and most healthy adults tolerate them well. Mild stomach upset is the most common supplement side effect.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, have diabetes or take prescription medicine (especially blood-sugar or blood-thinning medicine), check with your doctor first. Berberine, chromium, ginseng and green tea can interact with some medicines.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden or severe dizziness, double vision, trouble speaking, weakness, a severe headache or hearing loss; these need urgent attention.

How Vertigenics compares with other options

  • Professional treatment: if a clinician diagnoses BPPV, repositioning maneuvers are the proven first step.[2] A supplement can sit alongside this but doesn't replace it.
  • Single-ingredient supplements: buying alpha lipoic acid or green tea on its own gives you a stated dose; Vertigenics offers ten ingredients in one routine, though individual amounts aren't published.
  • Lifestyle basics: hydration, sleep, regular meals and gentle movement all support steadiness.
