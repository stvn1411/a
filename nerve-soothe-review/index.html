What is Nerve Soothe and how is it meant to work?

Nerve Soothe is a herbal dietary supplement made to support healthy nerves. Rather than relying on one or two nutrients, it uses five plant extracts, with vitamin and mineral support, that the company chose for their traditional use and the research behind them.

The formula leans on herbs known for calming effects, such as passionflower and California poppy, together with prickly pear and marshmallow root, which have long been used as soothing, protective plants, and corydalis, a traditional Chinese herb that has been studied for pain comfort. The company recommends taking it for three to six months and daily over the long term.

It comes as a capsule taken twice a day, which suits people who like a clear morning and evening habit. The official page also adds two free bonus e-books on managing nerve health and staying active.

Do herbs like those in Nerve Soothe work?

Evidence varies by plant, and it is fair to say it is strongest for the calming herbs:

  • A systematic review of nine randomized trials on passionflower (Passiflora incarnata) found that most reported lower anxiety, with the authors noting the trials used many different measures.[2]
  • In a double-blind pilot trial of generalized anxiety, passionflower extract and the medicine oxazepam both worked, with no significant difference between them at the end.[3]
  • A controlled human trial of corydalis (with another herb) found lower pain intensity scores in a cold-pressor test, which suggests value for mild to moderate everyday discomfort.[4]

Most of this research used specific doses and extracts that may differ from those in Nerve Soothe, and the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial. The customer comments on the sales page are personal accounts, and results vary.

What this means for you: Nerve Soothe puts several traditionally used calming herbs into one twice-daily capsule, and the guarantee covers you even if the bottles are empty. Give it the full 60 days, note how you feel, and use the guarantee if it is not right for you.

Is Nerve Soothe safe? Side effects

Herbal supplements are usually well tolerated by healthy adults, but they are not risk-free. Several of the herbs in Nerve Soothe are used for their calming effect, so some people may feel relaxed or drowsy.

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially sedatives, sleep aids or antidepressants, ask your doctor first, because herbs can interact with medicines.[5]
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, or have diabetes, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Numbness, tingling or burning that is new, spreading or getting worse should be checked by a doctor.
