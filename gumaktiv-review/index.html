What is GumAktiv and how is it meant to work?

GumAktiv is a chewable tablet that you let melt under your tongue or chew slowly each morning. Most probiotics are swallowed and aimed at the gut. A tablet that dissolves in the mouth keeps its bacteria where your teeth and gums actually are.

The idea rests on the oral microbiome: the community of bacteria in your mouth. When the balance tips towards less friendly species, gums are more likely to become irritated. Oral probiotics aim to support a healthier balance by adding beneficial strains, and dental researchers have tested this approach in many clinical trials, with encouraging results for gum health (more on that below).

GumAktiv combines three probiotic species with a prebiotic fibre to feed them, a calcium source and peppermint for flavour. The species list is the same as in ProDentim, which we have also reviewed, so the research below applies to both.

Do oral probiotics like GumAktiv work?

The research on oral probiotics is genuinely encouraging, especially for gums:

  • A meta-analysis of 50 randomized trials with 3,247 participants found that probiotics lowered gum-disease bacteria and gingivitis scores and reduced pocket depths compared with placebo, and called them supportive for managing gingivitis and periodontitis.[2]
  • In a randomized trial, people with chronic periodontitis who took a B. lactis probiotic alongside a professional deep cleaning had better pocket depth and attachment gain after 90 days than those who had the cleaning alone.[3]
  • The same review did not find a clear effect on cavities, so keep brushing with fluoride toothpaste.[2]

What this means for you: oral probiotics are a research-backed way to support healthier gums, and GumAktiv puts three well-studied species into an easy daily chewable. The finished tablet hasn't had its own clinical trial yet, so give it a few weeks of daily use alongside your usual routine to see the difference.

How to get the most out of GumAktiv

  1. Take it after brushing in the morning and let it melt under your tongue or chew it slowly, so the probiotics stay in your mouth longer.
  2. Avoid rinsing or eating for a little while afterwards to give the bacteria time in your mouth.
  3. Be consistent. The manufacturer suggests about three months, and oral probiotic studies usually measured results after several weeks of daily use.
  4. Keep up the basics: brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth and keep your dental check-ups.
  5. Notice the changes: how your gums feel when you brush, and how fresh your breath is in the morning.

Is GumAktiv safe? Side effects

Probiotics are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and GumAktiv contains no stimulants. The most common effect of probiotics in general is mild, temporary bloating or a change in digestion as your body adjusts.

  • If you are immunocompromised, pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting any probiotic; the manufacturer gives the same advice.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth; these need a professional check.
