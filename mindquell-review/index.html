What is MindQuell and how is it meant to work?

MindQuell is a liquid supplement with a one-step routine: one drop a day, preferably in the morning, either straight or stirred into tea, coffee or juice. The directions ask you to shake the bottle first so the ingredients mix well. There is nothing to count, swallow or measure out.

Its nine listed ingredients are moringa, pine bark extract, Ginkgo biloba, tamarind, Lion's Mane mushroom, chlorella, Bacopa monnieri, spirulina and neem. Three of them, Bacopa, Lion's Mane and Ginkgo, are among the plants most often studied for memory and attention, and the rest add antioxidant plant compounds and nutrients.

The manufacturer's goal is stated plainly: to help support memory function. We think that is the right way to describe it. MindQuell is best seen as a daily habit that sits alongside sleep, movement and mental and social activity rather than a substitute for them.

Does MindQuell work?

MindQuell itself has not been tested in a clinical trial, so the best guide is the research on its leading ingredients:

  • A meta-analysis pooling nine placebo-controlled trials found that standardized Bacopa extract used for at least 12 weeks improved attention speed.[2]
  • In a double-blind trial of Japanese adults aged 50-80 with mild cognitive impairment, those taking Lion's Mane had higher cognitive scores than placebo at weeks 8, 12 and 16.[3] The trial was small and the effect faded once the supplement was stopped.
  • Cochrane reviewers found Ginkgo to be safe, with mixed results across trials on cognition.[4]

What this means for you: MindQuell groups several plants that have real human research behind them into one drop a day. Because the finished product has not had its own trial and the amounts are not published, we see it as a supportive habit, not a guaranteed fix. Use it consistently for a few months, alongside good sleep and activity, to judge it fairly.

How the 365-day money-back guarantee works

MindQuell is covered by a 365-day money-back guarantee. Here is how to use it, based on the official page and the shipping and return policy:

  1. You have 365 days to request a refund if you are not satisfied.
  2. Before sending anything back, email support@themindquell.com to say you intend to return your order. The policy asks you to do this first.
  3. The policy page asks that returned items be unused, unopened and in the original packaging, while the sales page FAQ says you can return what you haven't used. Ask support which applies to you.
  4. The return address is 285 Northeast Ave, Tallmadge, Ohio 44278. ClickBank's customer support at clkbank.com can also help with order questions.

Helpful tip: because the policy and the FAQ describe the returned item slightly differently, ask support in writing what they need back, and keep the reply. A calendar reminder well before the 365 days end is also a good idea.

How to get the most out of MindQuell

  1. Choose a regular time, such as with your morning drink, so it becomes part of the routine.
  2. Shake before use, as directed, to mix the ingredients.
  3. Allow a few months. The Bacopa studies we reviewed lasted 12 weeks or more.
  4. Look after the basics: sleep, activity, a varied diet and staying mentally and socially engaged all support brain health.
  5. Track how you feel with a short monthly note on focus and recall, then decide whether it is worth continuing.

Is MindQuell safe? Side effects

MindQuell is made from widely used plants and algae and contains no stimulants. Herbal products can still interact with some medicines, so it pays to be careful.

  • If you take blood thinners or other prescription medicine, ask your doctor first; Ginkgo is the ingredient most often raised.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition, check with your doctor, as the manufacturer's own page also recommends.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor about persistent memory changes or confusion; these need a professional assessment.
