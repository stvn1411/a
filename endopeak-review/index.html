What is EndoPeak and how is it meant to work?

EndoPeak is a once-a-day capsule for men. The official page describes it as plant-based and aimed at supporting stamina, libido and energy, which for most men means feeling more like themselves as the years go on.

The formula brings together eight ingredients: tongkat ali, tribulus, epimedium (horny goat weed), saw palmetto, hawthorn berry, chrysin, winged treebine and magnesium. Several of these have a long history in traditional men's formulas, and the idea is to support healthy circulation, energy and hormone balance in a general way.

The label does not list the amount of each ingredient and we did not find a clinical trial of the finished capsule, so we focused on what matters most to buyers: the ingredients themselves, the price and the guarantee. Here the picture is encouraging, because tongkat ali and tribulus both have placebo-controlled human studies behind them.

Do ingredients like those in EndoPeak work?

Men's vitality herbs are widely studied, and the strongest findings for two of EndoPeak's ingredients are encouraging:

  • A 12-week placebo-controlled trial in 105 men aged 50 to 70 found that a standardized tongkat ali extract raised total testosterone compared with placebo and reduced fatigue and ageing-male symptom scores. Two of the authors worked for the sponsor.[2]
  • A phase IV trial in 180 men with mild or moderate sexual difficulties found that a tribulus extract improved sexual function scores more than placebo over 12 weeks, with similar side effects. One author was linked to the maker of the extract.[4]
  • A 2025 meta-analysis of eight trials found tribulus scored higher than placebo on standard sexual function questionnaires, with similar side effects. It found no difference in total testosterone.[5]
  • For tongkat ali, a meta-analysis of two trials with 139 men found no overall difference in erection scores at 12 weeks, though men with lower starting scores improved.[3] Results are therefore promising but not conclusive.

What this means for you: EndoPeak is built around ingredients with real human research behind them, especially tongkat ali and tribulus, and puts them into an easy daily capsule. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the doses are not listed, so give it a steady two to three months and mention it to your doctor at your next check-up.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Orders are covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, which makes EndoPeak easy to try. Here is how to use it if you need to, based on the official return policy:

  1. The 60 days run from your date of purchase, and the return must be requested and approved within that time.

How to get the most out of EndoPeak

  1. Take one capsule with water after your first meal of the day, as the official page directs.
  2. Be consistent. The tongkat ali and tribulus trials measured results after about 12 weeks of daily use, so give it two to three months.

  4. Keep up your check-ups. A yearly visit with your doctor, including blood pressure and heart health, matters more than any supplement.
  5. Track the changes in energy, stamina and mood so you can judge before the guarantee window closes.

Is EndoPeak safe? Side effects

The ingredients in EndoPeak are generally well tolerated, and in the tribulus trials side effects were similar to placebo.[4][5] The most common complaints with herbal formulas are mild stomach upset or headache.

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for the heart, blood pressure or blood thinning, check with your doctor before starting. Hawthorn in particular is traditionally used for circulation.
  • If you have a heart, circulation or hormone-related condition, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for chest pain, dizziness, fainting or a persistent change in performance or energy. These can have several causes and need a professional check.
