What is Advanced Amino Formula and how is it meant to work?

Advanced Amino Formula is a tablet containing eight of the nine essential amino acids. These are the amino acids your body cannot make itself, so they have to come from food or supplements, and they are the raw material for building and repairing muscle.

The formula was developed by Dr. Frank Shallenberger, who is named on the page as the formulator and, as the page discloses, is compensated by the company. The page positions the formula for people who want to hold on to muscle as they get older and recover well from exercise.

Unlike many supplements, the full supplement facts panel is shown, with each amino acid named. One thing to note: the label leaves out histidine, the ninth essential amino acid.

Do essential amino acid supplements like this work?

Essential amino acids have been studied for years in muscle research, and the picture is encouraging when they are paired with activity:

  • In a randomized, counterbalanced trial of 7 younger and 7 older adults, an essential amino acid supplement taken alone or with resistance exercise raised blood amino acids and muscle-building signals, and the combination with exercise produced the strongest response.[2]
  • In a small randomized trial of 15 older adults, whey protein improved muscle protein accrual more than a 6.7 g essential amino acid mixture, so a balanced diet with enough total protein still matters.[3]
  • Both studies are small and short, so they point to a sensible approach rather than proof of long-term results: pair amino acids with strength activity and enough dietary protein.

What this means for you: Advanced Amino Formula gives you the essential building blocks in a clearly labelled, vegan tablet. The research suggests the best results come when you combine them with resistance exercise and a protein-rich diet. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a couple of months of daily use alongside your usual activity.

How to get the most out of Advanced Amino Formula

  1. Follow the label. A serving is five tablets; many people split them across the day with water.
  2. Pair it with strength activity. Research suggests essential amino acids work best alongside resistance exercise.
  3. Keep dietary protein up: eggs, fish, dairy, beans and lean meat all provide the full set of amino acids.
  4. Be consistent. Give it a couple of months of daily use and track how you feel during workouts and recovery.
  5. Stay hydrated and keep your order details for the 90-day window.

Is Advanced Amino Formula safe? Side effects

Essential amino acids are building blocks that occur naturally in food, and supplements at this level are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. Mild stomach upset is the most common complaint.

  • If you have kidney or liver disease, have been advised to limit protein, or have phenylketonuria (the formula contains phenylalanine), check with your doctor before starting.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for unexplained muscle loss, persistent weakness or fatigue, as these can have medical causes that need a proper check.

How Advanced Amino Formula compares with other options

  • Whey or plant protein powders: they provide total protein along with calories and flavour. In the small older-adult trial we cite, whey did better than an amino acid mixture, so protein foods and powders remain a sound base.[3]
  • BCAA powders: these contain only three amino acids, whereas this formula includes eight essential amino acids in one tablet.
  • Food and training: regular strength training and enough dietary protein are the foundations; a supplement is an extra on top.
