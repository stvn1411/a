What is BioVanish and how is it meant to work?

BioVanish is a cocoa-flavored powder you stir into water, milk or a milk alternative once a day. The maker's idea is to give you a simple source of medium-chain fats, which the page calls 9-c fats, along with L-theanine and B vitamins.

Medium-chain fats from coconut are digested differently from the long-chain fats in most foods, and they have been studied for their effect on body weight and body composition. The mix is designed to sit alongside a healthy diet and an active lifestyle, not replace them.

It is sold as a dietary supplement and is not available in stores, only through the maker's website.

Does BioVanish work? What the research on medium-chain fats shows

We could not find a published trial on BioVanish itself. Here is what the research on its main ingredient type shows:

  • One meta-analysis of randomized trials found that replacing other fats with medium-chain triglycerides was linked with about 0.5 kg lower body weight and a smaller waist, with no harm to blood fats.[2]
  • A second meta-analysis, limited to trials of at least four weeks, found an average difference of about 0.7 kg in body weight.[3]
  • In a 16-week randomized trial of 49 overweight adults on a weight-loss diet, those using MCT oil lost more weight than those using olive oil.[4]
  • The effects were modest and appeared alongside healthy diets, so the mix is best seen as a helpful addition to good habits.

What this means for you: medium-chain fats are a researched, sensible ingredient for a healthy-weight routine, and BioVanish makes them easy to take in a drink you may enjoy. The finished mix hasn't had its own trial and the effects in studies were modest, so pair it with sensible meals and regular movement.

How to get the most out of BioVanish

  1. Mix one scoop into 8 oz of water, milk or a milk alternative, stir and drink.
  2. Make it a habit, for example with breakfast, so you don't skip days.
  3. Give it time. The maker suggests using it for a few months, and the research on medium-chain fats ran for weeks.
  4. Build on the basics: balanced meals, regular movement and enough sleep.
  5. Track your progress with measurements you trust, such as waist size or how your clothes fit.

Is BioVanish safe? Side effects

Cocoa-flavored mixes with coconut-derived fats are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. Some points to keep in mind:

  • Medium-chain fats can cause mild digestive changes such as stomach upset when first used; starting regularly with one scoop is the maker's direction.
  • If you take prescription medicine, have diabetes, or have liver, gallbladder or digestive conditions, ask your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Pregnant, nursing or under 18? Check with your doctor before using it.
