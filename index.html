How we review supplements

We don't run clinical trials, and we say so. What we do is a careful, repeatable desk review of every product, using the same method each time.

Read the full label

We list every ingredient and dose the manufacturer discloses and flag proprietary blends that hide amounts.

Check the research

We look for human studies on the main ingredients and explain what they did and did not show.

Compare price & refunds

Cost per day, bundle deals, shipping, auto-ship traps, and the real conditions of the money-back guarantee.

Give a plain verdict

Who it may suit, who should avoid it, safety notes, and cheaper or better-studied alternatives where they exist.

What you will always find in our reviews

  • A last-updated date and a clear affiliate disclosure
  • Both pros and cons, never only the good parts
  • Who the product is not for
  • Safety notes and when to talk to a doctor first

What you won't find

  • Fake countdown timers or "only 3 left" pressure
  • Promises of cures or guaranteed results
  • Made-up customer testimonials
  • Rankings that are for sale

Frequently asked questions

How does TruePicks Review evaluate supplements?

We look at the full ingredient list and doses where they are disclosed, the published research on the main ingredients, the manufacturer's refund policy and pricing, and how the product is marketed. Every review lists pros, cons, and who the product is and is not a good fit for.

Do you make money from these reviews?

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change our verdicts, and we disclose affiliate links on every page that has them.

Are supplements a replacement for medical treatment?

No. Dietary supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before starting a supplement, especially if you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a health condition.

Where should I buy a supplement I am interested in?

For most of the products we cover, the manufacturer's official website is the only source that honours the refund guarantee. Third-party marketplace listings can be resellers or counterfeits, so check the seller before you buy.

How often are reviews updated?

We re-check pricing, ingredients and refund terms regularly and show the last-updated date at the top of every review.
