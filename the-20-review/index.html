What is The 20 FLOW and how is it meant to work?

FLOW is a nitric oxide support capsule from The20, a US brand run by Susan Bratton and her team. Nitric oxide is a molecule that helps blood vessels relax, so blood can move easily around the body. The brand's pitch is simple: nitric oxide production tends to fall with age, so give the body the building blocks to keep making it.

The main building block is L-citrulline, an amino acid the body converts into arginine and then into nitric oxide. Rather than the lab-made citrulline powders found in many products, FLOW uses citrulline from organic watermelon rind and organic spinach, which the seller describes as fermented real foods.

Two supporting ingredients round out the formula: vitamin C from organic acerola cherry, which the seller adds to help absorption, and pine bark extract, included for its circulatory effects. The result is a short formula taken as two capsules a day.

Does The 20 FLOW work?

The research behind FLOW's two main ingredients is encouraging, with a few points worth knowing:

  • A placebo-controlled study found that oral L-citrulline improved erection hardness in men with mild erectile dysfunction, with 12 of 24 men moving up a grade versus 2 of 24 on placebo.[2]
  • In a double-blind crossover trial, a French maritime pine bark extract improved flow-mediated dilation in people with stable coronary artery disease, with no change in blood pressure.[3]
  • Both studies were small and short, and neither tested FLOW itself, so they show that the ingredients are promising rather than proving the exact formula.

What this means for you: citrulline and pine bark are sensible, research-supported choices for circulation support, and FLOW packages them in an easy daily capsule. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a month of daily use alongside the basics: movement, good sleep and a balanced diet.
