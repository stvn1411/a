What is Complete Collagen Plus and how is it meant to work?

Complete Collagen Plus is a thick, caramel-flavoured liquid. You take one tablespoon a day, stirred into coffee, oatmeal or a smoothie, or simply off the spoon. It was designed to be easier to stick with than collagen capsules or powders that can taste of beef or clump.

The collagen comes from grass-fed beef and is delivered in a micellized, liposomal form, which the maker says helps it absorb. Around it are MCT oil, a plant-based omega-3 (algal DHA) and vitamin E, which are meant to add energy, satiety and antioxidant support.

Collagen is the main structural protein in skin, and its levels fall with age, which is why collagen supplements have become one of the most popular beauty-from-within products.

Does Complete Collagen Plus work?

Collagen is one of the few beauty supplements with a real body of human research behind it:

  • A systematic review and meta-analysis of 19 randomized, double-blind trials with 1,125 participants found favourable effects of hydrolyzed collagen on skin aging outcomes such as hydration and elasticity.[1]
  • In one double-blind trial, women aged 35 to 55 who took 2.5 g or 5 g of collagen peptides daily for 8 weeks had significantly better skin elasticity than those on placebo, and no side effects were reported.[2]
  • Complete Collagen Plus provides 1 g of collagen per tablespoon, less than the doses in many trials, and the micellized delivery has not been compared head-to-head in published human studies that we could find. Many people take it for the combination of ingredients and its easy format.

What this means for you: Collagen supplements are a reasonable, research-supported way to support skin from within, and Complete Collagen Plus makes the habit easy and enjoyable. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a couple of months of daily use, as the maker suggests, alongside sunscreen and a balanced diet.

How to get the most out of Complete Collagen Plus

  1. Make it a ritual. Stir one tablespoon into your morning coffee, oatmeal or smoothie, or take it off the spoon.
  2. Take it every day. Collagen studies typically measured results after about 8 to 12 weeks of daily use.
  3. Protect your skin. Daily sunscreen is the most important thing you can do for skin aging.
  4. Add vitamin C foods such as citrus and peppers, which the body uses when making collagen.
  5. Notice the changes: skin feel, nails and hair over a few weeks; take photos in the same light.

Is Complete Collagen Plus safe? Side effects

Collagen supplements are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and the maker lists no stimulants. The most common complaints with collagen and MCT are mild, such as a feeling of fullness or occasional digestive upset when starting.

  • The formula contains whey protein (milk) and beef-derived collagen; avoid it if you have a milk or beef allergy.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, taking medicine (including blood thinners) or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first, as the label advises.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[3]
  • Stop and see a doctor if you have a serious or lasting reaction.
