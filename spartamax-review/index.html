What is Spartamax and how is it meant to work?

Spartamax is a daily male vitality supplement in gummy form. Each bottle holds 30 gummies, one for each day, so there are no scoops to measure and no capsules to swallow.

The formula works on two fronts. The first is drive and well-being: tongkat ali, maca and ashwagandha are traditional herbs that have been tested in modern trials for libido, energy, stress and male hormones. The second is healthy blood flow: L-arginine and beetroot support the body's production of nitric oxide, the molecule that helps blood vessels relax, and grape seed extract adds antioxidant support.

It's aimed at adult men, especially those aged 40–70 who feel they've lost a bit of their edge, and it's sold only through the official site.

Do male vitality herbs like those in Spartamax work?

The herbs at the heart of Spartamax have some of the most encouraging research in the male vitality category:

  • In a 12-week randomized trial of 109 men aged 30–55, a tongkat ali extract improved erectile function scores and raised libido by 14% by the end of the study, with safety results similar to placebo.[2]
  • In moderately stressed adults, 4 weeks of tongkat ali reduced cortisol exposure by 16% and improved tension and mood.[3]
  • In overweight men aged 40–70, ashwagandha led to a 14.7% greater rise in testosterone than placebo over 8 weeks.[5]
  • Arginine supplements improved erectile function and satisfaction scores in a meta-analysis of 10 trials.[6]

What this means for you: Spartamax brings together herbs with real human research for drive, energy and well-being in one easy daily gummy. The studies used specific extracts and doses, the finished gummy hasn't had its own clinical trial, and maca's evidence is still limited, so give it several weeks of daily use. The one-year guarantee gives you all the time you need.

Is Spartamax safe? Side effects

The herbs in Spartamax were well tolerated in the trials we reviewed: the tongkat ali study reported safety results similar to placebo.[2] As with any herbal blend, mild stomach upset or trouble sleeping are possible in sensitive people.

  • Ashwagandha can affect thyroid hormones and may interact with sedatives, and arginine and beetroot can lower blood pressure. If you take thyroid, blood pressure, ED or diabetes medicine, or have a hormone-sensitive condition such as prostate problems, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for erection problems that are new or persistent, or for chest pain, shortness of breath or very low energy, as these can point to a health problem that needs checking.
