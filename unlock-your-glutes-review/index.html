What is Unlock Your Glutes and how is it meant to work?

Unlock Your Glutes is a short digital training program built around one idea: the glutes are your body's biggest muscle group, they work from several angles, and a plan that targets each of them directly can make them stronger.

It was created by Brian Klepacki, MS, CSCS, a certified strength and conditioning specialist who trains athletes at his facility in Florida. The program is two workouts a week over four weeks, each around 15 minutes, with a short warm-up he calls the Wake Up Protocol. You get a manual with the plan and photos, plus videos of all 36 exercises in a Bodyweight edition for home and a Gym edition for equipment.

Because the workouts are short, it is easy to fit into a busy week, and you can repeat the four weeks as many times as you like.

Does Unlock Your Glutes work? What the research shows

The principles behind the program match what training research shows:

  • In a matched-volume trial, hip thrust training and back squat training produced similar growth in the glute muscles, so targeting the glutes with the right exercises is a sound approach.[1]
  • Another trial in well-trained women found squats gave larger gains in glute muscle thickness than hip thrusts, which is why the program's bonus Strong Legs Workout is a useful addition.[2]
  • Reviews comparing lighter and heavier loads found that muscle growth can be similar across a range of loads, while heavier loads build more maximal strength. That supports using the Bodyweight edition at home and the Gym edition when you want extra resistance.[3]
  • A systematic review has looked at hip muscle strengthening for non-specific low back pain; the evidence there is still developing, so view stronger glutes as one helpful habit rather than a cure.[4]

What this means for you: glute-focused training is well supported, and Unlock Your Glutes organizes it into a short plan with clear video guidance. The program itself has not had its own trial, so treat it as an excellent, structured way to do the training that research supports and judge by your own strength and shape over a couple of months.

How to get the most out of Unlock Your Glutes

  1. Do the Wake Up Protocol first. The 3 to 5 minute warm-up is part of the plan, so don't skip it.
  2. Start with the Bodyweight edition if you are new to training, and move to the Gym edition when you want more resistance.
  3. Keep to two sessions a week with a rest day between, and aim for good form before more effort.
  4. Support the work with food and sleep. Enough protein and rest help muscles recover and grow.
  5. Track simple measures such as how many reps you can do and how you feel on stairs or hills, then repeat the four weeks.

Is Unlock Your Glutes safe? Side effects

Unlock Your Glutes is bodyweight and light-equipment training, which is generally a gentle way to begin strength work. As with any exercise program, a few precautions apply.

  • Start with a comfortable range of motion and stop if you feel sharp pain, especially in the hips, knees or back.
  • If you have joint or back problems, are pregnant or recently gave birth, or have a heart or other medical condition, check with a doctor or physical therapist first.
  • Muscle soreness for a day or two after new exercises is normal; ongoing pain is not.
  • Remember that exercise programs are not a treatment for pain or injury. See a professional for pain that lasts.
