What is Weight Loss Relaxed and how is it meant to work?

Weight Loss Relaxed Kickstart is a digital eating program. Rather than giving you a rigid meal plan or a calorie target, it teaches a handful of habits: plate and hand-size guides so you can judge a meal at a glance, easy swaps for common foods and filling meals built from normal supermarket ingredients.

It is written for adults who have tried standard diets and felt hungry, tired or discouraged. The author tells his own story of struggling to lose weight and shares the method he says worked for him, written in a book-style format with exercises at the end of each chapter.

The program is split into three steps: a 1 to 2 week kickstart to build momentum, a 30-day challenge to turn the changes into habits, and guidance for setbacks, eating out and keeping the weight off.

Does the Weight Loss Relaxed approach work?

The program is built on principles that diet research supports, even though the program itself has not been trialled:

  • In a two-year trial, participants lost an average of 6 kg (about 7% of body weight) at 6 months on reduced-calorie diets, whichever macronutrient mix they followed. Satisfaction and hunger were similar across diets, and attendance at group sessions predicted weight loss.[1]
  • A 12-month trial found a healthy low-carbohydrate diet and a healthy low-fat diet gave similar results, with average losses of 6.0 kg and 5.3 kg. The best diet is the one you can keep following.[2]
  • A trial at the NIH Clinical Center found that people on an unprocessed diet ate less and lost weight compared with an ultra-processed diet, even though the meals were matched for presented calories, macronutrients, sugar and fiber.[3] This is the kind of change real-food swaps aim for.

What this means for you: diet research keeps pointing to the same thing: sensible, sustainable eating habits work, and the exact style matters less than being able to follow it. Weight Loss Relaxed turns those ideas into a simple plan. The program has not been tested in its own trial, and the page notes that results vary, so treat the quick first-week changes it describes as one person's experience rather than a promise.

How to get the most out of Weight Loss Relaxed

  1. Start with the kickstart plan and give it the full one to two weeks before judging it.
  2. Shop with the 14-day list, so the right foods are already in the house.
  3. Use the plate rules at every meal, including when eating out.
  4. Complete the workbook exercises. They are designed to turn ideas into habits.
  5. Add gentle movement if you enjoy it, such as a daily walk. It is not required, but it supports overall health.
  6. Look beyond the scale: energy, clothes fit and how full you feel after meals are also signs the plan is working.

Is Weight Loss Relaxed safe? Who should check first

The plan is built around ordinary foods and balanced plates rather than extreme restriction, which makes it a gentle approach for many adults. The official page itself advises consulting a doctor, dietitian or other professional before starting any weight loss program if you have a medical condition.

  • If you have diabetes, kidney disease or heart disease, or take medicine that is affected by diet, check with your doctor before changing how you eat.
  • Pregnant or nursing women, anyone under 18 and anyone with a history of disordered eating should seek professional advice first.
  • Aim for steady progress. See a doctor if you feel dizzy, unusually tired or unwell after changing your diet.
