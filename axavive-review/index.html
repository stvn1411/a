What is Axavive and how is it meant to work?

Axavive is a once-a-day botanical supplement aimed at skin that looks and feels firmer, smoother and better hydrated. Instead of a single headline ingredient, it blends six plants and plant compounds that are each known for supporting skin or protecting it from everyday oxidative stress.

The manufacturer explains the formula through what it calls "axon renewal", the idea that the nerve connections in and around skin play a part in how it repairs itself. That is the maker's own framework. What we can say from the published research is that several of the individual ingredients have been studied in people for skin hydration, elasticity and wrinkles, and those studies are what we focus on below.

Axavive is sold through ClickBank and ships from the US. Results are described as gradual: the maker suggests some people notice softer, more hydrated-feeling skin in the first weeks, with the most visible changes after three to six months of daily use.

Do the ingredients in Axavive work?

Axavive itself has not had a published clinical trial that we could find, so we looked at what research says about its key plants:

  • In a 12-week study of 20 postmenopausal women, a pine bark extract was linked with better skin hydration and elasticity, with the biggest change in women who started with dry skin.[2]
  • A 24-week randomized, placebo-controlled trial of a red ginseng herbal blend found improved facial wrinkles and signs of increased collagen formation in women over 40.[3]
  • Oral Centella asiatica has now been tested in a randomized, placebo-controlled trial on skin-aging parameters in Korean women, a good sign that the herb is being taken seriously by researchers.[4]

What this means for you: the plants in Axavive have genuine research interest for skin, and the formula brings several of them together in one daily serving. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet, and the "axon renewal" explanation is the manufacturer's own, so give it a few months of steady use alongside sun protection and your usual skin care.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee, which makes Axavive easy to try. Here is how it works:

  1. The 90 days start from your order date, not the delivery date.
  2. Contact support (contact@axavive.com) and return all the bottles from the order, empty, full or part full, with the original packing slip.
  3. Return postage is paid by you, and shipping fees or customs charges on international orders are not refunded.
  4. Once the fulfilment centre has processed the return, the refund can take up to 5 to 10 business days to show on your statement.

Helpful tip: the clock starts at the order date, so put a reminder in your calendar for around day 75. The company itself suggests giving Axavive at least two months before deciding.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. Its order phone support (1-800-390-6035 at the time of writing) can help with any order question.

How to get the most out of Axavive

  1. Take it every day at the same time so it becomes a habit. Follow the directions on the bottle.
  2. Be patient. The maker suggests skin may feel softer in the first weeks, with more visible changes between three and six months.
  3. Protect your skin from the sun. Daily sunscreen does more for skin ageing than any supplement, and works well alongside Axavive.
  4. Keep up the basics: a gentle cleanser, moisturizer, plenty of water and a balanced diet.
  5. Take photos in the same light every few weeks so you can see gradual changes honestly.

Is Axavive safe? Side effects

Axavive is made from plant ingredients and contains no stimulants, according to the manufacturer. As with any botanical supplement, a small number of people may notice mild digestive upset, and some plants can interact with medicines.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing or taking prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting. The official site gives the same advice.
  • Panax ginseng and some other botanicals can interact with certain medicines, so mention Axavive to your doctor or pharmacist if you take any regularly.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor or dermatologist for any new, changing or painful skin spot, rash or persistent skin problem.
