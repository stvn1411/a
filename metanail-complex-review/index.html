What is Metanail Complex and how is it meant to work?

Metanail Complex is the name of the offer, and Metanail Serum Pro is the main product inside it. The sales page describes it as a 20-in-1 nail and foot formula built to support the look, strength and comfort of nails, cuticles and feet. The larger packages add a second product, Total Cleanse, and two free digital guides.

The ingredient list reads like a modern nail-care shelf in one bottle: moisturizers such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid, antioxidants such as green tea and vitamins C and E, soothing botanicals such as aloe and gotu kola, and nourishing oils such as jojoba. The page does not print directions or the product format in detail, so follow the label that comes with your bottle.

Healthy nails are slow to change. Fingernails grow only a few millimetres a month, so any nail-care routine, this one included, is best judged over a couple of months rather than a couple of weeks.

Does a formula like Metanail Complex work?

Research on brittle and weak nails is limited and mostly small, but the existing studies are encouraging for moisturizing and nourishing approaches:

  • In a clinical study of 23 women with brittle nails, a water-soluble nail strengthener containing hyaluronic acid improved nail appearance and weakness over 3 months, while the untreated nails showed no improvement. Three of the authors worked for the product's maker.[2]
  • In a randomized, open-label trial of 50 women, a biomineral formulation improved nail hardness and reduced roughness after 3 months, whether used on the nails, taken orally or both. The abstract does not list the ingredients, so this is a different formulation from Metanail.[3]

Together these studies suggest that a consistent 3-month routine with moisturizing and nourishing ingredients is a reasonable way to look after brittle-looking nails. Neither study tested Metanail itself.

What this means for you: Metanail Complex follows the same approach as products that have been studied, with moisturizing and nourishing ingredients and a routine you keep up for about three months. The finished formula has not had its own published clinical trial, so give it a fair trial and judge by how your nails look and feel over that period.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the policy page is refreshingly clear. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 60 days start from the day you receive the order.
  2. Send all the items you received back to the warehouse address on the refund page (Aurora, Colorado). Return postage is paid by you.
  3. Posting on day 59 is fine, even if the parcel arrives a few days later.
  4. Once the return is received and checked, the refund is processed within a few days, minus any shipping fees.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 45. That gives you time to judge your results and still post a return comfortably if you decide to use the guarantee.

How to get the most out of Metanail Complex

  1. Follow the directions on the label that comes with your bottle, since the sales page does not print them.
  2. Be consistent. The nail studies we found measured results after about 3 months of daily use.
  3. Protect your nails from daily wear: wear gloves for washing-up and cleaning, and keep nails trimmed.
  4. Moisturize hands and feet and dry between the toes after showering to keep skin comfortable.
  5. Photograph your nails in the same light every few weeks, and decide before day 60.

Is Metanail Complex safe? Side effects

Metanail Complex is made from common botanical and nutrient ingredients, and the maker states it is produced in an FDA-registered facility. As with any new product, it is sensible to start gently and watch how you respond.

  • If you have sensitive skin or known allergies, check the label for any ingredient you react to, such as botanical extracts, and stop if you notice irritation.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing, on prescription medicine or have diabetes or circulation problems, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor or podiatrist for painful, discolored, thickened or crumbling nails, as these can have causes that need treatment.
