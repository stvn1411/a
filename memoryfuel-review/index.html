What is MemoryFuel and how is it meant to work?

MemoryFuel is a powder you stir into a glass of water once a day. Each scoop combines five nutrients that the brain and nerves rely on, with creatine as the headline ingredient. Creatine is best known as a sports supplement, because muscles use it for quick energy, but the brain uses it too, which is why researchers have tested it for memory and thinking skills.

The other four ingredients have clear roles in nerve and brain function: choline is used to make a chemical messenger involved in memory, magnesium and vitamin D3 support normal nerve and muscle function, and vitamin B12 is needed for healthy nerves. Together they make a short, easy-to-understand formula.

The sales page also describes the formula as having six ingredients but names five on its label list, so we reviewed the five that are named.

Does creatine-based MemoryFuel support memory?

Of MemoryFuel's ingredients, creatine has the most direct research on memory:

  • A systematic review of six randomized controlled trials with 281 healthy people found evidence that oral creatine may improve short-term memory and reasoning.[2]
  • The authors noted that findings for other thinking skills were less consistent and called for larger trials, so creatine is best seen as a promising, not proven, memory nutrient.[2]
  • The other four ingredients are essential nutrients. They matter most for people who are low in them, so a daily formula can be a sensible way to cover the basics for brain and nerve health.

What this means for you: MemoryFuel pairs a promising memory ingredient with four nutrients the brain needs, in a simple daily drink. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the amounts per scoop are not published, so treat it as a daily support for memory rather than a quick fix, and give it a couple of months.

How to get the most out of MemoryFuel

  1. Take one scoop a day mixed with 6 to 8 oz of water, at the same time each day.
  2. Stay hydrated. Creatine draws water into muscle, so drinking enough water through the day is sensible.
  3. Be patient. Studies on creatine typically run for weeks, so give it at least two to three months.
  4. Cover the other basics: regular sleep, exercise and learning new things all support memory.
  5. Tell your doctor you are taking it, especially if you have a medical condition or take medicine.

Is MemoryFuel safe? Side effects

Creatine, choline, magnesium and the two vitamins are all common in food and supplements, and MemoryFuel contains no stimulants. The usual cautions for any supplement apply.

  • The manufacturer advises asking a doctor first if you are pregnant, nursing, have a medical condition or take prescription medication.
  • People with kidney problems should check with their doctor before using creatine.
  • Some people notice mild stomach upset with magnesium or creatine; taking it with water and food can help.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • If you notice ongoing memory loss or confusion, see a doctor rather than relying on a supplement.

How MemoryFuel compares with other options

  • Plain creatine monohydrate: inexpensive and well known, but it is only one ingredient. MemoryFuel adds choline, magnesium and two vitamins in a single scoop.
  • Probiotic memory formulas: these take a gut-brain route instead. See our Synaptigen review for one example.
  • Food and lifestyle: eggs, fish, leafy greens, regular exercise and good sleep support the same nutrients and brain health, and work well alongside a supplement.
