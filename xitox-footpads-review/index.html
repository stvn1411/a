What is Xitox Footpads and how is it meant to work?

Xitox Footpads are adhesive pads that you stick to the soles of your feet at bedtime. Each pad holds a blend of minerals and plant extracts, and you wear it while you sleep for at least eight hours. In the morning you peel the pads off, wash your feet and carry on with your day.

The idea is a simple one and fits an old tradition: foot pads and herbal foot care have long been used in parts of East Asia as a restful bedtime habit. Xitox's own description is modest and comfort-focused. The manufacturer says the pads are meant to give a soothing experience after a long day and to help skin on your feet feel moisturised and soft.

Many people also simply enjoy the ritual. Taking two minutes at the end of the day to look after your feet is a pleasant habit in its own right, and the pads are easy to apply with no mess, no creams and no rinsing before bed.

Do foot pads like Xitox work?

It is worth being clear about what the research can and cannot tell you here. The honest picture is a gentle one:

  • Every ingredient in Xitox Footpads has a documented history of use, and several have published laboratory or review research behind them.[3][6][4]
  • We did not find a published clinical trial of the finished pads, so the manufacturer's description of a soothing, refreshed feeling is best read as a comfort claim rather than a medical result.
  • The pads are designed to feel soothing and to support soft skin, which is a goal most users can judge for themselves within a few nights.
  • A visible colour change on the pad overnight is a feature of the product's design. We would not treat it as a measurement of anything in your body.

What this means for you: Xitox Footpads are best thought of as a comfort and self-care product. If a soothing bedtime ritual and softer-feeling feet are what you want, the pleasant routine and the 365-day guarantee make them an easy thing to test for yourself.

How the 365-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 365-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest we have seen. Here is how it works according to the company's shipping and return policy:

  1. You have up to a year from your purchase to request a refund.
  2. Pack the bottles or boxes back up (used, unused or empty) and put your name and order ID on a sheet of paper inside the parcel.
  3. Send the package to the returns address given in the policy. The policy does not say who pays return postage, so assume you arrange and pay for the drop-off.
  4. Then contact the company on 1-800-259-9522 or by email, with a photo of the postage receipt or the tracking number, to speed things up.
  5. Once the returns team receives the parcel you are emailed a refund confirmation. Processing can take up to two weeks after the carrier receives it, plus another 10 to 14 business days for the bank to post the money.

How to get the most out of Xitox Footpads

  1. Wash and dry your feet first. The pad sticks best to clean, dry skin.
  2. Press firmly for a few seconds so the pad stays in the centre of the sole overnight.
  3. Wear them for at least eight hours, as the manufacturer suggests, ideally while you sleep.
  4. Remove the pads and wash your feet in the morning, then moisturise as usual if you like.
  5. Try them for a couple of weeks and note how your feet feel when you wake up. Keep the order ID somewhere safe in case you want a refund.

Is Xitox Footpads safe? Side effects

Xitox Footpads are applied to the skin rather than taken by mouth, and they contain plant and mineral ingredients. Skin-contact products can occasionally cause irritation or an allergic reaction in sensitive people.

  • Do a quick test on a small patch of skin first if you have sensitive skin, and stop using the pads if you notice redness, itching or a rash.
  • Chitosan comes from shellfish, so check with your doctor first if you have a shellfish allergy.
  • If you have diabetes, poor circulation or reduced feeling in your feet, check with your doctor before using any adhesive product on your soles.
  • Never apply the pads to broken or irritated skin.
  • Wellness products like this are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]
