What is Nervala and how is it meant to work?

Nervala is a nutritional supplement from Barton Nutrition, part of Barton Publishing, a US company best known for its health education programs. It is sold in bottles of 30 capsules and is aimed at adults who want to support the health of their peripheral nerves, the long nerves that run to the hands and feet.

The idea is straightforward. Nerves need a steady supply of certain nutrients to work well, and two of them have attracted a lot of research: alpha-lipoic acid (ALA), an antioxidant the body makes in small amounts, and benfotiamine, a fat-soluble form of vitamin B1. Barton describes Nervala as pairing the two in a single capsule.

The sales page is a video presentation that focuses on everyday foods and lifestyle for tingling and numbness, and then introduces Nervala as a supplement to go with them. The video page itself does not print a full supplement facts panel, so we recommend reading the label on your bottle for the exact amounts.

Do ingredients like those in Nervala work?

Both ingredients have been studied by independent researchers, which puts Nervala on firmer ground than many supplements:

  • A meta-analysis of randomized trials of alpha-lipoic acid for symptomatic diabetic neuropathy found that it improved symptoms compared with placebo.[5]
  • In the 509-person ALADIN III trial, people who had IV ALA followed by oral ALA showed a modest benefit on a nerve impairment score compared with placebo.[2]
  • In SYDNEY 2 (181 people), oral ALA improved symptoms at all three doses tested.[3]
  • Benfotiamine showed a significant improvement in a small 3-week pilot trial, and the authors suggested larger studies.[4]

Most of this research was done in people with diabetes, using specific doses, and the finished Nervala capsule has not had its own clinical trial. Results can differ from person to person.

What this means for you: the two ingredients behind Nervala are among the best-studied nutrients for nerve comfort, and having them in one capsule makes the routine easy. Give it a few weeks of daily use, and use the long guarantee window to judge it fairly.

How to get the most out of Nervala

  1. Follow the directions on the bottle. The label has the serving size and timing for your bottle.
  2. Be consistent. The studies on ALA and benfotiamine measured results over weeks, not days.
  3. Keep a simple note of how your feet and hands feel each week so you can judge the effect fairly.
  4. Support it with healthy habits: regular walking, balanced meals and, if you have diabetes, the blood sugar plan your doctor has agreed with you.
  5. Use the guarantee window instead of deciding too early.

Is Nervala safe? Side effects

Alpha-lipoic acid and benfotiamine are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and Nervala is described as free from caffeine and stimulants. The most common complaints with these ingredients are mild, such as an upset stomach.

  • If you have diabetes, check with your doctor before adding ALA, because it can affect blood sugar and your medicine may need to be reviewed.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Numbness, tingling or burning that is new, spreading or getting worse should be looked at by a doctor, because it can have causes a supplement will not address.

Nervala FAQ

Does Nervala work?

Nervala is built around alpha-lipoic acid and benfotiamine, two nutrients that have been studied in people with nerve discomfort and showed improvements in symptoms in several trials. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and results vary from person to person.

Is Nervala legit?

Yes. Nervala is a real product from Barton Nutrition, part of Barton Publishing, with published contact details, US phone support and a 365-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Nervala cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $67 plus shipping, 3 bottles cost $177 ($59 each) and 6 bottles cost $294 ($49 each), with free US shipping on the larger packages.

How long does Nervala take to work?

The sales page suggests some people notice changes within days, but the research on its ingredients measured results over several weeks, so we suggest giving it at least a month or two of daily use.

How do I get a refund on Nervala?

Within 365 days of your order, contact the US customer service team and ask for a refund. You can keep the free Blood Sugar Solution Kit bonus. Ask when you call whether bottles must be returned.

Does Nervala have side effects?

Alpha-lipoic acid and benfotiamine are usually well tolerated, with mild stomach upset the most common complaint. People with diabetes, anyone pregnant or nursing, and anyone on prescription medicine should check with a doctor first.

Final verdict: is Nervala worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants simple, well-chosen nutritional support for their nerves, Nervala is a focused and sensibly priced formula, and it earns our score of 4.6 out of 5.
