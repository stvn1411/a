What is Zeneara and how is it meant to work?

Zeneara is an easy-to-swallow capsule built around seven ingredients. The company describes it as supporting the "nerve environment of the ear", and its formula pairs two kinds of ingredients: those with a long history in ear and circulation support, such as ginkgo biloba, zinc and taurine, and calming plants, such as valerian and passion flower, which are traditionally used to promote relaxation.

That second group is a thoughtful touch. Many people who think about ear health also feel the strain of everyday noise, poor sleep and stress, and a formula that addresses relaxation as well is a sensible, well-rounded design.

The capsules contain no stimulants, and they are taken twice a day: one on waking and one before bed. There is a similar-style ear formula in liquid form on our site too, so see our ZenCortex review if you prefer drops.

Does Zeneara work?

Here is what the research can and cannot tell us about the ingredients in Zeneara:

  • A systematic review of randomized, placebo-controlled trials covering 16 studies and 1,093 people found that valerian might improve sleep quality without side effects, although most of the studies had methodological limits.[4]
  • A Cochrane review of ginkgo biloba for tinnitus (four trials, 1,543 participants) found the evidence limited and not enough to show benefit when tinnitus is the main complaint.[3] The US National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders adds that no vitamin, herbal extract or supplement has been proven to cure tinnitus.[2]
  • Protecting your ears day to day matters most: the NIDCD notes that long or repeated exposure to sounds at or above 85 decibels can cause hearing loss, and that earplugs or earmuffs help.[5]

What this means for you: Zeneara is best thought of as an ear-and-relaxation wellness formula, not a treatment. Its calming ingredients have the strongest research, and the generous guarantee lets you judge it for yourself. Keep protecting your ears from loud noise, and see a doctor about any change in hearing.

How the 365-day money-back guarantee works

Zeneara offers one of the longest guarantees we have come across: a refund up to 365 days (12 months) after purchase if you are not satisfied. In summary:

  1. You have 365 days from your purchase to ask for a refund.
  2. Email the support team at support@zeneara.com to start your refund request.
  3. Zeneara's shipping page lists a return address in Tallmadge, Ohio. It does not say who pays return postage, so ask support before you send anything back.
  4. The company asks that you give Zeneara a fair chance to work before you request a refund.

Helpful tip: note the date you start in your calendar and set a reminder a month before the year is up. With a guarantee this long it is easy to forget the deadline.

Zeneara is sold through ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which can also help with order questions through its website. The shipping page lists delivery of about 5 to 7 business days after 3 to 5 days of order preparation, while the product page quotes 3 to 5 business days, so allow up to about two weeks for your first bottle.

How to get the most out of Zeneara

  1. Take one capsule on waking and one before bed, as directed. You can take them with or without food.
  2. Be consistent. The company suggests giving it time, and the larger packages cover a longer trial.
  3. Protect your ears in loud places with earplugs or earmuffs, and keep headphone volume moderate.[5]
  4. Wind down in the evening. The bedtime capsule fits nicely with a calm routine and regular sleep hours.
  5. Note how you feel over a few weeks, including relaxation, sleep and any changes in how your ears feel.

Is Zeneara safe? Side effects

Zeneara contains plant extracts and common nutrients that are generally well tolerated, and it has no stimulants. A few cautions apply:

  • If you take blood thinners, sedatives, sleep medicine or other prescription medicine, check with your doctor first. Ginkgo can affect bleeding, and valerian and passion flower have calming effects.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition, ask your doctor before you start, as the company itself advises.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden hearing loss, new ringing in one ear, dizziness or ear pain. These need a professional check rather than a supplement.[2]

Zeneara FAQ

Does Zeneara work?

Zeneara combines ear-related nutrients with calming botanicals, and valerian has research suggesting it may improve sleep quality. Evidence that ginkgo helps tinnitus is limited, so we see Zeneara as a wellness formula rather than a treatment. Results vary, and the 365-day guarantee lets you judge for yourself.

Is Zeneara legit?

Yes. Zeneara is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with published support contact details, a one-time payment and a 365-day money-back guarantee.

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $59, 3 bottles cost $147 and 6 bottles cost $234, with free shipping on the larger packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.30 to $1.97 per day.

How long does Zeneara take to work?
