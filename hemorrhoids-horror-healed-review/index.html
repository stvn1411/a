What is Hemorrhoids Healing Protocol and how is it meant to work?

The Hemorrhoids Healing Protocol is a digital guide by Scott Davis, presented through the Blue Heron Health News site. It is built on a simple idea: everyday habits around food and movement play a big part in how comfortable your bowels are, so changing them is a sensible place to start.

The guide covers two areas. First, food: which everyday foods are rich in the kinds of fiber and plant compounds that support smoother bowel movements, and how to turn them into quick, tasty recipes. It is not a diet, and the foods are ordinary supermarket items. Second, movement: gentle exercises, most done sitting or lying down, that are meant to support healthy bowels and ease discomfort, along with a reminder to sit less.

Everything is organized into a 3-week plan so you don't have to work out how to apply the information yourself, and the guide also includes quick-start tips for day-to-day comfort while the plan gets going.

Do food and fiber changes help with hemorrhoids?

The food side of this guide is the part with the clearest research behind it:

  • A systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 7 randomized trials with 378 people and found that fiber had a consistent beneficial effect on symptoms and bleeding in symptomatic hemorrhoids. Trial quality was moderate for most outcomes.[1]
  • One of the trials in that review also suggested fiber may reduce how often symptoms return.[1]

The movement side fits with common sense: breaking up long periods of sitting and keeping bowels regular are habits many doctors recommend for comfort.

What this means for you: a food plan built around fiber is a research-backed place to begin, and this guide turns that into a plan you can follow. The full protocol hasn't been through its own clinical trial, so use it as a helpful routine alongside, not instead of, your doctor's advice, and see a doctor about any bleeding.

How to get the most out of Hemorrhoids Healing Protocol

  1. Read the plan through first, then follow the 3-week schedule in order.
  2. Add the foods gradually. Increasing fiber slowly and drinking enough water helps your body adjust.
  3. Pair movements with a routine such as your favourite TV show, as the author suggests, so they become a habit.
  4. Break up long sitting with short walks.
  5. Note how you feel each week, and tell your doctor about anything that isn't improving.

Is Hemorrhoids Healing Protocol safe? What to know first

The guide is about food and gentle movement, with no drugs or devices. Adding fiber can cause temporary gas or bloating if you increase it too quickly, so go step by step and drink water.

  • The author advises checking with your doctor first if you are underweight, because some people notice weight changes.
  • See a doctor for any rectal bleeding, particularly if it is new, heavy or dark, as well as for severe or lasting pain, a lump that doesn't settle or a change in bowel habits. These need a professional check.
  • Check with your doctor first if you are pregnant, have a bowel condition or take regular medicine.
