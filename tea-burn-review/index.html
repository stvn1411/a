What is Tea Burn and how is it meant to work?

Tea Burn is a dissolvable packet that goes into your tea. The maker describes it as tasteless and says it dissolves instantly, whatever kind of tea you drink and however you take it.

The idea is simple. Tea already brings plant compounds to the cup, and Tea Burn adds a proprietary nutritional complex and vitamins designed to work with it for metabolism and energy support. There's nothing new to learn: you make your tea as usual and stir in one packet, in the morning or at any other time of day.

The official page is light on label detail. It describes the formula as patent-pending and mentions immune-supporting vitamins, but it doesn't list each ingredient or its amount, so this review is open about what we could and couldn't check.

Does Tea Burn work?

We couldn't find a clinical trial on the finished Tea Burn formula, so we looked at the research on tea, the one ingredient we know you'll be pairing it with. It's encouraging:

  • A meta-analysis of 15 randomized trials with 1,243 people found that green tea catechins taken with caffeine lowered body weight by about 1.4 kg compared with caffeine alone. The authors describe the effect as modest, and catechins without caffeine did not show a benefit.[2]
  • That finding suits a tea habit: a cup of tea is where catechins and caffeine already come together.

What this means for you: the effects in these studies are modest, so think of Tea Burn as a supportive extra on top of balanced eating, regular movement and good sleep, not a shortcut. The maker itself suggests at least 90 to 180 days of consistent use, so give it a steady few weeks before judging how you feel.

How to get the most out of Tea Burn

  1. Mix one packet into your tea each day. The maker suggests the morning, though any time of day works.
  2. Be consistent. The maker suggests at least 90 to 180 days, and studies on tea catechins ran for weeks to months.
  3. Read the label on your pouch so you know exactly what you're taking.
  4. Keep the basics in place: balanced meals, regular movement and good sleep do most of the work.
  5. Watch your total caffeine. Tea contains caffeine, so keep an eye on how many cups you drink, especially late in the day.

Is Tea Burn safe? Side effects

The official page describes the formula as free of artificial stimulants and made in a GMP-certified facility in the USA, but it doesn't name the ingredients, so reading the label is the best way to know exactly what you're taking. The main thing to watch is caffeine from the tea itself: people who are sensitive may notice jitters, a faster heartbeat or trouble sleeping.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting any supplement.
  • If you already take other products containing green tea extract or stimulants, check the total with your doctor or pharmacist.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Stop and see a doctor if you notice unusual symptoms such as yellowing of the skin or eyes, dark urine, chest pain or a racing heart.

Tea Burn FAQ

Does Tea Burn work?

Tea Burn is designed to be mixed into tea, and research on tea catechins taken with caffeine shows a modest effect on body weight. The finished Tea Burn formula has not had its own clinical trial that we could find, and its ingredients are not named on the official page, so results vary from person to person.

Is Tea Burn legit?

Yes. Tea Burn is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, a published support email and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Tea Burn cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 2 pouches cost $158 plus shipping, 3 pouches cost $207 plus shipping and 6 pouches cost $294 with free shipping. The packages are labeled as 60, 90 and 180 days of supply, so the 6-pouch package works out to about $1.63 per day.

How long does Tea Burn take to work?

The maker suggests taking a packet with your tea every day for at least 90 to 180 days. Studies on tea catechins typically ran for several weeks to a few months.

How do I get a refund on Tea Burn?

Within 60 days of purchase, email support@teaburn.com or call the toll-free number on the order page, and return the product, even empty pouches. The refund is issued within 48 hours of the product being returned, less shipping and handling.

Does Tea Burn have side effects?

The official page says the formula has no artificial stimulants, but it does not name the ingredients, so it is sensible to check the label. Your tea still contains caffeine. People who are pregnant or nursing, take medication or have a medical condition should ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is Tea Burn worth it?

Yes. For anyone who already enjoys a daily cup of tea, Tea Burn is an easy, low-effort and sensibly priced addition, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It's tasteless, takes seconds, and goes into a drink with real human research behind it. Keep up balanced meals, movement and sleep, and let Tea Burn do its part on top. With a 60-day money-back guarantee and no subscription, there's very little to lose by giving it a try.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-pouch package gives you the best price per day, free shipping and enough time to see how you feel.
