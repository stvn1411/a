What is Always Eat After 7 PM and how is it meant to work?

Always Eat After 7 PM is a diet book by Joel Marion, a fitness professional who says he gained almost 50 pounds in under a year before rethinking how he ate. The book is built around one friendly idea: you don't need to stop eating at 7 pm to lose weight.

Many of us have been told that food after a certain hour automatically turns into fat. The book argues that what you eat, how much and how it fits your day matter more than the clock, and gives you a plan with evening meals, desserts and bedtime snacks included.

The offer page gives away a free hardcopy and asks you only to help with shipping. You also get instant access to the ebook and audiobook, and three bonus guides.

Does Always Eat After 7 PM work?

We did not find a trial of this specific book. The wider research on eating late is mixed, and it helps to know what it does and doesn't say:

  • A review of the health impact of nighttime eating concluded that the effect depends on what and how much is eaten, and that small evening snacks of about 150 kcal do not appear harmful.[1]
  • The same review noted that adverse effects of nighttime feeding appeared to disappear in obese people when paired with exercise training.[1]
  • The book's wider claims, including larger evening meals, come from the author's own experience and approach. The offer page says he lost 46 pounds, and results will vary.[2]

What this means for you: the book is a relaxed way to stop dreading the evening, and the research supports at least the idea that a sensible evening snack is not a problem. Keep an eye on total calories and portions, add activity, and use the 60-day refund if it isn't for you.

How to get the most out of Always Eat After 7 PM

  1. Start with the Quick Start Guide so you know what a typical day looks like.
  2. Plan your evenings. Pick a dinner and a bedtime snack from the 75 recipes ahead of time.
  3. Watch portions and total calories. The research on night eating is about sensible amounts.
  4. Add movement. Use the Exercise Guide, or any activity you enjoy.
  5. Track how you feel. Check weight, energy and sleep every couple of weeks.

Is Always Eat After 7 PM safe? Who should check first

The book is a diet and recipe guide, so there are no pills or devices involved. The main thing to keep in mind is that eating habits affect some health conditions.

  • Check with your doctor first if you have diabetes, reflux, a history of disordered eating or take medicine that affects blood sugar.
  • People who get heartburn when lying down may find large late meals uncomfortable, so go gently.
  • If you are pregnant or have another health condition, ask your doctor before changing how you eat.
  • Rapid weight loss claims should be taken with caution; steady changes are easier to keep.

How Always Eat After 7 PM compares with other options

  • Strict diets: plans like keto or paleo ban whole food groups. Always Eat After 7 PM is deliberately flexible.
  • Time-restricted eating: some people do better with an eating window. This book takes the opposite view on evenings, so pick what suits your routine.
  • Professional guidance: a registered dietitian can build a plan around your health history. Nothing replaces that.
