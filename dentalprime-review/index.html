What is DentalPrime and how is it meant to work?

DentalPrime is a daily tablet built around a short list of ingredients that connect to teeth and gums: vitamin D3, vitamin K2 and a form of calcium called microcrystalline hydroxyapatite, plus xylitol, cranberry powder and zinc citrate.

Hydroxyapatite is the mineral that tooth enamel and bone are made of, and vitamin D and K2 are nutrients involved in how the body handles calcium. Xylitol, cranberry and zinc are familiar names in oral care. Together they make a formula that is easy to understand, which we like.

Unlike a probiotic, DentalPrime works as a general nutrition-style supplement, so it suits people who prefer a straightforward tablet to a chewable with live bacteria. The official page describes it as supporting healthy gums and teeth, maintaining oral and gut balance and promoting fresh breath.

Does DentalPrime work?

Here's what the research says about the ingredients in DentalPrime:

  • In a randomized controlled trial in people with gingivitis, daily vitamin D for 3 months improved gum scores in proportion to the dose, with the placebo group showing no significant effect. This is the clearest human evidence for any single ingredient in the formula.[2]
  • A Cochrane review of 10 studies with 5,903 participants found xylitol toothpaste may protect children's permanent teeth better than fluoride-only toothpaste. The authors rated the evidence as low quality and found too little evidence on other xylitol products.[3]
  • In a six-month study, a toothpaste with zinc citrate and triclosan kept plaque lower and gums healthier than a control. DentalPrime contains zinc citrate as a tablet, so the effect may differ.[4]

What this means for you: DentalPrime is built from familiar, sensible ingredients, and vitamin D has human evidence for gum inflammation. The finished formula hasn't published its own clinical trial and the amounts aren't listed, so give it a few weeks of daily use alongside your usual routine, and use the generous guarantee if it isn't for you.

How the 365-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 365-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest we've seen. To use it:

  1. Write to support at support@thedentalprime.com within the guarantee period and say you'd like a refund.
  2. The official page says you can return what you haven't used for a full refund.
  3. The shipping page says to email support first and to return the item in its original packaging to the address support gives you (the company's address is in Tallmadge, Ohio). It doesn't say who pays return postage, so ask when you write.

Helpful tip: the two pages describe returns slightly differently (“what you haven't used” versus unopened packaging), so email support early, before you open the last bottles, and ask exactly what they need. Keep your order confirmation and tracking details.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and ClickBank's customer support can help with order questions. US orders are processed in 3 to 5 business days and typically arrive in 5 to 7 business days, according to the shipping page.

How to get the most out of DentalPrime

  1. Take one tablet a day, as the official page directs, at a time you won't forget, such as with breakfast.
  2. Be consistent. The gum research on vitamin D measured changes over about 3 months of daily use.
  3. Keep up the basics: brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning between your teeth and regular check-ups.
  4. Check your other supplements. If you already take vitamin D, calcium or zinc, mention DentalPrime to your doctor or pharmacist.
  5. Keep the bottle out of reach of pets. Xylitol is harmful to dogs.

Is DentalPrime safe? Side effects

The ingredients in DentalPrime are common nutrients and are generally well tolerated at ordinary intakes. Because amounts aren't listed, it's worth being thoughtful if you already take supplements.

  • Vitamin K can interact with blood-thinning medicine, so check with your doctor first if you take one. The official page also asks pregnant or nursing readers and anyone on medication to speak to a doctor.
  • Combining several supplements that contain vitamin D, calcium or zinc can add up, so tell your doctor or pharmacist what you already take.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth: these need a professional check.

How DentalPrime compares with other options

  • Oral probiotics: products like the one in our ProvaDent review add live bacteria; DentalPrime takes a nutrient-and-mineral approach instead. Choose whichever style you'd find easier to stick with.
  • Fluoride or xylitol toothpaste and mouthwash: these work directly on the tooth surface and are worth keeping in your routine alongside any supplement.
  • Professional care: nothing replaces regular dental cleanings and check-ups.

DentalPrime FAQ

Does DentalPrime work?

DentalPrime combines vitamin D3, vitamin K2, hydroxyapatite calcium, xylitol, cranberry and zinc. Vitamin D has a randomized trial showing improved gum scores in people with gingivitis, and the other ingredients are familiar in oral care. The finished formula has not published its own clinical trial, and results vary from person to person.

Is DentalPrime legit?

Yes. DentalPrime is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, a published support email and a 365-day money-back guarantee.

How much does DentalPrime cost?

On the official page at the time of our review: 2 bottles cost $147.99 including $9.99 shipping, 4 bottles cost $245.99 including $9.99 shipping and 6 bottles cost $294 with free US shipping. Each bottle is a one-month supply, so the cost is roughly $1.63 to $2.47 per day.

How long does DentalPrime take to work?

The official page doesn't give a timeline. The vitamin D gum trial we found measured changes over 3 months of daily use.

How do I get a refund on DentalPrime?

Email support@thedentalprime.com within the 365-day guarantee period and ask for a refund. The company asks that returns be arranged with support first, so ask them what to send back and who pays postage.

Does DentalPrime have side effects?

The ingredients are common nutrients and are usually well tolerated. People taking blood-thinning medicine, who are pregnant or nursing, or who have a medical condition should ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is DentalPrime worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a simple, clearly labelled daily tablet to go with their brushing routine, DentalPrime is a sensible and low-risk addition, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It uses six familiar ingredients, one of which has a human trial for gum inflammation, it takes seconds to take, and its 365-day money-back guarantee leaves plenty of time to decide. Keep brushing and seeing your dentist, and let DentalPrime do its part on top.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-bottle package gives you the best price per day, free US shipping and six months to see how it suits you.
