What is Keravita Pro and how is it meant to work?

Keravita Pro is a capsule created by a researcher the sales page introduces as Benjamin Jones, who says he built it around a simple idea: healthy nails and hair are supported by what you take in as well as what you put on them.

The formula brings together vitamins and plant ingredients that people already know from the kitchen and the supplement aisle. The official page names curcumin, cat's claw, garlic, quercetin, pomegranate and olive; it describes the formula as a blend and does not publish a full label with amounts on the page we reviewed.

The sales page pairs the product with practical foot and nail habits: keeping feet clean and dry, wearing breathable cotton or linen socks, and avoiding going barefoot in public wet areas. We like that, because those habits matter as much as any supplement.

Does Keravita Pro work? What the research says

Keravita Pro itself has not been through a published clinical trial, so we looked at what is known about its ingredients and about nail care in general:

  • Individual ingredients are well known and widely used. Garlic, for example, has randomized-trial evidence for modest effects on a general inflammation marker.[2]
  • We did not find published human trials of this ingredient combination for nail or hair outcomes, so we describe the capsule as supportive, not as a treatment.
  • Nail health is slow to change. Nails grow slowly, so any change from a supplement would take months to show, and the manufacturer's own refund page suggests giving supplements time before judging.
  • If a nail is thick, discoloured or painful, that can be a nail infection (onychomycosis), which is a medical issue. Reviews of treatment note that it often needs prescribed treatment and that topical products struggle to penetrate the nail.[3]

What this means for you: Keravita Pro is a sensible, well-priced daily supplement for people who want to support healthy-looking nails and hair, and it works best alongside good foot care. It has not been tested as a finished formula, and it is not a replacement for medical treatment of a nail infection.

How to get the most out of Keravita Pro

  1. Follow the directions on the bottle and take it at the same time each day.
  2. Give it time. Nails and hair grow slowly, so judge over months, not days.
  3. Dry your feet well after washing, especially between and around the toes, and wear breathable cotton or linen socks, as the sales page advises.
  4. Avoid walking barefoot in pools and gym showers, and trim nails with clean, disinfected tools.
  5. Log your progress with a photo every couple of weeks so you can judge fairly before the guarantee window ends.

Is Keravita Pro safe? Side effects

The manufacturer states that the capsules are non-GMO, free of stimulants and not habit forming. Because the full ingredient amounts are not published, it is sensible to be cautious if you take medicines.

  • Garlic and curcumin supplements can interact with some medicines, such as blood thinners, so ask your doctor or pharmacist first if you take prescription medicine.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for nails that are thick, discoloured, painful or crumbling, and especially if you have diabetes or poor circulation.
