What is NewEra Protect and how is it meant to work?

NewEra Protect is a daily dietary supplement positioned around bladder comfort and pelvic floor health. The official page describes it as 100% natural, built from plant ingredients, sugar free, non-GMO, free of stimulants and non-habit forming.

The idea behind it is a combined one: give the body nutrients and plant extracts that research has explored in connection with bladder and urinary comfort, and pair that with gentle strengthening work for the pelvic floor. That is why the 6-bottle package includes two movement programs, Pelvic Floor Strong and Simple Strength, which the seller describes as step-by-step video routines you can do lying down, sitting or standing.

One thing worth knowing up front: the official page is built around a video, and the page text does not show a supplement facts panel. We therefore describe the formula only as far as the page does, and we list the research families the page cites rather than claiming a specific ingredient list.

Does NewEra Protect's approach work?

The research behind this kind of routine is genuinely encouraging, especially when a supplement is paired with pelvic floor exercise:

  • A Cochrane review of 31 trials with 1,817 women found that pelvic floor muscle training cured or improved stress incontinence in around 74% of women compared with 11% in control groups (3 trials, 242 women), and led the authors to suggest it as part of first-line care.[2]
  • A frankincense-based herbal product reduced leakage frequency and amount against placebo in postmenopausal women, and its effects at two weeks after stopping were reported as greater than the standard drug it was compared with.[3]
  • Correcting low vitamin D reduced stress incontinence severity scores in a three-month placebo-controlled trial.[4]

What this means for you: pairing a daily plant-based supplement with a movement routine is a sensible, research-aligned way to look after your bladder and pelvic floor, and the 6-bottle package gives you both. The finished NewEra Protect formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of daily use alongside your exercises and judge for yourself.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the seller says you can return the bottles even when they are empty. Here is how it works in the terms of sale:

  1. The refund applies to products received within 60 days of the order date, so count from the day you order rather than the day the parcel arrives.
  2. Email the support address in the terms of sale with your full name, email address and order number.
  3. Send back all opened and unopened bottles to the address given in the terms of sale. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. The refund is issued once the items arrive, less the original shipping and handling. No processing time is stated.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 40. Because the 60 days count from the order date, that leaves time to see how you are getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably.

Orders are sold through ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and the seller's terms of sale give the contact details for returns and support.

  1. Read the label when it arrives and follow the directions printed on the bottle.

  4. Keep a simple diary of daytime leaks and night-time bathroom trips so you can see your own progress.
  5. Keep up the basics: staying hydrated, limiting bladder irritants you notice affect you and keeping regular check-ups.

Is NewEra Protect safe? Side effects

Plant-based supplements are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and NewEra Protect is described as stimulant free and non-habit forming. As with any supplement, a small number of people notice mild digestive changes when they start.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing or taking prescription medicine, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first. The official page gives the same advice.
  • Because the formula is not shown on the page, check the bottle label if you have allergies to plants or herbs.
  • Dietary supplements have not been evaluated by the FDA for effectiveness, and the seller states this on its page.
  • See a doctor for leakage that starts suddenly, is painful, comes with burning, fever or blood in the urine, or does not improve.
