What is Vertigo and Dizziness Program and how is it meant to work?

The Vertigo and Dizziness Program, also called the Natural Vertigo and Dizziness Relief Exercise Program, is a digital guide to a series of head and balance exercises. The official video page describes a routine of about 15 minutes.

The idea is that regular, guided movement can retrain the balance system, which relies on the inner ear, vision and the body's sense of position. Exercise-based approaches of this kind are widely used by physical therapists for balance problems.

The program is sold as a download, so you can start straight away, with the option of adding a printed copy for the cost of printing after you order.

Does Vertigo and Dizziness Program work?

Research on exercise-based approaches to dizziness is among the more encouraging in this field:

  • A Cochrane review of 39 studies with 2,441 participants concluded that there is moderate to strong evidence that vestibular rehabilitation is a safe, effective management for unilateral peripheral vestibular dysfunction.[1]
  • Clinical guidelines for benign paroxysmal positional vertigo, a common cause of spinning dizziness, cover diagnosis and specific repositioning manoeuvres, which is why finding the cause first matters.[2]

What this means for you: exercise-based approaches have real research behind them, and a low-cost guide is a convenient way to practise. The research studied supervised programs for particular inner-ear conditions rather than this guide, so check the cause of your dizziness with a doctor first and use the program as a supplement to that advice.

How to get the most out of Vertigo and Dizziness Program

  1. Get a diagnosis first. Dizziness has many causes, and the right exercises depend on yours.
  2. Start slowly in a safe place, sitting or near a wall or sturdy support, in case you feel unsteady.
  3. Practise at the same time every day so the routine becomes a habit.
  4. Keep a simple diary of how often spells happen and how strong they feel, so you can see changes over several weeks.
  5. Tell your doctor if exercises make symptoms worse, and stop until you have been checked.

Is Vertigo and Dizziness Program safe? Side effects

Balance exercises can briefly bring on dizziness while you do them, which is part of how the balance system adapts, but they can also make you unsteady. Practise in a safe, supported place.

The program is educational and does not replace a medical assessment of the cause of your dizziness.

  • Get sudden, severe or new dizziness checked by a doctor, especially with headache, double vision, weakness, numbness, trouble speaking or hearing loss. Seek urgent care if these appear suddenly.
  • Check with your doctor first if you have neck or spine problems or are at risk of falling.
  • Ask a doctor or pharmacist whether any medicine you take may be causing dizziness.
