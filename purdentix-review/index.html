What is PurDentix and how is it meant to work?

PurDentix is a chewable lozenge that you let dissolve or chew slowly once a day. Because it is taken in the mouth rather than swallowed whole, its probiotics spend time where your teeth and gums are.

The idea comes from the study of the oral microbiome, the community of bacteria that lives in your mouth. When it drifts out of balance, gums are more likely to become irritated. Oral probiotics aim to support a healthier balance with beneficial strains, and this approach has been tested in many dental trials.

PurDentix combines four probiotic species with organic mint leaf for a fresh taste and chicory root, a prebiotic fibre that helps feed beneficial bacteria. The page calls the six ingredients "The Super 6" and describes the formula as 100% plant-based.

Do oral probiotics like PurDentix work?

The research on oral probiotics is genuinely encouraging, especially for gums:

  • A meta-analysis of 50 clinical trials with more than 3,200 participants found that probiotics reduced gum bleeding, gum inflammation and gum pocket depth compared with placebo.[6]
  • In a randomized trial, people with gum disease who took a B. lactis probiotic alongside a professional deep cleaning had better pocket depth and gum attachment after 90 days than those who had the cleaning alone.[5]
  • Tablets containing L. salivarius WB21 lowered the count of five gum-disease bacteria in the plaque of healthy volunteers within 4 weeks.[3]
  • Benefits were strongest for gum health; the same review did not find a clear effect on cavities, so keep brushing with fluoride toothpaste.[6]

What this means for you: oral probiotics are a research-backed way to support healthier gums, and PurDentix puts four well-studied species into an easy daily chewable. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet, so give it a few weeks of daily use alongside your usual routine to see how it suits you.

How to get the most out of PurDentix

  1. Chew it slowly or let it dissolve, once a day, so the probiotics stay in your mouth longer.
  2. Avoid rinsing straight afterwards to give the bacteria time in your mouth.
  3. Be consistent. Studies on oral probiotics typically measured results after 4 to 12 weeks of daily use.[6]
  4. Keep up the basics: brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning between your teeth and regular check-ups.
  5. Notice the changes: how your gums feel when you brush, and how fresh your breath is in the morning.

Is PurDentix safe? Side effects

Probiotics are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and PurDentix contains no stimulants. The most common side effect of probiotics in general is mild, temporary bloating or a change in digestion as your body adjusts.

  • If you are immunocompromised, pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting any probiotic.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[7]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth: these need a professional check.

How PurDentix compares with other options

  • Mouthwash: antiseptic rinses kill bacteria broadly. PurDentix takes the opposite approach, adding beneficial bacteria. If you use an antiseptic mouthwash, take PurDentix at a different time of day.
