What is Pineal 10X and how is it meant to work?

Pineal 10X is a liquid supplement that you take by eye-dropper each morning, either straight or mixed into tea, coffee or juice. The makers position it as a blend that supports general wellness, healthy body function and steady energy, and the name refers to the pineal gland, a small gland in the brain involved in sleep and body rhythms.

The formula is built from familiar ingredients: two vitamins (B6 and C), the mineral iodine, turmeric, and four plants traditionally used for vitality, namely damiana, ashwagandha, maca and tribulus.

We'd describe it as a general wellness blend. The sales page uses expressive language about the pineal gland, but we didn't find published research showing what a supplement like this does for that gland specifically, so our review focuses on what is known about the ingredients themselves.

Does Pineal 10X work?

Pineal 10X itself hasn't been tested in its own clinical trial, so the best guide is the research on its main ingredients:

  • A 2024 meta-analysis of nine randomized trials with 558 people found that ashwagandha was linked with lower scores on stress and anxiety scales and with lower cortisol.[2]
  • An updated meta-analysis of curcumin trials found a benefit for global cognition, strongest with 24 weeks or more of use and in people aged 60 and over.[3]
  • In a 12-week double-blind trial, red and black maca extracts were linked with improvements in mood, energy and health status compared with placebo.[4]
  • Most of the research is on single ingredients taken on their own, so the combined liquid is best seen as a general wellness blend.

What this means for you: several ingredients in Pineal 10X, especially ashwagandha, have encouraging research, and the blend is simple and stimulant-free. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of steady use and judge how you feel. Results vary from person to person.

How to get the most out of Pineal 10X

  1. Shake the bottle well and take one dropper a day, preferably in the morning, as the directions say.
  2. Make it a habit: add it to your morning tea, coffee or juice so you don't forget.
  3. Give it a few weeks, as most herb studies measured results after four to twelve weeks.
  4. Support it with the basics: regular sleep, daylight, exercise and a balanced diet.
  5. Show the label to your doctor if you take medicine, so they can check for interactions.

Is Pineal 10X safe? Side effects

The ingredients in Pineal 10X are familiar herbs and nutrients and the formula is stimulant-free. A few points are still worth knowing. Ashwagandha is generally well tolerated in trials, although rare cases of liver problems have been reported, so people with liver conditions should be careful. Iodine matters for the thyroid, so anyone with a thyroid condition should check with their doctor first.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, have a thyroid, liver or hormone-related condition, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Turmeric can cause mild stomach upset in some people, and the sales page says to show the bottle to your doctor if you take medication.
  • Stop and speak to a professional if you notice stomach upset that doesn't settle, yellowing of the skin or eyes, or any unusual symptom.

Pineal 10X combines familiar ingredients such as ashwagandha, turmeric and maca that have published research for stress, mood and cognition. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet, and results vary from person to person, so most people give it a few weeks of steady use.
