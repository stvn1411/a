What is NervoLink and how is it meant to work?

NervoLink is a capsule supplement that combines nine medicinal and edible mushrooms. The official page presents it as a way to support the health of the peripheral nerves in the arms, hands and feet while also supporting energy levels.

The formula is described as a founder-created blend made from natural ingredients, produced in a GMP-certified US facility, non-GMO, with no dangerous stimulants and nothing habit forming. The page also says a third party verified the purity of the ingredients.

Mushrooms have a long history as food and traditional remedies, and lion's mane in particular has been studied in people. Below we look at what the research says about the lead ingredient and where the evidence is still developing.

Do mushroom supplements like NervoLink work?

The research is strongest for the lead ingredient, and still developing for the blend as a whole:

  • In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, adults aged 50 to 80 with mild cognitive impairment who took lion's mane for 16 weeks scored significantly higher on a cognitive scale than the placebo group at weeks 8, 12 and 16, and laboratory tests showed no adverse effect.[2]
  • That benefit faded when the mushroom was stopped, which suggests steady use matters.[2]
  • A 2025 acute study in 18 healthy young adults found no significant overall effect from a single dose, so benefits may depend on the person, the dose and the time taken.[3] These trials looked at thinking, not nerve pain, so they support the ingredient rather than the finished product.

What this means for you: lion's mane is a well-chosen lead ingredient with real human research behind it, and NervoLink places it in a blend of eight other mushrooms. The finished capsule has not had its own clinical trial, so give it consistent use over a couple of months and use the guarantee if it is not for you.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the official page says it applies whether or not you have used the bottles. Here is how to use it:

  1. The 60 days start from the day your order is shipped, which you can see in the shipping email or on the package.
  2. Fill in the Return and Refund Form that came with your order.
  3. Send all the bottles back to NervoLink, 19655 E 35th Dr #100, Aurora, CO 80011, USA. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. You still qualify if you post the bottles on day 59 and they arrive a few days later. The refund is processed within a few days of the package arriving, and you get an email when it is done.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50. That gives you time to see how you're getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably if you need it.

Questions can go to contact@nervolink-product.com. Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which can also help with order questions.

Is NervoLink safe? Side effects

Mushrooms are food-type ingredients, and NervoLink contains no stimulants according to the manufacturer. Even so, supplements affect people differently.

  • The official page advises checking with your physician if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or have a medical condition.
  • Numbness, tingling or pain in the hands and feet can have many causes, including diabetes and vitamin deficiencies, so see a doctor if it is new, worsening or unexplained.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Stop taking it and speak to a doctor if you notice an unusual reaction.

NervoLink FAQ

Does NervoLink work?

NervoLink is built on nine mushrooms led by lion's mane, which has human research for cognition in older adults with mild cognitive impairment. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and those studies did not look at nerve pain, so results vary from person to person.

Is NervoLink legit?

Yes. NervoLink is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, published contact details and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does NervoLink cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $69, 3 bottles cost $177 and 6 bottles cost $294, all with free US shipping. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.63 to $2.30 per day.

How long does NervoLink take to work?

Mushroom research typically measures changes over weeks to months; the lion's mane trial we cite showed differences from week 8. The manufacturer advises taking some time with the supplement before drawing a conclusion.

How do I get a refund on NervoLink?

Within 60 days of the shipping date, fill in the Return and Refund Form that came with your order and send all the bottles back to NervoLink in Aurora, Colorado, even if used. Return postage is paid by the customer, and the refund is processed within a few days of the package arriving.

Does NervoLink have side effects?

The manufacturer says it has no dangerous stimulants and is not habit forming. People who are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or managing a medical condition should ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is NervoLink worth it?

Yes. For readers interested in a mushroom-based supplement for nerve support, NervoLink is a well-explained, fairly priced option, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. Its lead ingredient has genuine human research behind it, it is made in the USA, there is no subscription, and the 60-day guarantee even covers used bottles.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-bottle package gives the best price per day and enough time to judge it properly. Talk to your doctor about any ongoing numbness or tingling.
