What is BioDentex and how is it meant to work?

BioDentex is a capsule you take with water each day. Rather than a mouthwash or paste, it is an "inside-out" addition to your routine, built around a short list of familiar ingredients: xylitol, peppermint, cranberries, grape seed extract, carrot powder and stevia.

Xylitol is the headline ingredient. It is a sugar substitute that oral bacteria cannot easily feed on, and it has been studied in dental research for years, mostly as chewing gum and lozenges. The other ingredients are plant-based extras chosen for flavour, freshness and general plant nutrition.

The product page says the formula has "11+ ingredients and nutrients" but names six, so we reviewed the six that are named.

Does BioDentex work? What the research says

BioDentex has not had its own clinical trial, so the best guide is the research on its lead ingredient, xylitol:

  • In a randomized trial of 204 high-risk schoolchildren, six months of daily xylitol chewing gum produced better plaque pH and lower salivary mutans streptococci than the control gum.[1]
  • Xylitol is the most studied of the BioDentex ingredients, and it fits the product's goal of a sugar-free, mouth-friendly formula.
  • The trial used chewing gum at a high daily dose, and BioDentex does not publish its per-capsule amount, so results may differ.

What this means for you: BioDentex is built on a sensible, familiar base: xylitol, with peppermint and plant extras to round it out. Think of it as a gentle daily addition on top of brushing, flossing and dental visits, and give it a couple of months so you can judge how your mouth feels.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee, so you can try BioDentex with confidence. Here is how it works, based on the official shipping and returns page:

  1. The 90 days are counted from your original purchase date, and the returned package needs to arrive at the fulfillment center within that window.
  2. Contact the support team through the support link on the official site, and include your ClickBank order ID.
  3. Send back every bottle, including empty, partly used and free bonus bottles, with a note containing your order ID, full name, address, email, phone number and the packing slip if you have it.
  4. Return postage is paid by you. Once the package arrives and is confirmed, the refund goes back to your card or bank account and may take 3 to 5 business days to appear.

Helpful tip: Because the 90 days run from the purchase date and the package has to arrive in time, plan to decide by about day 70. That leaves comfortable time for shipping the bottles back.

How to get the most out of BioDentex

  1. Follow the label. Take the capsule with water as directed on the bottle.
  2. Be consistent. Daily use for a few weeks gives you a fair picture of how it suits you.
  3. Keep up the basics: brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth and keep your dental check-ups. BioDentex works best on top of these habits.
  4. Keep an eye on the changes you care about, such as how your mouth feels in the morning and how fresh your breath is.
  5. Save your order ID and the packing slip in case you ever need the guarantee.

Is BioDentex safe? Side effects

BioDentex is made from food-type ingredients and contains no stimulants. The ingredients are generally well tolerated, though everyone is different.

  • Xylitol can cause mild digestive effects such as loose stools in some people when taken in large amounts, and it is harmful to dogs, so keep the bottle out of pets' reach.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting any supplement.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[3]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth: these need a professional check.

BioDentex FAQ

Does BioDentex work?

BioDentex is built around xylitol, which has a long record in dental research, and several plant ingredients. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so results vary from person to person. It is best used daily alongside brushing, flossing and dental check-ups.

Is BioDentex legit?

Yes. BioDentex is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, published contact details and a 90-day money-back guarantee.

How much does BioDentex cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 2 bottles cost $158 plus shipping, 3 bottles cost $207 and 6 bottles cost $294, with free US shipping on the larger packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.63 to $2.63 per day.

How long does BioDentex take to work?

The official page does not give a timeline. Most dental-health studies measure changes after several weeks to a few months of daily use, so we suggest giving it at least two months.

How do I get a refund on BioDentex?

Final verdict: is BioDentex worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a simple way to do more for their smile, BioDentex is a well-priced, easy and sensibly built daily addition, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. Keep brushing and seeing your dentist, and let BioDentex do its part on top.
