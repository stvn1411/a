What is Dentolyn and how is it meant to work?

Dentolyn is a tablet you take once a day, built around a short list of ingredients: vitamin C, vitamin B6, iodine, xylitol, chlorella and a zeolite powder, with magnesium stearate to help form the tablet.

The manufacturer says it is designed to help support healthy gums and teeth, help maintain the oral and gut microbiome and promote fresh breath. It is sold through ClickBank as a one-time purchase and the order page shows no subscription.

The appeal is the simplicity. There is no long blend to decode and no complicated schedule, just one tablet a day to sit alongside the brushing and flossing you already do.

Does Dentolyn work?

We found no clinical trial on Dentolyn itself, so we looked at its main ingredients. Two have encouraging research behind them:

  • In a controlled vitamin C study, gums were less prone to inflammation and bleeding when intake moved from deficient to normal, and again to supplemental levels.[3]
  • A Cochrane review of 10 trials with 5,903 participants found that xylitol in fluoride toothpaste may reduce decay in children; results for other xylitol products were unclear.[2]

What this means for you: Dentolyn pairs a well-studied gum-health vitamin with xylitol in a simple daily tablet. The finished tablet has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a supportive habit alongside brushing, flossing and dental visits. The 365-day guarantee gives you a whole year to judge it.

How the 365-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 365-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works, based on the official page and its Shipping & Return Policy:

  1. You have 365 days from your order to ask for a refund.
  2. Email support@dentolyn.com first to say you would like to return your order.
  3. The Shipping & Return Policy asks for items to be unused and unopened, in the original packaging, while the FAQ says to return what you have not used. Ask support how this applies to your bottles before posting anything.
  4. Send the package to the return address in the policy (Tallmadge, Ohio). The policy does not say who pays return postage, so confirm this by email.

Helpful tip: with such a long guarantee it is easy to forget the deadline. Put a reminder in your calendar for around day 330, and email support early rather than at the last minute.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which can also help with order questions through the link at the bottom of the official page.

How to get the most out of Dentolyn

  1. Take one tablet a day, as the official page directs, at a time you will remember, such as with breakfast.
  2. Be consistent. Daily supplements are best judged after a couple of months of regular use.
  3. Keep up the basics: brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning between your teeth and regular dental check-ups.
  4. Eat for your gums: fruit and vegetables rich in vitamin C support the same goal as the tablet.
  5. Keep notes: how your gums feel when you brush and how fresh your mouth feels, so you can judge it well within the guarantee.

Is Dentolyn safe? Side effects

Dentolyn contains common vitamins, a trace mineral and natural powders. The official page advises showing the bottle to your doctor if you have a medical condition or take prescription medicine.

  • Iodine affects the thyroid. If you have a thyroid condition, are pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Xylitol can cause mild digestive upset in some people at higher amounts, and it is toxic to dogs, so keep the bottle out of pets' reach.
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth. These need a professional check.
