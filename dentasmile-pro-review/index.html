What is DentaSmile Pro and how is it meant to work?

DentaSmile Pro is a daily dietary supplement that the official site describes as an "8-in-1" oral care formula, combining antioxidants, minerals, vitamins and herbs for teeth and gum support. It is made in an FDA-registered facility, according to the manufacturer.

Its approach is from the inside out. Researchers have long studied the link between gum health and the way the body handles blood sugar, and several of DentaSmile Pro's ingredients are known mainly from that metabolic research. The idea is to support overall wellbeing in a way that fits alongside, not instead of, brushing and dental visits.[4]

The sales page does not state the format (for example capsule or powder), the number of servings per bottle or the directions, so check these on the official site and your package before you start.

Does DentaSmile Pro work?

No clinical trial has been published on the finished DentaSmile Pro formula, so we look at what is known about its parts and the idea behind them:

  • Berberine, the lead ingredient, has been studied in dozens of human trials, mostly for blood glucose, where results link to baseline glucose and HbA1c levels.[2]
  • Cinnamon has been studied in many small trials with small, variable effects on glucose measures.[3]
  • Reviews of trials on gum treatment and blood sugar find that the two are connected, although the measured effect is small.[4]

What this means for you: the ingredients are real, studied and used at sensible doses in other products, which makes DentaSmile Pro a reasonable daily extra. Because the amounts are not listed and the finished formula has not been tested on its own, we would treat it as a supplement to your dental routine and give it a couple of months before judging.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works, based on the official refund policy:

  1. The 60 days start from the day you receive your order.
  2. Contact the support team through the official site and follow their return instructions.
  3. Send back all the items you received to the return address in the policy, in good condition. Return postage is paid by you, and original shipping fees are deducted from the refund.
  4. After the package is received and inspected, you get an email, and an approved refund returns to your original payment method within a few days.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50. That leaves time to decide and to ship the package back before the window closes.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which can also help with any order question. The refund policy does not state a ClickBank phone number or a statement descriptor, so check your order confirmation email for those.

Is DentaSmile Pro safe? Side effects

The official site states that DentaSmile Pro has no stimulants. The ingredients are generally well tolerated by healthy adults; mild digestive upset is the most commonly reported effect of berberine and similar botanicals.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine, or manage your blood sugar with medication, check with your doctor first, since berberine, cinnamon and chromium are studied for their effect on glucose.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth; these need a professional check.
