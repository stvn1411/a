What is Eye Floaters No More and how is it meant to work?

Eye floaters are the small specks, threads or cobweb shapes that drift across your vision, most noticeable against a bright sky or a white screen. They are very common and are caused by changes in the vitreous, the clear gel that fills the eye. In one large clinic series, the causes were mostly posterior vitreous detachment (a normal age-related change) and a related change seen in nearsighted eyes.[2]

Eye Floaters No More is a guide that sets out one person's program for coping with floaters and looking after your eyes. According to the official page, it covers why floaters form, how to find out whether yours may be a sign of another eye condition, how to help prevent more from forming, and natural ways to support healthy vision. It is delivered online, so you can start reading as soon as you order.

It helps to be clear about what it is. The guide is a book-style program rather than a medical treatment, and its content is not visible before purchase. We did not purchase or read the guide, so this review looks at how it is presented, the price and the guarantee, and how it fits with what eye specialists say about floaters.

Does Eye Floaters No More work? What we know about floaters

Most reviews of this product focus on the sales page. Here is what the published research tells us about floaters, and why a low-cost guide like this can sit comfortably alongside it:

  • In a clinic series of 393 patients (651 eyes) with floaters, more than half of the eyes (55.6%) were managed by observation alone, with a procedure chosen for the rest.[2] Many floaters are simply watched, so good information and reassurance have real value.
  • A study of 333 patients at an emergency eye clinic found that a reduction in vision with new floaters or flashes was strongly linked to urgent retinal problems.[3] A guide that teaches you to recognise warning signs is therefore useful.
  • We did not find a published clinical trial of Eye Floaters No More or of a home program for removing floaters, so we cannot say it works. The testimonials on the sales page are the seller's own and individual results vary.

What this means for you: Eye Floaters No More is best seen as an inexpensive piece of education and eye care guidance, not a medical treatment. At $37 with a 60-day guarantee, the financial risk is small. If your floaters are new or suddenly worse, see an eye doctor first.

How to get the most out of Eye Floaters No More

  1. Get your eyes checked first. If you have never had your floaters examined, book a dilated eye exam before you start any program.
  2. Read the guide through once and note which parts apply to you before you change any habits.
  3. Know the warning signs. A sudden shower of new floaters, flashes of light or a shadow or curtain in your vision needs an urgent eye exam.[3]
  4. Look after your eyes: wear sunglasses outdoors, take regular screen breaks, and stay hydrated and well rested.
  5. Be patient and realistic. Many floaters are simply monitored rather than treated.[2]

Is Eye Floaters No More safe?
