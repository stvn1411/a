What is Oxydental Pro and how is it meant to work?

Oxydental Pro is a soft, chewable tablet that you take once each morning. You chew it for 10–15 seconds or swallow it with a glass of water, so it needs no preparation and fits easily around brushing.

The idea behind the formula is to combine a handful of minerals and plant ingredients that are traditionally associated with a clean, healthy mouth. Calcium is the building block of teeth and bones. Chlorophyllin and chlorella are green plant ingredients often used for freshness. Peppermint adds taste. The official page describes the blend as supporting the mouth by helping keep bacteria in check and gums comfortable.

It is an easy, no-fuss product, and it is positioned as an addition to, not a replacement for, regular brushing, flossing and dental check-ups, which is exactly how we recommend using it.

Does Oxydental Pro work? What the research says about its ingredients

It is worth being clear about the evidence, because it shapes how we recommend using the product:

  • We searched PubMed for human studies of chlorophyllin, shilajit and fulvic acid in oral health and bad breath and did not find any, which tells us these ingredients are not yet studied for the mouth in people.[2]
  • The official page links a references list. Several entries are laboratory or animal studies and general articles on individual ingredients, rather than trials of this formula in people.[3]
  • Calcium, iodine, copper and chromium are essential nutrients, so a small daily dose from a supplement is generally a sensible, familiar addition rather than something unusual.

Oxydental Pro has not had its own clinical trial. What it offers is a pleasant daily routine and a gentle set of familiar ingredients, rather than proven dental treatment, and the official page's larger claims are best read with that in mind.

What this means for you: Oxydental Pro is best seen as a pleasant daily habit that adds a little extra mouth support, not a substitute for dental care. Keep brushing with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth and keep your check-ups, and give it a few weeks of daily use to see how your mouth feels.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works, based on the official refund policy:

  1. The 60 days start from the day your order is delivered; check your tracking number for the delivery date.
  2. Email the support team with “Refund Request” in the subject line before you ship anything back.
  3. Send back all bottles, empty or opened, with the packing slip and a note with your name and email. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Refunds are processed within a few days of the package arriving and can take 5–10 days to show on your statement.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50. The policy suggests using the product for about three months, so decide early enough that you can still post a return comfortably inside the 60 days.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. The refund page lists an order support line of 1-800-390-6035 at the time of writing, and ClickBank's self-service site can also help with order questions.

How to get the most out of Oxydental Pro

  1. Take one tablet each morning and chew it for 10–15 seconds, or swallow it with water, as the directions say.
  2. Keep it daily. A few weeks of regular use gives you a fair idea of how it suits you; the refund policy suggests about three months.
  3. Keep up the basics: brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth and see your dentist regularly.
  4. Use the bonus guides if you order six bottles; the simple fresh-breath routines are a nice extra.
  5. Keep your delivery email and packing slip so the guarantee is easy to use.

Is Oxydental Pro safe? Side effects

Oxydental Pro contains no stimulants, and the ingredients are mostly minerals and plant extracts that are widely used. As with any supplement, a small number of people notice mild stomach upset.

  • It contains iodine and copper, so if you have a thyroid condition, are pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, loose teeth or bad breath that will not go away. These need a professional check.

How Oxydental Pro compares with other options

  • Oral probiotics: products such as DentaBiome and ProDentim use beneficial bacteria, which have more published oral-health research behind them. Oxydental Pro takes a different, mineral-and-plant route and is easy to use.
  • Mouthwash: antiseptic rinses work in a different way. If you use one, take your Oxydental Pro tablet at another time of day.
  • Professional care: nothing replaces regular dental cleanings and check-ups.

Oxydental Pro FAQ

Does Oxydental Pro work?

Oxydental Pro combines familiar minerals and plant ingredients in an easy daily chewable.
