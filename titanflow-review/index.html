What is TitanFlow and how is it meant to work?

TitanFlow is made by Zenith Labs and is taken as two capsules once a day. It is formulated for men who want to support urinary comfort and prostate health as they get older.

The label is refreshingly short. Pumpkin seed oil and beta-sitosterol come from the same plant-sterol family found in pumpkin seeds, pygeum is an extract from the bark of an African tree used for centuries in urinary care, and lycopene (from tomato skin) and broccoli sprout extract add antioxidant support.

The sales page describes a mechanism around the strength of the urethra and cites Johns Hopkins research. We could not verify that mechanism from the references we reviewed, so our score rests on the published studies of the individual ingredients instead.

Does TitanFlow work?

Three of the five ingredients have published human research on urinary comfort:

  • A Cochrane review of 18 trials with 1,562 men found that a standardized pygeum extract gave a moderately large improvement in symptoms and flow measures, and men on pygeum were about twice as likely to report overall improvement. Side effects were mild and similar to placebo.[3]
  • A Cochrane review of four trials with 519 men found beta-sitosterols improved symptom scores and peak urine flow versus placebo, without changing prostate size.[2]
  • In the one-year GRANU trial of 1,431 men, pumpkin seed gave a higher response rate than placebo.[4]
  • The studies were mostly small or short, they used different preparations and doses from this product, and the reviewers of the beta-sitosterol work noted that long-term effects are not known.[2] We did not find a published trial of the finished TitanFlow formula.

What this means for you: TitanFlow is built on ingredients with encouraging published research, and it shows what it contains. Give it a few weeks of daily use alongside your usual check-ups, and use the 180-day guarantee to judge for yourself.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest windows we see in this category. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days start from the day you placed your order, not the day it arrives, so note the date.
  2. Contact Zenith Labs with your name, the email used for the order and your order confirmation number (phone (800) 928-1184, Monday to Saturday, Pacific time, or by email through the support link on the site).
  3. Send back any remaining unopened bottles with the packing slip to the address in the refund policy. The policy says opened or empty bottles do not need to be returned. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Refunds take up to 5-7 business days and cover the full purchase amount including shipping, though a payment processor may keep its non-refundable fee.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150, because the clock starts on the order date. That leaves plenty of time to contact support while you are still inside the window.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, whose order support line is 1-800-390-6035 at the time of writing.

How to get the most out of TitanFlow

  1. Take two capsules once a day, ideally with your first meal.
  2. Stay consistent. The sales page notes the studies it cites ran about 90 days, so plan to review how you feel after two to three months.
  3. Keep up the basics: a balanced diet, regular activity, enough water and routine check-ups.
  4. Cut back on late-day caffeine and alcohol if night-time bathroom trips bother you.
  5. Track how you feel in a simple note, such as night-time trips, stream strength and overall comfort.

Is TitanFlow safe? Side effects

TitanFlow's ingredients are plant extracts and food-type antioxidants that are generally well tolerated, and the pygeum review found side effects were mild and similar to placebo.[3]

  • Mild stomach upset is the most commonly reported effect with plant extracts such as these; taking the capsules with a meal helps.
  • The label advises against use for anyone under 18 and for pregnant or nursing women, and recommends checking with a physician first if you take medication or have a medical condition.
  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for prostate or blood pressure conditions, or are scheduled for surgery, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for difficulty urinating, a weak or interrupted stream, pain, or blood in your urine. These need a proper check.

TitanFlow FAQ

Does TitanFlow work?

TitanFlow is built on pumpkin seed oil, beta-sitosterol and pygeum, which have published research on urinary comfort in men. The trials used different preparations and doses, and the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so results vary from person to person. The 180-day guarantee lets you judge for yourself.

Is TitanFlow legit?

Yes. TitanFlow is a real product from Zenith Labs, sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, published contact details and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does TitanFlow cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle cost $69, 3 bottles cost $177 and 6 bottles cost $234, before shipping, which depends on your location. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.30 to $2.30 per day before shipping.

How long does TitanFlow take to work?

The sales page notes that the studies it cites ran about 90 days, so we suggest two to three months of daily use before judging. There is no published trial of the finished formula.

How do I get a refund on TitanFlow?

Within 180 days of your order date, contact Zenith Labs with your name, email and order confirmation number, and send back any remaining unopened bottles with the packing slip. Return postage is paid by the customer, and refunds take up to 5 to 7 business days.

Does TitanFlow have side effects?

The ingredients are generally well tolerated, with mild stomach upset the most common effect of plant extracts. People who are pregnant, nursing, under 18 or taking medication should ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is TitanFlow worth it?

Yes. TitanFlow is a simple, transparent and well-priced daily supplement for men who want to support urinary comfort, and it earns our score of 4.6 out of 5.
