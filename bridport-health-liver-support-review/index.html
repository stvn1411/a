What is Bridport Krill and how is it meant to work?

Bridport Health Super Strength Krill is a krill oil supplement from the health brand founded by Georgia Foster. Krill are small Antarctic crustaceans, and oil from them contains the same long-chain omega-3s as fish oil (EPA and DHA), but carried in phospholipids rather than triglycerides. Krill oil also contains some naturally occurring choline and a red antioxidant pigment called astaxanthin.

The sales page positions the product around body composition and liver-friendly nutrition, especially for people who are dieting or using weight-loss medicines and want to look after their muscle and metabolism. We treat that as the maker's positioning: what we can verify is the nutrition in the capsule, and that omega-3s are among the most studied supplements in the world.

The practical appeal is simplicity: one familiar supplement, a clear price list, a long refund window and no subscription.

Does Bridport Krill work?

Omega-3s are among the best-studied supplements, and there is good evidence behind krill oil specifically for blood fats:

  • A meta-analysis of seven randomized trials with 662 participants found that krill oil lowered triglycerides and LDL cholesterol compared with control.[1]
  • A network meta-analysis of 64 trials found krill oil and fish oil changed blood fats in a similar way, with the effect depending on the amount of omega-3 taken, so a lower-dose krill oil is a fair choice if you prefer it to fish oil.[3]
  • For fatty liver, the human evidence comes from general marine omega-3 trials, which pooled data showing less liver fat than placebo; we did not find a human liver trial on krill oil itself.[4]

The sales page also describes a 2025 double-blind study on the maker's own krill oil, reporting that people lost a larger share of fat and less muscle than on placebo. We could not find the full paper to read, so we treat those figures as manufacturer-reported and do not rely on them in our score.

What this means for you: krill oil is a research-supported way to get EPA and DHA, with the clearest benefit shown for blood fats. It works best as a supportive extra next to healthy eating, regular activity and your doctor's advice, and results vary from person to person.

How to get the most out of Bridport Krill

  1. Follow the directions on the bottle and take it at the same time each day, ideally with a meal.
  2. Take it with food that contains some fat, which helps the body absorb omega-3s.
  3. Be consistent. Studies of omega-3s usually measure changes over several weeks to months.
  4. Keep the basics in place: a balanced diet, regular movement and enough protein, especially if you are losing weight.
  5. Store it somewhere cool and dry and use the bottle before its date.

Is Bridport Krill safe? Side effects

Krill oil is generally well tolerated by healthy adults. The most common side effects of omega-3s in general are mild, such as a burp with an aftertaste, loose stools or an upset stomach.

  • Krill is a crustacean, so avoid it if you have a shellfish allergy.
  • Omega-3s can affect blood clotting at higher doses, so check with your doctor first if you take blood thinners or have surgery planned.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicines (including weight-loss medicines), check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[5]
  • Speak to a doctor about liver symptoms such as yellowing of the skin or eyes, dark urine or persistent upper-right abdominal pain; a supplement is not a substitute for a medical check.
