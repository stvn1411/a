What is NanoDefense Pro and how is it meant to work?

NanoDefense Pro is a topical solution that you apply to the nail or skin area you want to look after, for about 60 seconds a day. The manufacturer's idea is to use ingredients in nano form: particles the company describes as about 1,000 times smaller than those in regular topical products, so that they can reach deeper layers of the skin and nail.

Nanotechnology is a real and active area of skin-care research. Scientists have found that putting well-known plant compounds into nano-sized carriers can improve how well they stay stable and penetrate the skin, which is why many ingredients with poor absorption are now studied this way.[3]

The formula combines three nano-ingredients (silver, curcumin and quercetin) with luteolin, deoxyribonuclease and cape aloe.

Does NanoDefense Pro work?

It helps to be clear about what the research does and does not cover:

  • Silver in nano form has shown activity against common skin and nail fungi in the laboratory, at low concentrations.[2]
  • Curcumin is one of the most-studied plant compounds, and delivering it in nano form is a promising way to help it reach the skin.[3]
  • Most of this research is laboratory or animal work. We did not find a published trial of the finished NanoDefense Pro formula on people.

What this means for you: NanoDefense Pro is built on ingredients with genuine research interest and puts them in a quick, easy daily routine. Because the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, treat it as a skin and nail care routine rather than a medical treatment, and give it at least three months, as the manufacturer suggests. Results vary from person to person.

How to get the most out of NanoDefense Pro

  1. Apply to clean, dry skin and nails after washing, and spend about a minute on the area, as directed.
  2. Test a small area first if you have sensitive skin, and wait a day to check how your skin responds.
  3. Be consistent. Nails grow slowly, so the manufacturer suggests using the product for at least three months before deciding.
  4. Keep up good habits: keep nails trimmed, dry your feet and hands well, and wear breathable shoes and clean socks.
  5. Take a photo every few weeks in the same light so you can see changes objectively.

Is NanoDefense Pro safe? Side effects

NanoDefense Pro is a topical product with no stimulants. The most likely issue with any topical is mild irritation or an allergic reaction in someone sensitive to an ingredient, such as turmeric, aloe or silver.

  • Patch-test first, and stop using it if you notice redness, stinging, itching or swelling.
  • The manufacturer advises showing a bottle to your doctor first if you have a medical condition or take prescription medicine, and we agree.
  • Statements about dietary supplements and similar products are not evaluated by the FDA, which is true of every product in this category.[1]
  • See a doctor for a nail that is painful, swollen, very thick, changing colour quickly, or has pus or an odor, and take extra care to get advice if you have diabetes or poor circulation. These need a professional check.
