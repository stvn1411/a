What is Neuro-Thrive and how is it meant to work?

Neuro-Thrive is a vegetarian capsule taken once a day. Each bottle holds 30 capsules, so one bottle is a month. The manufacturer, Critical Bench, says it is non-GMO, Prop 65 compliant and made in a GMP-certified facility in the United States.

The formula has seven named ingredients. The headline one is PQQ (pyrroloquinoline quinone), an antioxidant compound found in foods such as natto, the fermented soybean dish at the centre of the sales page's story. Neuro-Thrive does not contain natto itself; PQQ is the link. PQQ is joined by Bacopa monnieri standardized to 50% bacosides, Alpha GPC (a source of choline), GABA, and a small vitamin blend of D3, B3 and B6.

The manufacturer says the formula is not a proprietary blend and that the ingredients are in effective amounts. The product page names every ingredient, which makes it easier to look up the research than with many competing formulas.

Does Neuro-Thrive work?

Neuro-Thrive has not had its own clinical trial, but its two lead ingredients have been studied in people:

  • In a placebo-controlled trial, 41 healthy older adults took 20 mg of PQQ a day for 12 weeks. The PQQ group showed a smaller Stroop interference change than placebo, a measure of selective attention.[3] The trial was small, and the amount used there is not confirmed to match the capsule.
  • A meta-analysis of nine randomized trials of standardized Bacopa found improved speed of attention after 12 weeks or more of use.[2]
  • Choline, which Alpha GPC provides, was linked with better verbal and visual memory scores in a large community cohort, though that was an observational study of diet.[4]

What this means for you: Neuro-Thrive pairs two ingredients with real human trials, PQQ and Bacopa, with supporting nutrients in a clearly labelled capsule. The finished formula hasn't been trialled on its own, and the research on PQQ and Bacopa measured attention more than memory, so a steady two to three months of daily use is a sensible way to judge it.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Neuro-Thrive is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works, based on the official return policy:

  1. You have 60 days to return your purchase for a refund of the purchase price. The policy says shipping and handling is not refunded.
  2. The policy asks that your return reaches Critical Bench's shipping facility within 60 days, with the packing slip from the original package.
  3. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Request the refund through ClickBank's order lookup at clkbank.com: find your order, choose Get Support, and select Refund Request. The policy says the refund is processed within one business day.

Helpful tip: the sales page says to email the support team within 60 days, while the return policy says the return must reach the facility within 60 days. Start the process early, around day 30 to 40, and confirm the return address with support before posting.

Orders are processed by ClickBank. For product questions, the contact page lists Critical Bench, Inc. at 15373 Roosevelt Blvd, Suite 203, Clearwater, FL 33760, phone 1-727-351-3065.

How to get the most out of Neuro-Thrive

  1. Take one capsule a day at the same time, for example with breakfast, so it becomes a habit.
  2. Give it time. The Bacopa trials ran for 12 weeks or more, and the PQQ trial for 12 weeks.
  3. Keep the basics in place: good sleep, regular exercise, a balanced diet and mentally engaging activities all matter for brain health.
  4. Don't double up on similar supplements without checking, as some multivitamins also contain vitamin D, B3 and B6.
  5. Note how you feel each month, particularly focus and alertness, so you can decide whether to continue.

Is Neuro-Thrive safe? Side effects

The ingredients in Neuro-Thrive are well known, and the product contains no stimulants. As with any supplement, it is worth checking with a doctor if you take medicine or have a health condition.

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure, sleep or anxiety, check with your doctor first, as the manufacturer's page also advises.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition, ask your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor about ongoing memory changes, confusion or other new symptoms; they need a proper assessment.

How Neuro-Thrive compares with other options
