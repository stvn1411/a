What is Claritox Pro and how is it meant to work?

Claritox Pro is a once-a-day capsule made with nine plant and mineral ingredients. The maker says it is manufactured in the USA in an FDA-approved, GMP-certified facility, and that its ingredients come from growers who use no herbicides.

Its formula is a little different from many balance-themed products. Rather than centering on one herb, it combines minerals (zinc, chromium), polyphenol-rich plants (green tea, resveratrol), and metabolic-support botanicals (berberine, banaba, Korean ginseng, cayenne, milk thistle). Many of these are best known for supporting steady energy and metabolic health, which is one reason people link them to feeling steady and clear-headed through the day.

It is a dietary supplement, not a medicine. The best way to think about it is as daily support alongside good hydration, regular meals, sleep and check-ups.

Does Claritox Pro work?

Claritox Pro is built from ingredients with a broad research base for general wellness, so the clearest findings are about the ingredients rather than the finished capsule:

  • Berberine has some of the strongest human data in the formula: meta-analyses of randomized trials report better cholesterol readings[2] and lower blood glucose.[3]
  • Ginseng has been studied in many trials, and a meta-analysis found a small benefit for fatigue.[4]
  • The sales page does not cite clinical studies for the balance claim, and we did not find trials on this formula for dizziness or balance, so its balance role should be seen as supportive.

What this means for you: Claritox Pro brings together recognizable wellness ingredients, two of which (berberine and ginseng) have meaningful human research. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it the 3 months the seller advises, keep up good daily habits, and see a doctor about any dizziness that persists.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the refund policy is plainly written. Here is how it works:

  1. The 60 days start from the day your order is delivered; the tracking number shows the date.
  2. Email contact@claritox.com with "Refund Request" in the subject line.
  3. Send back all the bottles, opened or empty, with the packing slip. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Your refund is processed within about 5 to 10 days of the package arriving.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50. That gives you time to see how you're getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably if you need it.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. ClickBank order support is 1-800-390-6035 (US toll free) at the time of writing. The seller advises using the product for at least 3 months before deciding, so plan your trial timeline with the 60-day window in mind.

  3. Give it time. The seller suggests at least 3 months of use before judging results.

Is Claritox Pro safe? Side effects

Claritox Pro is made from familiar ingredients and is described by the manufacturer as free of dangerous stimulants. Individual responses vary.

  • Berberine and ginseng can affect blood sugar, cholesterol or blood pressure medicine, so check with your doctor first if you take any, or if you are pregnant or nursing.
  • Green tea and cayenne can cause mild stomach upset in some people, particularly if taken on an empty stomach.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for dizziness, lightheadedness or balance problems that are new, frequent or severe, or come with fainting, chest pain, vision changes or weakness. They have many possible causes and need a professional check.
