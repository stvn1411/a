What is Reverse Your Fatty Liver and how is it meant to work?

Reverse Your Fatty Liver is a program, not a pill. You receive it online as a PDF straight after ordering, together with a recipe collection and a short cleanse plan. It is written by Jonathan Mizel, who says he developed it after his own fatty liver diagnosis, with co-author Susan Peters.

The program is built around diet. It covers which foods to favour and which to avoid, a 7-day plan that is meant to be a reset to start with, recipes for each meal type, and guidance on lifestyle habits. The sales page also mentions certain supplements and herbs; those are optional extras, and anything you take should be cleared with your doctor first.

The approach lines up with how fatty liver is managed in research: reviews of clinical trials conclude that weight loss through diet and physical activity is the mainstay of managing non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.[2]

Does diet and lifestyle help fatty liver? What the research shows

We could not find a published trial of the Reverse Your Fatty Liver program itself, so we looked at the research behind the approach it takes.

  • A systematic review of 23 studies in adults with non-alcoholic fatty liver disease found that diet and physical activity changes consistently reduced liver fat and/or liver enzyme levels, with the strongest link being weight reduction.[1]
  • The same review found improvements in blood sugar control in most studies, and a trend towards less liver inflammation in studies that measured it.[1]
  • A later systematic review of 24 trials found that every trial showed a significant reduction in liver fat and/or markers of disease activity, and that combined diet and exercise interventions appeared most effective.[2]
  • That review suggested a goal of 5 to 10% weight loss using a modest calorie reduction together with 30 to 60 minutes of exercise on 3 to 5 days a week.[2]

What this means for you: diet and lifestyle are the best-supported ways to improve fatty liver, and this program is a structured way to do exactly that. Pair it with regular movement, and keep your doctor involved, especially if you have a diagnosis or abnormal blood tests.[2]

How to get the most out of Reverse Your Fatty Liver

  1. Get a baseline. If you can, ask your doctor for liver blood tests before you start so you have something to compare with.
  2. Begin with the 7-day plan, then carry on with the recipes so the changes last.
  3. Add movement. Research on fatty liver points to combining diet with regular exercise.[2]
  4. Aim for steady weight loss if you are overweight, rather than crash dieting; the reviewed studies point to a gradual 5 to 10% loss.[2]
  5. Review progress after a couple of months with your doctor, who can repeat tests and advise you.

Is Reverse Your Fatty Liver safe?

The core of the program is food, which is generally gentle, but a liver condition is a medical matter, so involve your doctor.

  • Check with your doctor before starting if you have fatty liver, NASH, cirrhosis, diabetes, kidney problems or take prescription medicine.
  • Check with your doctor before trying any herbs, supplements or cleanse the program mentions, since some can affect the liver or interact with medicines.
  • Do not stop or change prescribed treatment because of any program.
  • See a doctor promptly for yellowing of the skin or eyes, dark urine, persistent upper-right abdominal pain or swelling.
  • The program is not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, and nothing here replaces medical care.
