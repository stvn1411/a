What is 24 BURN and how is it meant to work?

24 BURN is a liquid supplement taken as drops. The manufacturer says its formula is designed for fast absorption and to support the body's metabolism around the clock. We could not verify the absorption figures quoted on the sales page, so we treat them as the maker's own description.

The formula brings together 13 plant extracts that are widely used in weight-management products: familiar names such as green tea and African mango, plus energy-focused herbs like ginseng and guarana, and others such as forskolin, raspberry ketones and gymnema. Liquid drops suit people who find capsules awkward or who like to adjust their routine easily.

As with any supplement, it is meant to sit on top of a balanced diet and regular activity, not to replace them.

Does 24 BURN work?

There is no published trial of 24 BURN itself, but several of its key extracts have human research behind them:

  • A meta-analysis of 15 randomized trials (1,243 participants) found that green tea catechins taken with caffeine were linked to lower body weight, BMI and waist size than caffeine alone, although the authors judged the size of the change modest.[2]
  • In a 12-week, double-blind trial of 240 adults, a higher-catechin green tea led to greater reductions in body weight, body fat and waist size than a lower-catechin control.[3]
  • In a 10-week, placebo-controlled trial of 102 overweight volunteers, an Irvingia gabonensis seed extract was linked to improvements in body weight, body fat and waist size versus placebo.[4] A smaller one-month trial reported a similar direction.[5]

What this means for you: some of the building blocks in 24 BURN have encouraging research, and the formula gathers them in an easy daily drop. The finished formula and its amounts have not been studied, so the most realistic plan is to pair it with balanced meals and regular movement and see how you feel over a couple of months.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works according to the official refund policy:

  1. The 60 days start from the date of purchase.
  2. Contact support with your full name, email, ClickBank invoice and order number, and wait for confirmation before sending anything.
  3. Send back all products, including used or empty bottles, with your order number written on paper inside the package. Refunds cover only the items received.
  4. Posting the package by day 60 is enough, even if it arrives a few days later. Keep your tracking number.
  5. Your refund is issued after the package reaches the warehouse. The policy does not state a timeframe or who pays return postage.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 45. That gives you time to see how you are getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably if you need it. The refund is a one-time offer per customer.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and the official page links to ClickBank order support for any order question.

How to get the most out of 24 BURN

  1. Follow the label directions for the number of drops and timing.
  2. Take it earlier in the day if you are sensitive to caffeine, since green tea and guarana both contain it.
  3. Be consistent. The studies we reviewed ran for roughly 4 to 12 weeks.
  4. Keep up the basics: balanced meals, plenty of water, regular movement and good sleep. 24 BURN works best on top of these.
  5. Track your progress with a weekly weigh-in or waist measurement, and decide before day 60 whether to keep going.

Is 24 BURN safe? Side effects

The official page does not list side effects. Several extracts in the formula are common in food and herbal products, but green tea and guarana contain caffeine, so some people may notice jitteriness, a faster heartbeat or trouble sleeping.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine, or manage blood sugar, blood pressure or a heart condition, check with your doctor before starting. Gymnema in particular can affect blood sugar.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Stop taking it and see a doctor if you notice chest pain, a racing heart, severe stomach upset or an allergic reaction.

24 BURN FAQ

Does 24 BURN work?

24 BURN combines 13 plant extracts, several of which have been studied in human trials, including green tea and African mango, where small average weight changes have been reported. Results vary from person to person and the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a daily support alongside balanced eating and regular activity.

Is 24 BURN legit?

Yes. 24 BURN is a real product with an official website, published refund, shipping and contact pages, orders handled through ClickBank and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does 24 BURN cost?

On the official site on November 6, 2026: 1 month costs $59 plus shipping, 3 months cost $147 plus shipping and 6 months cost $234 with free US shipping. That works out to roughly $1.30 per day on the 6-month package.

How long does 24 BURN take to work?

The official page does not give a timeline. The human studies we reviewed ran from about 4 to 12 weeks, so a consistent routine over a couple of months is a sensible way to judge it within the 60-day guarantee.

How do I get a refund on 24 BURN?

Within 60 days of purchase, contact support with your name, email, ClickBank invoice and order number and wait for confirmation. Then send back all products, including used or empty ones, with your order number inside the package. Only items received are refunded.

Does 24 BURN have side effects?

The formula includes caffeine-containing extracts (green tea and guarana), so some people notice jitteriness or trouble sleeping if they take it late in the day. People who are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or managing blood sugar should check with a doctor first.

Final verdict: is 24 BURN worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a simple daily addition to a weight-management routine, 24 BURN is an easy, well-priced formula built from familiar plant extracts, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. Green tea and African mango have encouraging human research behind them, the larger packages bring the cost to about $1.30 a day, and the 60-day guarantee keeps the risk low. Pair it with balanced meals and regular movement and give it a couple of months.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-month package gives you the best price per day, free US shipping and the free cookbook.
