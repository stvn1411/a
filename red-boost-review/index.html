What is Red Boost and how is it meant to work?

Red Boost is a powder you take once a day. Its official page describes it as a male health formula and, as is common for this kind of product, it also appears under the name Hard Wood Tonic on some pages and domains. Its label claims centre on men's sexual wellness, healthy blood flow and nitric oxide, and prostate and urinary comfort.

The idea behind the formula is to combine two groups of ingredients. Icariin, Tongkat Ali and fenugreek are traditional herbs that have been studied for their role in men's hormonal and sexual wellness. Citrulline, an amino acid, and nettle root are included for circulation and prostate support respectively. The manufacturer describes the aim as supporting the body's natural processes, not replacing medical care.

Does Red Boost work?

Red Boost has not been tested as a finished formula, but the research on its individual ingredients gives it a reasonable foundation:

  • In a single-blind study of 24 men, a month of 1.5 g a day of L-citrulline was linked with better erection hardness scores than a month of placebo (50% of men improved on citrulline versus 8.3% on placebo), and no adverse events were reported.[3]
  • A 2015 meta-analysis concluded that Tongkat Ali extract may have a clinical effect on erectile function, while noting that only two trials were pooled and more are needed.[2]
  • A standardized fenugreek extract and mineral formulation improved arousal and orgasm scores over 6 weeks in healthy men.[4]

It is worth noting that the studies are small, used specific extracts and doses, and were not run on Red Boost itself. The authors of the citrulline study also noted that it was less effective than prescription options in the short term.[3]

What this means for you: Red Boost pulls together ingredients with some encouraging human research and puts them into an easy daily routine. Because the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the doses are not listed, treat it as a supportive supplement, give it a few weeks of consistent daily use and use the 180-day guarantee if it is not for you.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest windows we see on supplements. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days (six months) start from the day your order ships, not from the day it arrives. Your shipping email or order slip shows the date.
  2. Email the seller with "Refund Request" in the subject line, including your full name, order number and the email address used for the order.
  3. Get confirmation from a support agent before you send anything back, then return all the bottles to the address they give you and email them the tracking ID. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Once the return is received, you get a full refund and an email confirmation. It usually takes 5 to 10 days to appear on your statement.

Helpful tip: note the shipping date when your order leaves and put a reminder in your calendar a few weeks before day 180. That leaves plenty of time to decide and, if needed, complete the return.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and order help is available at clkbank.com. The product support email listed on the official contact page is support@hardwoodtonic.com, and the page says replies come within 48 hours or less.

How to get the most out of Red Boost

  1. Follow the directions on the label. The official page says to take one serving a day, so keep to that and do not double up.
  2. Make it a routine. Pick a time that suits you, such as with breakfast, so you do not skip days.
  3. Give it a few weeks. The studies on its ingredients ran from about 6 to 12 weeks, so judge it over a similar time frame.
  4. Support it with the basics: regular activity, good sleep, a balanced diet and regular check-ups all matter for men's health.
  5. Track how you feel, and use the 180-day guarantee if it is not right for you.

Is Red Boost safe? Side effects

The official page does not list side effects. Herbal and amino acid ingredients are generally well tolerated by healthy adults in studies, though mild stomach upset can happen with any new supplement. In the citrulline study we reviewed, no adverse events were reported.[3]

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure, heart conditions, blood sugar or prostate problems, check with your doctor before starting, since some ingredients are described as affecting blood flow and hormones.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor about ongoing changes in sexual function, urinary symptoms, chest pain or dizziness. These deserve a professional check and are not something a supplement should replace.

Red Boost FAQ

Does Red Boost work?

Red Boost combines five ingredients that have each been looked at in research, including small human studies of Tongkat Ali, fenugreek and citrulline. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet, the doses are not listed in the page text, and results vary from person to person.

Is Red Boost legit?

Yes. Red Boost is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, published support emails and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Red Boost cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $69, 3 bottles cost $177 and 6 bottles cost $234, each bottle being a 30-day supply. Shipping is listed as an extra on the smaller packages and described as free on 3- and 6-bottle orders in the site's FAQ, so confirm it at checkout. Before shipping, that is about $2.30, $1.97 and $1.30 per day.

How long does Red Boost take to work?

The official page does not give a timeline. The studies on its ingredients ran from about 4 weeks to 12 weeks, so a few weeks of daily use is a sensible trial period.

How do I get a refund on Red Boost?

Within 180 days of the date your order ships, email the seller with "Refund Request" in the subject line, get confirmation from an agent, return the bottles and email the tracking ID. Return postage is paid by the customer, and refunds usually take 5 to 10 days to appear on your statement.

Does Red Boost have side effects?

The official page does not list side effects. Herbal ingredients can cause mild stomach upset in some people, and men who take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure, heart or prostate conditions, should ask a doctor before starting.

Final verdict: is Red Boost worth it?

Yes. For men who want a simple daily supplement built on familiar ingredients, Red Boost is an easy, well-chosen and good-value option, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. Keep up the healthy basics and regular check-ups, and let Red Boost do its part on top.
