What is The Zōē Method and how is it meant to work?

The Zōē Method is a three-month online coaching program for women. After purchase you book an onboarding call, share your goals, training history, lifestyle, cycle and energy patterns, and your coach builds a starting plan for training and nutrition. Every four weeks the plan is reviewed and updated by email, and between reviews an AI assistant called Mr Z, trained on the coach's methodology, can adjust a session or a meal on the day.

The idea is simple and appealing: women are often handed advice written for men's bodies, and a plan that respects energy, recovery and appetite as they change can be easier to keep up. The program aims for results that last without constant restarting, strict rules or hours of extra training.

It is positioned as an educational coaching experience, not medical care, and the sales page says so clearly.

Does cycle-aware coaching work?

Personalised coaching for women is a sensible idea, and the research helps explain why the program's emphasis on flexibility is a strength:

  • An umbrella review of the research concluded that cycle phase has no clear, consistent influence on acute strength performance or on the gains women make from resistance training. In other words, women can train effectively through the month.[1]
  • A separate meta-analysis of maximal strength found small differences between phases (for example, isometric strength tended to peak in the late follicular phase), but not so large that everyone needs the same adjustment.[2]

What this means for you: the research does not show that one fixed schedule by cycle phase is necessary, and it also shows individual differences. That is where a plan that is reviewed every four weeks and adjusted daily has an advantage: it follows how you respond, not a calendar. The program itself has not had a published trial, so treat its promises as a guide, and judge it by how your training and energy feel over the three months.

How the 30-day satisfaction guarantee works

The program is covered by a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. Here is what the refund policy says:

  2. Send a written request to hello@byzoewomen.com with your full name, purchase email, order number if you have it, and the reason.
  3. Because the coaching is personalised, the value of services already completed and delivered (for example the onboarding call or the plan) may be deducted from a refund. Services not yet provided are not charged.

Helpful tip: note your purchase date and set a reminder for around day 20. That gives you time to judge how the first plan feels and still make a written request comfortably inside the window.

How to get the most out of The Zōē Method

  1. Complete the onboarding honestly: goals, history, cycle and energy patterns all shape the plan.
  2. Use Mr Z for real-time questions such as adjusting a session on a tired day, rather than skipping it.
  3. Log how you feel before each four-week review so the update reflects real data.
  4. Use the Aligned Woman Journal to notice energy and appetite patterns.
  5. Book the free consultation first if you are unsure the program fits.

Is The Zōē Method safe? Who should check first

Coaching programs are generally low-risk, but any new training and eating plan is a change for your body. The page states that results vary and that the program is not a substitute for medical advice.

  • If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or postpartum, take hormonal medication or have an injury, check with your doctor before starting.
  • If you have a history of disordered eating, discuss a nutrition plan with a professional first.
  • Stop and see a doctor for chest pain, dizziness, fainting or unusual pain during exercise.
