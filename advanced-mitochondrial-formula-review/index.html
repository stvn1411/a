What is Advanced Mitochondrial Formula and how is it meant to work?

Advanced Mitochondrial Formula is a daily capsule built around one idea: your cells make energy in tiny structures called mitochondria, and several nutrients take part in that process. Instead of adding a stimulant, the formula supplies those nutrients so your body has what it needs for steady, natural energy.

The product is sold by Advanced Bionutritionals and presented by physician Dr. Frank Shallenberger. The company describes three "primary" nutrients (niacinamide, D-ribose and Itadori extract) and eight supporting ones. Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 that the body uses to make NAD, a molecule involved in cellular energy. D-ribose is a simple sugar that is part of ATP, the cell's energy currency.

It is a different approach from coffee or energy drinks, which give a short lift. Here the aim is gentle, background support, which is why the company suggests giving it about three months.

Does Advanced Mitochondrial Formula work?

The research behind individual ingredients is a real strength of this formula:

  • A meta-analysis of 13 randomized trials with 1,126 participants found that CoQ10 was associated with a statistically significant reduction in fatigue compared with placebo.[2]
  • In a randomized, triple-blind pilot trial, healthy adults aged 35 to 65 who took a nicotinamide and D-ribose combination had higher NAD-related markers, and their fatigue, concentration and motivation scores improved over baseline. It was a small, one-week study, and two authors were employed by the ingredient supplier.[3]
  • NAD-boosting is an active research area. A 24-week trial of nicotinamide riboside (a related, but different, NAD precursor) in people with long COVID raised NAD levels, although fatigue was not significantly different from placebo, and the authors call for larger trials.[4]

What this means for you: several of the formula's ingredients, especially CoQ10 and the niacinamide with D-ribose pairing, have encouraging human research behind them. The finished capsule has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a well-chosen supporting formula and give it the three months the company suggests.

How to get the most out of Advanced Mitochondrial Formula

  1. Take two capsules a day, as the company directs.
  2. Give it time. The company suggests about three months, and many people notice changes sooner.
  3. Build on the basics: regular sleep, balanced meals, daily movement and water. A supplement works best on top of these.
  4. Take it consistently at the same time each day so it becomes a habit.
  5. Keep a simple note of your energy, afternoon slump and mood each week to see whether it suits you.

Is Advanced Mitochondrial Formula safe? Side effects

The nutrients in Advanced Mitochondrial Formula are common ones that most healthy adults tolerate well. As with any supplement, a few precautions apply:

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor first. Curcumin, quercetin, CoQ10 and black pepper extract can interact with some medicines, including blood thinners and diabetes medicine.
  • Mild stomach upset is the most common complaint with supplements like this; taking capsules with food can help.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Ongoing tiredness can have many causes. See a doctor if you feel exhausted all the time, are short of breath, or have unexplained weight changes.

How Advanced Mitochondrial Formula compares with other options

  • Single-ingredient CoQ10: simple and well studied for fatigue,[2] but it supplies just one of the formula's nutrients. Advanced Mitochondrial Formula is the broader option.
  • Caffeine and energy drinks: a quick lift without supporting the cells' own energy chain. Many people use both, but the formula adds no caffeine of its own.
  • Lifestyle first: sleep, activity and a balanced diet are the most reliable energy tools, and a supplement is a supporting extra.
