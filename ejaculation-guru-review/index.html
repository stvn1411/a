Does the approach behind Ejaculation Guru work?

  • A systematic review of 10 randomized trials with 521 participants looked at behavioral therapies for premature ejaculation, including stop-start, squeeze, sensate focus and pelvic floor rehabilitation. In two trials against a waiting list, men gained 7 to 9 minutes in time to ejaculation.[1]
  • In a randomized study of 40 men with lifelong premature ejaculation, 57% of men in the pelvic floor rehabilitation group regained control of ejaculation after 12 weeks.[2]
  • The reviewers also noted that the overall quality of the trials was mixed, so practice and patience matter.[1]

What this means for you: non-drug, practice-based methods are a research-supported place to start, and Ejaculation Guru follows that general approach. We cannot confirm which techniques the program teaches, so treat the research as context rather than a promise, and use the 60-day guarantee to judge it for yourself.

Is Ejaculation Guru safe? What to know first

Behavioral practice is low-risk for most healthy adults. A few points are worth knowing first:

  • Erection or ejaculation changes can have medical causes, including heart and blood-vessel conditions, diabetes, hormone issues, medicines and stress. A doctor's check is a sensible first step, and it is quick and private.
  • If you take prescription medicine, including ED or blood-pressure medicine, do not stop or change it because of a guide. Talk to your doctor first.
  • Quick ejaculation that is new, or comes with pain or other symptoms, deserves a doctor's check; the cause is not always behavioral.
  • See a doctor promptly for chest pain, shortness of breath, an erection that lasts more than four hours, pain, or a sudden change in sexual function.
