Privacy policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit health.truepicksreview.com, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated October 29, 2026

Summary

  • You can read the whole site without creating an account or giving us your name.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit each page, roughly where they're from and what device they use.
  • If you use our contact form, we receive the details you type in so we can reply.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

This policy covers health.truepicksreview.com, operated by TruePicks Review ("we", "us"). You can reach us through our contact page.

Information we collect

Information collected automatically (analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google, to measure how the site is used. When you visit a page, Google Analytics collects information such as:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay;
  • your approximate location (country and city level), derived from your IP address;
  • your device type, browser, operating system and screen size;
  • how you arrived at the site (for example, a search engine or another website);
  • clicks on links to official product websites.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to tell visits apart. We use these reports only in aggregate — to see which reviews are useful and which countries and devices our readers use — not to identify you.

Information you give us (contact form)

If you send us a message, we receive your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to us by our form provider, Formspree. We use this information only to reply to you and keep a record of the conversation.

Cookies

  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics (for example, cookies named _ga) to measure visits.
  • Seller and affiliate cookies — when you click through to a product's official website, that website or ClickBank may set its own cookies, including cookies used to track affiliate referrals. Those cookies are controlled by the seller or ClickBank and covered by their privacy policies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also stop Google Analytics from collecting your visits with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How we use information

  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To reply to messages and correct errors you report.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

How information is shared

We share information only with the service providers that help run the site: Google (analytics), Formspree (contact form) and our website host. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Links to other websites

Our reviews link to manufacturers' official websites and other sites. When you leave TruePicks Review, the other site's privacy policy applies. If you buy a product, your order and payment details are handled by the seller or ClickBank, not by us.

How long we keep information

Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Contact form messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and then deleted.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live — for example the European Union, the United Kingdom or California — you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact us and we will respond within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
