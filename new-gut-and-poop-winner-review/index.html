What is SynoGut and how is it meant to work?

SynoGut is a capsule supplement designed around dietary fiber. Most of its ingredients are fibers or fiber-rich plants: psyllium husk, oat bran, flaxseed, prune, apple pectin and glucomannan. Alongside them sit bentonite clay, aloe vera leaf, black walnut hull and a probiotic, L. acidophilus.

The idea is straightforward. Fiber adds bulk and helps hold water in stools, which can make them softer and easier to pass. Many people in the US eat less fiber than they would like, so a daily fiber capsule is a practical way to close the gap. Fiber supplements have been tested in clinical trials for constipation, with encouraging results for stool frequency and consistency (more on that below).

The official site describes a small-batch formula made in the USA, and the capsules are non-GMO. The label asks you to take two capsules a day with a meal.

Do fiber supplements like SynoGut work?

The research on fiber for regularity is genuinely encouraging:

  • A meta-analysis of 7 randomized trials in adults with chronic constipation found that fiber increased stool frequency and softened stools compared with placebo, and 77% of people on fiber responded versus 44% on placebo. The authors called fiber moderately effective, and noted the trials were small and the evidence quality low.[6]
  • The same review found more flatulence with fiber than placebo, which is why it helps to start gradually and drink plenty of water.[6]
  • A 2022 review of 30 trials found probiotics as a group modestly increased stool frequency in adults, though results depended on the strain.[5]

What this means for you: fiber is a research-backed way to support regular bowel movements, and SynoGut combines several fibers with a probiotic in a simple daily capsule. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial yet, and the blend amount is smaller than the doses used in many studies, so give it a few weeks of daily use with plenty of water and a fiber-rich diet to see how you get on.

How to get the most out of SynoGut

  1. Follow the label: take two capsules daily with a meal and don't exceed the recommended dose.
  2. Drink plenty of water through the day. Fiber works by holding water, so it helps to stay well hydrated.
  3. Be consistent. The company suggests at least 3 months, and fiber trials typically measured changes after several weeks of daily use.
  4. Keep up the basics: plenty of vegetables, fruit and whole grains, regular movement and not ignoring the urge to go. SynoGut works best on top of these.
  5. Mind your medicines: fiber can affect how some medicines are absorbed, so ask your doctor or pharmacist about timing if you take any prescription medicine.

Is SynoGut safe? Side effects

Fiber supplements are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. The most common effect is mild gas or bloating, especially when you first start, and it usually settles as your body adjusts.

  • The label advises that products like this are not intended for pregnant or nursing mothers or children under 18, and that people taking prescription medicine should speak to a doctor first.
  • SynoGut contains tree nuts (black walnut) and is made in a facility that also processes milk, soy, wheat, egg, peanuts, tree nuts, fish and crustacean shellfish, per the label.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.
  • See a doctor for blood in stools, ongoing pain, unexplained weight loss or a lasting change in bowel habits; these need a professional check.
