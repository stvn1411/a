What is FlowForce Max and how is it meant to work?

FlowForce Max is a soft, peppermint-flavoured chew taken once a day. Rather than a large capsule, it puts a blend of plant extracts and minerals into a format that is simple to stick with.

Its centrepiece is Graminex flower pollen extract, a purified pollen ingredient that has been tested in clinical studies. It is joined by saw palmetto, grape seed extract, ViNitrox, Muira puama, silk protein and a group of plant compounds, namely fisetin, luteolin, monolaurin, oregano leaf and perilla leaf.

The manufacturer explains the formula through the idea of keeping the urinary tract comfortable by limiting yeast growth, and cites laboratory studies on several of these plant compounds. Those findings come from lab work rather than from trials of this chew, so we have focused our review on the published research on the key ingredients themselves.

Does FlowForce Max work? What the research says

The research behind the key ingredients gives a balanced but encouraging picture:

  • A comprehensive review of flower pollen extract included four randomized trials with 384 patients and found a significant improvement in quality of life, with no significant adverse events.[2]
  • Those trials were in men with chronic prostatitis / chronic pelvic pain syndrome, so they do not map exactly onto everyday urinary comfort.[2]
  • For saw palmetto on its own, a large Cochrane review of 32 trials with 5,666 men did not find a clear advantage over placebo for urine flow or prostate size, which is why the combination of ingredients matters.[3]

What this means for you: FlowForce Max pairs a pollen extract with a promising research record and other well-known plant ingredients in a convenient chew. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it at least two to three months, as the maker suggests, and judge by how you feel.

  1. Chew one soft chew a day, at the same time each day, so it becomes part of your routine.
