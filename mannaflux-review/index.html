What is MannaFlux and how is it meant to work?

MannaFlux is a plant-based gel capsule designed to support metabolism and healthy weight management. It is built on five plants: Egyptian blue vervain, eleuthero root, lady's mantle, licorice root and peppermint oil. You take one capsule in the morning and one before bed.

The formula draws on traditional herbal ideas. Eleuthero, sometimes called Siberian ginseng, has long been used as an adaptogen for stress and energy. Licorice and peppermint are two of the best-known herbs in modern research, and the soft gel format keeps the peppermint oil fresh and easy to swallow without an upset stomach.

The page also describes the formula as working with a gold ORMUS component, but that is not itemised alongside the five ingredients, so this review focuses on the five plants listed. As with any supplement, it works best alongside balanced meals, movement and sleep.

Do ingredients like MannaFlux's work?

MannaFlux itself has not been through a published clinical trial that we could find, but two of its ingredients have encouraging research behind them:

  • In a randomised, double-blind trial of 84 moderately overweight adults, a licorice flavonoid oil reduced total body fat and, at the highest dose, visceral fat after 8 weeks.[2]
  • A later, smaller 8-week trial did not find a significant difference between licorice flavonoid oil and placebo, so the evidence is mixed.[3]
  • A meta-analysis of 12 trials found peppermint oil improved overall symptoms in people with irritable bowel syndrome, with similar side effects to placebo.[4]

What this means for you: MannaFlux uses plants that people have relied on for generations, and two of them have real clinical research behind them. Because the amounts are not listed, treat it as a daily supporting habit rather than a stand-alone solution. The long 180-day guarantee gives you plenty of time to judge it for yourself.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest we have seen. The page says it will refund you for any reason within 180 days, or six months, of your purchase. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days start from your purchase date.
  2. Email the support team at support@mannaflux.com and say you would like a refund.
  3. Follow the return instructions they give you. The return address in the policy is 285 Northeast Ave, Tallmadge, Ohio 44278.
  4. The policy does not say who pays return postage, so check with support before you send anything.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150. That gives you time to judge the capsules over several months and still use the guarantee comfortably if you need it.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which also handles order support. One paragraph of the page mentions a refund of up to a year, but the FAQ states 180 days, so we use 180 days as the safe figure.

How to get the most out of MannaFlux

  1. Take one capsule in the morning and one before bed, as the page directs, with or without food.
  2. Be consistent. Studies on plant extracts typically measured results after 8 weeks or more.
  3. Pair it with the basics: balanced meals, enough protein and fibre, and regular walking.
  4. Track more than the scale: waist size, energy and appetite are all useful signs.
  5. Tell your doctor you are taking it, especially if you take medication or manage blood pressure.

Is MannaFlux safe? Side effects

MannaFlux is made of plants and contains no stimulants. Most healthy adults tolerate ingredients like these well, with mild stomach upset the most common complaint for supplements in general. The page says to consult your doctor first if you have a medical condition.

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure or diabetes, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Licorice-containing supplements can affect blood pressure and potassium in some people, so check first if you have high blood pressure or a heart or kidney condition.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, or planning surgery, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for dizziness, palpitations, severe stomach pain or any symptom that worries you.

MannaFlux FAQ

Does MannaFlux work?

MannaFlux is built on five plant ingredients traditionally used for energy and metabolism, and research on two of them, licorice flavonoids and peppermint oil, is encouraging in different areas. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the amount of each ingredient is not listed, so results vary from person to person.

Is MannaFlux legit?

Yes. MannaFlux is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with published contact details, a US shipping address and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does MannaFlux cost?

On the official page at the time of our review: 1 bottle cost $69, 3 bottles cost $177 and 6 bottles cost $294, with free shipping on the 3- and 6-bottle packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.63 to $2.30 per day.

How long does MannaFlux take to work?

The manufacturer says some people notice a difference within a few days to a week and that it works best the longer you take it. Studies on the individual ingredients generally ran for 8 weeks or more, so we suggest giving it a few months.

How do I get a refund on MannaFlux?

The page promises a refund for any reason within 180 days of purchase. Contact support at support@mannaflux.com first, and follow the return instructions they give you, using the return address in the shipping and return policy.

Does MannaFlux have side effects?

The manufacturer describes it as plant-based and non-habit-forming, with no stimulants. People who are pregnant, nursing, on medication or managing blood pressure should ask a doctor first, partly because licorice can affect blood pressure in some people.

Final verdict: is MannaFlux worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a simple plant-based daily addition to healthy eating, MannaFlux is an easy, low-risk choice, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It combines five traditional plants in a gel capsule with no stimulants, two of which, licorice and peppermint oil, have real clinical research behind them. Keep up balanced meals and regular movement, ask your doctor if you take medication, and let MannaFlux do its part on top. With a 180-day money-back guarantee, there is very little to lose by giving it a try.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-bottle package gives you the best price per day and free shipping.
