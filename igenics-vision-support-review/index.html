What is iGenics Vision Support and how is it meant to work?

iGenics Vision Support, sold by the company ScienceGenics under the product name iGenics, is a capsule that focuses on two themes: giving the eyes the nutrients used in the well-known AREDS2 research, and supporting a healthy inflammatory response with plant extracts.

According to the official page, ginkgo biloba is the main ingredient, joined by AREDS2-type ingredients, bilberry, saffron, and turmeric combined with BioPerine, a black pepper extract commonly used to help the body absorb turmeric. The page describes the formula as vegan, made with natural ingredients and free from fillers.

It is a straightforward product: one formula, a handful of packages, and no subscription to manage.

Does iGenics Vision Support work?

No study has tested iGenics Vision Support itself, so we looked at the research on the nutrients it names:

  • AREDS2 nutrients: in the large AREDS2 trial of people aged 50 to 85 at risk of advanced macular degeneration, adding lutein and zeaxanthin to the original formula did not change overall results, but those with low dietary intake of the two carotenoids appeared to benefit.[2]
  • Saffron: small randomized trials found short-term improvements in retinal function in people with age-related macular degeneration.[3][4]
  • Anthocyanins and eye comfort: a 6-week multi-ingredient trial in adults with screen-related eye fatigue included anthocyanin, astaxanthin and lutein.[5]

What this means for you: the best-researched parts of this formula are the AREDS2-type nutrients and saffron, and both have encouraging if modest evidence. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so treat it as nutritional support rather than a treatment, and keep up regular eye exams.
