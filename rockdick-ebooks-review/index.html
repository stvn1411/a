What is Rockdick Ebooks and how is it meant to work?

Rockdick Ebooks is a download bundle rather than a supplement. It combines traditional Chinese qigong and conditioning books, a jelqing video guide, a book titled Exercising the Penis and a tai chi video set.

The sales page describes the material as natural, no surgery and no pills, with step-by-step video tutorials. Some of the books are described as helping blood flow and kidney and pelvic strength.

Because it is all digital, you simply open the emailed links and start at your own pace, with no equipment needed for most of the movements.

What does research say about exercise and the methods in this bundle?

The research splits into a positive part and a part to approach with realistic expectations:

  • Exercise helps erection health: a meta-analysis of 7 randomized trials (478 men with erection difficulties) found aerobic, pelvic-floor or combined exercise improved erectile-function scores by about 3.85 points on average.[1]
  • Pelvic-floor exercises in particular were tested in a trial of 55 men; 40% reached normal function by 6 months with supervised exercises and biofeedback.[2]
  • On size, a review of non-surgical lengthening found that devices such as extenders are the only method with some evidence, and that most other non-invasive methods, including exercises, are not supported by scientific evidence.[3]
  • A systematic review of 17 studies of interventions for men with normal-sized penises described the evidence as lacking and the study quality as poor.[4]

What this means for you: the exercise and movement content has research behind it, and at $3 it is easy to try. We would not count on size changes: published research does not support that claim, so judge the bundle by how good the movement routines make you feel.

How to get the most out of Rockdick Ebooks

  1. Start with the gentle material such as the tai chi and qigong sets, and move on at your own pace.
  2. Warm up and go easy with any manual technique; stop if you notice pain, bruising or numbness.
  3. Add general activity. Walking, cycling or swimming showed the clearest benefit in pooled research.[1]
  4. Be consistent. Exercise trials ran for weeks or months, not days.[1]
  5. Use the guarantee date. Check your progress at week 8, before day 60.

Is Rockdick Ebooks safe? Side effects

Qigong and tai chi are gentle, and most healthy adults can try them safely. Manual penis techniques need more care, because firm squeezing or pulling can irritate tissue.

  • Stop and see a doctor if you notice pain, bruising, swelling, numbness, a lump or a bend that wasn't there before.
  • Men with Peyronie's disease, a penile injury or a bleeding disorder should ask a doctor before trying any manual technique.
  • If erection difficulties are new, sudden or worsening, see a doctor; they can be linked to heart and blood-vessel health.
  • The page itself advises consulting a physician before starting any exercise program.

Rockdick Ebooks FAQ

Does Rockdick Ebooks work?

The bundle teaches exercise routines, and exercise has shown benefits for erectile function in published trials. Published research does not confirm penis-size gains from jelqing or similar exercises, and results vary from person to person.

Is Rockdick Ebooks legit?

Yes. It is a real digital product with a named support address, a one-time price and a 60-day money-back guarantee on its sales page.

How much does it cost?

On the official site at the time of our review it cost $3.00 as a one-time payment, with links delivered by email and no shipping.

How long does it take to work?

The sales page refers to 8 weeks for visible results. Exercise trials generally measure results over weeks to months, so a steady routine over two months is a fair test.

How do I get a refund?

The page promises a refund within 60 days if you do not see visible results in 8 weeks, and says you keep the material. Contact the support address listed on the page, webmaster@rockdick.com.

Does it have side effects?

Qigong and tai chi are gentle. Manual techniques can cause irritation or bruising if done too firmly, so go gently and stop if you have pain. Check with a doctor first if you have a penile condition or injury.

Final verdict: is Rockdick Ebooks worth it?

Yes. For a man who wants an inexpensive, low-risk way to explore exercise and movement, Rockdick Ebooks is an easy and very good-value bundle, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5.
