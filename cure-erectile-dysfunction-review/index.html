What is Cure Erectile Dysfunction and how is it meant to work?

The program is a step-by-step PDF guide. Rather than a pill or device, it teaches a set of exercises, mainly for the muscles around the pelvis, plus breathing and relaxation routines for the stress side of the picture.

The author explains erections in simple terms: they depend on blood flowing into the penis, and he argues that tight or weak pelvic muscles and stress can get in the way. The guide splits the exercises into physical and emotional groups and walks you through choosing the ones that match your situation.

Because the exercises are quiet and need no equipment, you can practise most of them anywhere, which many readers will appreciate. You get the PDF straight away after ordering, with nothing arriving in the post.

Do pelvic-floor and general exercise help erection health?

There is real research behind the idea of exercising for erection health, even though this particular guide has not been studied on its own:

  • In a randomized trial of 55 men with erection difficulties, men taught pelvic-floor exercises (with biofeedback and lifestyle advice) improved their erectile-function scores significantly more than men given lifestyle advice alone at 3 months. By 6 months, 22 of the 55 men (40%) had normal function and another 19 (34.5%) had improved.[1]
  • A systematic review and meta-analysis of 7 randomized trials (478 men) found that aerobic, pelvic-floor or combined exercise improved erectile-function scores by about 3.85 points on average, with moderate-to-vigorous aerobic exercise showing the clearest benefit.[2]
  • Both papers note limits: the trials were small and could not blind participants, and they tested supervised programs rather than a downloadable guide.

What this means for you: exercise is a research-supported, low-risk place to start for many men, and this guide packages pelvic-floor, breathing and relaxation work into an easy routine. It has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a supportive habit and keep your doctor in the loop, especially because erection changes can be an early signal of other health issues.

How to get the most out of Cure Erectile Dysfunction

  1. Start with a quick check-up if erection changes are new, so any underlying cause is not missed.
  2. Begin with the pelvic-floor exercises and add the other sets gradually, as the guide suggests.
  3. Be consistent. The pelvic-floor trial measured results at 3 and 6 months.[1]
  4. Add general activity. Walking, cycling or other aerobic exercise showed the clearest benefit in pooled research.[2]
  5. Keep notes on how you feel after a few weeks so you can discuss progress with your doctor.

Is Cure Erectile Dysfunction safe? Side effects

The program uses gentle body-weight exercise, breathing and relaxation, so the risk is low for most healthy adults. As with any exercise plan, start gently.

  • Stop and check with a doctor if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.
  • If erection difficulties are new, sudden or worsening, or you also have chest pain, shortness of breath or very high blood sugar, see a doctor promptly: erections and heart and blood-vessel health are linked.
  • Men who have had prostate surgery, or have pelvic pain, should ask their doctor before starting pelvic exercises.
  • The guide is general information, not individual medical care, and the sales page says to review it with your own health professional.
