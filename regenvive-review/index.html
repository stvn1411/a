What is RegenVive and how is it meant to work?

RegenVive is an herbal capsule blend from RegenVive Research. Its approach is breadth: instead of leaning on one hero ingredient, it combines 13 plant and mineral ingredients that each have a traditional or researched role in healthy glucose balance.

The six the sales page highlights are banaba (glucose metabolism), guggul gum resin (insulin sensitivity), bitter melon (blood sugar support), gymnema (blood sugar and heart health), juniper berries (glucose regulation) and white mulberry (slowing sugar absorption). The page also positions the formula for steady energy and mental clarity.

As with any supplement, it is meant to sit alongside the basics: a balanced diet, regular activity and your usual medical care.

Does RegenVive work?

We did not find a published clinical trial of the finished RegenVive formula, so the case for it rests on its ingredients. Several have encouraging human research:

  • A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 37 adults found that mulberry leaf extract reduced the glucose rise after a carbohydrate drink in a dose-related way, by 14% at the standard dose and 22% at double the dose, with no difference in digestive symptoms between groups.[2]
  • A pooled analysis of 8 randomized trials (423 people with type 2 diabetes) found that bitter melon was linked with lower fasting glucose, post-meal glucose and HbA1c.[3]
  • A review of 10 studies of gymnema found improvements in fasting glucose, post-meal glucose and HbA1c compared with starting values, again with large differences between studies.[4]

What this means for you: RegenVive combines several ingredients with human research behind them, including mulberry leaf, bitter melon and gymnema, in one capsule. That makes it a reasonable blend to try for a few months alongside healthy eating and activity. The finished formula hasn't had its own published trial, and results vary from person to person.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works:

  1. The 90 days start from your purchase date.
  2. Email support@regenvive.com to request a return authorization (RMA number).
  3. Send the product back, with the RMA number and your proof of purchase, to the returns address (Aurora, CO). Return postage is paid by you, and the policy asks you to ship it within 14 days of receiving the RMA.
  4. Once the return is received and inspected you will get an email confirming the refund, which goes back to your original payment method.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 75. That gives you time to see how you're getting on and still request a return authorization comfortably.

The sales page says refunds are made within 48 hours of the product being returned and that empty bottles can be sent back, while the return policy page mentions partial refunds for items showing obvious use. It is worth confirming the details with support before you ship anything. Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which can help with any order question.

Is RegenVive safe? Side effects

Herbal blends like RegenVive are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. The main point is to be thoughtful if you take medicine, since several of the herbs can influence blood sugar and some can interact with other prescriptions.

  • If you take medicine for blood sugar, blood thinners or other prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a health condition, check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for symptoms such as excessive thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss or blurred vision, and never stop prescribed treatment because of a supplement.
