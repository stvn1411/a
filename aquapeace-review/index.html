What is AquaPeace and how is it meant to work?

AquaPeace is a once-a-day capsule that combines seaweeds, microalgae and an antioxidant from the sea. The idea behind it is nutritional: the ear's delicate hair cells depend on good circulation and a steady supply of nutrients, so the formula brings together marine ingredients that are rich in antioxidants and healthy fats.

The manufacturer links the formula to research on how sea creatures keep their own hair cells healthy, and says the ingredients come from East Asia. We could not verify that connection, so we focused on what is known about each ingredient and about nutrition and hearing in general.

Think of AquaPeace as a nutritional support for the ears, to take alongside the basics: protecting your ears from loud noise and having your hearing checked regularly.

Does AquaPeace work? What research says about nutrition and hearing

No one has published a trial of this exact formula, so we looked at the research on its nutrients and on diet and hearing:

  • A 2025 meta-analysis of 33 studies found that eating more antioxidants and fish rich in unsaturated fatty acids may help preserve hearing, though it noted most of the evidence is observational.[5]
  • In the Blue Mountains Hearing Study, higher long-chain omega-3 intake was linked to lower odds of developing hearing loss.[6]
  • A UK Biobank analysis of more than 175,000 adults linked higher blood levels of omega-3 fats to lower odds of self-reported hearing difficulty.[7]
  • A comprehensive review concluded that omega-3 and hearing is a promising area, while noting that human intervention research is still limited.[4]

What this means for you: Good nutrition, including omega oils and antioxidants, is consistently linked to healthier hearing, and AquaPeace brings several of those nutrients together in one capsule. The formula itself hasn't had a clinical trial, so think of it as nutritional support rather than a treatment, and give it a few months alongside your usual care. Results vary from person to person.

How to get the most out of AquaPeace

  1. Take one capsule a day, in the morning or before bed, with or without food.
  2. Be consistent. The company suggests at least 3 months, as nutrition-based support builds up slowly.
  3. Protect your ears: avoid loud noise and use ear protection at concerts and with power tools.
  4. Keep up your hearing checks. A hearing test gives a baseline to compare against.
  5. Eat well: fish, leafy greens and a balanced diet all contribute to healthy hearing.

Is AquaPeace safe? Side effects

Seaweeds and microalgae are everyday foods in many countries, and the manufacturer states the capsule has no stimulants. Mild digestive upset is the most common complaint with supplements in general.

  • Seaweeds can contain iodine, so people with a thyroid condition should ask a doctor before starting.
  • If you take blood thinners or other prescription medicine, or are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor first.
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden hearing loss, ringing in one ear only, dizziness or ear pain; these need a professional check, not a supplement.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]

How AquaPeace compares with other options

  • Omega-3 from food: oily fish a couple of times a week is the best-studied dietary link to hearing health.
  • Single-ingredient supplements: fish oil or astaxanthin on their own cost less but cover fewer nutrients; AquaPeace's advantage is combining several in one capsule.
  • Professional care: regular hearing tests and an audiologist are the right step for any change in hearing.
