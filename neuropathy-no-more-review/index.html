What is Neuropathy No More and how is it meant to work?

Neuropathy No More is a digital program rather than a supplement. After you order, it is delivered to your email within minutes. You can read it on a phone, tablet or computer, or print it.

The book opens by explaining how nerves work and why they can start to misfire, then lays out a five-part daily framework: an eating approach, nine nerve-support nutrients with suggested amounts, four anti-inflammation compounds, gentle movements (many can be done from a chair) and a 20-minute mind-body practice.

A one-page Neuropathy-Beating Blueprint summarizes the whole plan, and the appendices add 16 recipes and a shopping guide. The author, Jodi Knapp, is described by the publisher as a natural health practitioner who previously ran her own clinic.

Does Neuropathy No More work? What the research shows

The book as a whole has not been tested in a clinical trial, but its main lifestyle building blocks have real research behind them:

  • A one-year study of 100 people with type 2 diabetes found that supervised weekly exercise increased skin nerve-fiber density in the lower leg compared with counseling alone, suggesting early nerve injury may be reversible.[2]
  • A research review from the same group describes aerobic exercise as acting on several nerve-damage pathways at once, and suggests that combining diet and exercise may offer sustained benefit.[3]
  • The publisher says the book cites more than 50 studies and gives a reason and amount for each recommendation, which readers can look up for themselves.
  • Individual results vary, and the publisher itself says the plan works gradually rather than overnight.

What this means for you: the lifestyle approach at the heart of this guide (steady eating, regular gentle movement and stress care) has genuinely encouraging research behind it, and the book turns it into a plan you can follow. It is best used alongside your doctor's care, not instead of it.

How to get the most out of Neuropathy No More

  1. Start with the Blueprint. Print the one-page summary and keep it somewhere you see daily.
  2. Begin with one piece, such as the daily movements, and build up. The book says you do not need to adopt everything on day one.
  3. Check with your doctor before adding nutrients or changing any medicine, especially for diabetes or nerve pain.
  4. Track your progress weekly: sleep, comfort in your feet or hands, and how far you walk.
  5. Give it time. The publisher says some readers notice changes within weeks, others over a few months.

Is Neuropathy No More safe? Side effects and cautions

Neuropathy No More is a guide to food, movement and habits, so the main safety points are about changing your routine sensibly.

  • Check with your doctor before starting any new supplement, exercise routine or diet, especially if you have diabetes, kidney disease or take prescription medicine.
  • Do not stop or change nerve-pain or diabetes medicine without medical guidance. The sales page says the book is not a replacement for medical care.
  • Dietary supplements named in any guide are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for numbness, weakness or pain that is new, spreading or getting worse, and for foot sores that do not heal.
