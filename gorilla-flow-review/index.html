What is Gorilla Flow and how is it meant to work?

Gorilla Flow is a prostate supplement built around a simple idea from its maker: support a healthy prostate by supporting normal inflammation and healthy hormone balance. The sales page explains that, as men age, a shift in the balance between testosterone and estrogen may play a part in prostate comfort, and the formula is designed with that in mind.

That is the maker's reasoning, and the research on this topic is still developing. What we can say is that the individual ingredients are well known and studied for urinary comfort in men.

"Gorilla cherry" is the maker's friendly name for Prunus africana, also known as pygeum, an African tree whose bark has been used for urinary comfort for generations. It is the lead ingredient.

Does Gorilla Flow work? What the research says

Several of the ingredients in Gorilla Flow have decent research behind them for urinary comfort in men:

  • A meta-analysis of pygeum (Prunus africana) found nocturia reduced by about 19% and peak urine flow up by about 23% compared with placebo, with side effects similar to placebo.[4]
  • A review of 18 randomized trials of saw palmetto in 2,939 men found fewer night-time trips and better flow than placebo, with mild and infrequent side effects.[2]
  • A Cochrane review of beta-sitosterols (the plant sterols found in pumpkin seed) found about 4.9 points better symptom scores than placebo in two studies; prostate size did not change.[5]
  • The idea that estrogen balance affects the prostate is the maker's explanation. It is a reasonable area of research but not yet proven by trials of this finished formula.

What this means for you: Gorilla Flow combines several botanicals with real research behind them for urinary comfort in men. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of steady use and judge for yourself within the 90-day guarantee.
