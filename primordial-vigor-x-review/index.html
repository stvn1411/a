What is Primordial Vigor X and how is it meant to work?

Primordial Vigor X is a capsule supplement designed for men who want to support sexual vitality, stamina and confidence. Rather than a long list of ingredients, the bottle names three botanicals: Tribulus terrestris, Tongkat Ali and saw palmetto berries.

Each has a long history in traditional use and has since been studied in modern trials. Tribulus and Tongkat Ali have both been tested for male sexual function and hormone levels, and saw palmetto is widely used for prostate comfort. The manufacturer describes the formula as having a dual action: supporting the body's natural cleansing systems and supporting hormonal balance, with a focus on testosterone and serotonin.

The formula is made in the USA, with foreign and domestic ingredients, in an FDA-registered facility, according to the manufacturer.

Does Primordial Vigor X work?

The research on the botanicals in Primordial Vigor X is more encouraging than many people expect:

  • A meta-analysis pooling eight randomized trials found that Tribulus terrestris improved erectile function scores compared with placebo, with a similar rate of side effects.[2]
  • A meta-analysis of randomized trials of Tongkat Ali found a meaningful improvement in erectile function in men with lower baseline scores, and a review of clinical trials supported higher total testosterone.[3][4]
  • Saw palmetto's record is more mixed: a Cochrane review found little to no benefit alone for urinary symptoms, but it remains a popular supporting ingredient.[6]
  • Most of these trials used specific extracts and were small, so the results show promise for the ingredients rather than for this exact formula.

What this means for you: Primordial Vigor X puts three well-known botanicals into one daily routine. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of daily use, alongside sleep, exercise and a healthy diet, before judging.

Is Primordial Vigor X safe? Side effects

Botanical supplements like these are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. In the Tribulus meta-analysis, side effects were no more common than with placebo.[2] As with any supplement, a little caution is sensible.

  • If you take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure, hormones or the prostate, check with your doctor before starting.
  • If you have a prostate, hormone-related or heart condition, or are having prostate tests, tell your doctor you take it.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for ongoing erection problems, chest pain or urinary symptoms; these deserve a proper check.
