What is Prosta Defend and how is it meant to work?

Prosta Defend is a plant-based formula aimed at men who want to look after their prostate as they get older. The prostate is a small gland that sits just below the bladder. As it grows with age it can press on the urethra, which is one reason many men over 40 notice more bathroom trips, a weaker stream or interrupted sleep.

The maker's approach is to combine long-used botanicals that are traditionally taken to support the prostate and urinary tract. The sales page describes the formula as gentle, non-habit forming and made from plant ingredients and natural minerals, and says it is assembled in the United States.

Prosta Defend is a dietary supplement, not a medicine. It is meant to support everyday urinary comfort and prostate health, not to diagnose or treat any condition.

Does Prosta Defend work? What the research says

The botanicals in Prosta Defend are some of the most studied in men's urinary health, and the research is encouraging:

  • A systematic review of 18 randomized trials in 2,939 men found saw palmetto extracts reduced night-time urination and improved peak flow compared with placebo, with mild and infrequent side effects.[2]
  • A meta-analysis of 15 randomized trials and 12 observational studies (5,800 men) on one saw palmetto extract found fewer night-time trips and better flow than placebo, with comparable results to tamsulosin.[3]
  • A meta-analysis of pygeum africanum found nocturia reduced by about 19% and peak urine flow up by about 23%, with side effects similar to placebo.[4]
  • Studies were usually short, and results came from standardized extracts, so results with any finished supplement can differ.

What this means for you: the main ingredients in Prosta Defend are botanicals with real research behind them for urinary comfort in men. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the amounts per ingredient are not listed on the sales page, so give it a few weeks of steady use and judge for yourself within the long 90-day guarantee.

Is Prosta Defend safe? Side effects

Plant-based prostate supplements are generally well tolerated. In the saw palmetto research, the most frequent side effects were mild stomach upsets.[3]

  • If you take prescription medicine, are scheduled for surgery or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Urinary changes can have several causes. See a doctor for pain, blood in the urine, a very weak stream or being unable to pass urine.
  • A supplement is not a substitute for prostate screening or advice from your doctor.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
