What is Steel Bite Pro and how is it meant to work?

Steel Bite Pro is a capsule you take once a day with water. Instead of a single active ingredient, it combines a broad range of plants and minerals, led by berberine, milk thistle, artichoke and zinc, with chanca piedra, yarrow, beetroot, dandelion, alfalfa, jujube seeds, chicory root, celery seed, burdock root and yellow dock.

The idea is whole-body, plant-based support alongside ordinary oral care. Plants such as berberine, milk thistle and dandelion are some of the most familiar herbal ingredients in supplements, so the formula will feel recognisable to many readers. The company says it contains 23 plants, herbs, minerals and vitamins in total, though it names only 14 on the page.

Because it's a capsule rather than a chewable or a rinse, Steel Bite Pro supports your routine from the inside rather than acting directly on the teeth, so it works best on top of brushing, flossing and dental visits.

Does Steel Bite Pro work?

Here's what the research says about the best-known ingredients in the formula:

  • A systematic review of 16 randomized trials with 2,147 participants found that berberine lowered total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol and triglycerides, with no significant difference in adverse events. The authors noted that trial quality varied, so results should be read with care. These trials weren't about teeth or gums.[2]
  • A review of clinical trials on milk thistle concluded that its extracts are well tolerated, with minimal adverse effects.[3]
  • In a six-month study, a toothpaste containing zinc citrate and triclosan kept plaque lower and gums healthier than a control. Steel Bite Pro contains zinc in a capsule, so the effect may differ.[4]

What this means for you: Steel Bite Pro is built from well-known ingredients, some with a solid safety record in human trials. We didn't find research on the finished formula, or on most of its ingredients, for teeth and gums specifically, and the amounts aren't listed. Treat it as a supportive daily habit alongside your usual routine, and use the guarantee if it isn't for you.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and it still applies if you've used the entire bottle. To use it:

  1. The 60 days start from the day your order is delivered, so check your carrier's tracking for that date.
  2. Email support with “Refund Request” in the subject line and tell them you're not fully convinced by the product. The contact page is at steelbitepro.com/help/contact-us.php.
  3. Send back all the bottles, opened or empty, with the packing slip and a note with your name and email. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Your refund is processed within a few days of the package arriving, and it can take 5 to 10 days to appear on your statement.

Helpful tip: the company suggests using Steel Bite Pro for at least 3 months, but the refund window is 60 days from delivery, so put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50 to decide in time.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. ClickBank order support is listed as 1-800-390-6035 at the time of writing.

How to get the most out of Steel Bite Pro

  1. Take one capsule a day with water, at a time you won't forget, such as with breakfast.
  2. Be consistent. The company suggests giving it at least 3 months.
  3. Keep up the basics: brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, cleaning between your teeth, limiting sugary drinks and having regular check-ups.
  4. Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you take prescription medicine or other supplements.
  5. Track your progress. Make a note of how your gums feel when you brush and how fresh your breath is, and decide before day 60 whether to keep going.

Is Steel Bite Pro safe? Side effects

The main ingredients in Steel Bite Pro are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and the company says it has no stimulants. The most common effect of herbal supplements in general is mild digestive upset.

  • Berberine can interact with some prescription medicines. If you take any, or you are pregnant or nursing or have a medical condition (including a liver condition), check with your doctor before starting. The official page gives the same advice.
  • Herbal blends can add up if you already take other supplements, so tell your doctor or pharmacist what you take.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a dentist for pain, bleeding or receding gums, or loose teeth: these need a professional check.

Steel Bite Pro FAQ

Does Steel Bite Pro work?

Steel Bite Pro combines berberine, milk thistle, artichoke, zinc and other plants, and its main ingredients have been studied in people for general safety and wellness. We did not find published research on the finished formula for teeth and gums, and results vary from person to person, so it is best used alongside brushing, flossing and dental check-ups.

Is Steel Bite Pro legit?

Yes. Steel Bite Pro is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a one-time payment, a published contact page and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Steel Bite Pro cost?

On the official page at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $69, 3 bottles cost $177 and 6 bottles cost $294, with free US shipping on every package. Each bottle is a 30-day supply, so the cost is roughly $1.63 to $2.30 per day.

How long does Steel Bite Pro take to work?

How do I get a refund on Steel Bite Pro?
