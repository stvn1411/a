What is The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution and how is it meant to work?

The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution is a downloadable guide written by Shelly Manning, a natural health researcher who founded Blue Heron Health News. The official page says she built it over nearly two decades of working with people who have chronic kidney disease.

It is organised into three phases. The first covers a day-by-day food framework, gut-focused eating and anti-inflammatory shifts. The second covers food choices and natural compounds. The third covers what the author calls cellular renewal, including a section named the Golden Nugget Protocol.

The official page is clear that the guide complements conventional treatment: readers are told to keep working with their nephrologist and to take prescribed medication, with any changes made through their doctor.

Does The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution work?

We looked for evidence on the kind of lifestyle approach the guide is built around:

  • A Cochrane review of 45 studies with 1,863 participants found that regular exercise improves physical fitness, walking capacity, heart rate and blood pressure in adults with chronic kidney disease.[1]
  • A review of the latest guidelines for primary care describes treatment as centred on lifestyle changes, blood pressure and blood sugar control, and preventive treatment for heart and kidney problems, which fits the structured, habit-based approach of this guide.[2]
  • The 2024 KDIGO guideline for evaluating and managing chronic kidney disease sets out current clinical advice, which is the right yardstick to compare any lifestyle plan against with your doctor.[3]

What this means for you: a structured lifestyle plan has solid general support, and this guide packages one in an easy format. The claims about large improvements in kidney function come from the guide's own case histories, not from independent trials, so treat it as a companion to your nephrologist, share it with them, and let your lab results guide how you use it.

How to get the most out of The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution

  1. Share it with your nephrologist or dietitian before you start, so any food or supplement advice fits your own lab results and restrictions.
  2. Begin with Phase 1 and give it the full 12 weeks, since the guide builds in order.
  3. Use the lab tracking sheets after every blood test and bring them to appointments.
  4. Keep taking prescribed medicine exactly as directed; the guide says medication changes go through your doctor.
  5. Stay active in a way your doctor approves, since the exercise research is among the strongest in this area.

Is The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution safe? Side effects

Reading a guide has no side effects of its own, but kidney health is an area where the details matter. Diets that are good for most people, such as high-potassium fruit and vegetables or high-protein eating, can be unsuitable at some stages.

The official page recommends staying under your nephrologist's care and says materials can support you at stage 5 or on dialysis, but you will still need medical intervention.

  • Ask your nephrologist or renal dietitian before changing protein, potassium, phosphorus, sodium or fluid intake.
  • Check with your doctor or pharmacist before taking any supplement, because some can strain the kidneys or interact with medicine.
  • Seek urgent medical care for sudden swelling, very little urine, chest pain, confusion or shortness of breath.
