What is Revitag and how is it meant to work?

Revitag is a light serum that you apply straight to the area of skin you want to look after. There are no capsules to swallow and no tools to use: you put a few drops on, massage them in and let the skin absorb them.

The manufacturer explains it in terms of collagen. Skin tags are small, soft growths, and one published study found that they contain a distinctive pattern of collagen fibers and mast cells.[3] Revitag's makers say their blend is designed to support those fibers and leave skin looking smoother and clearer. That is the manufacturer's explanation rather than something our research can confirm, but it gives a clear idea of what the formula is aiming for.

What we can say is that the ingredient list is made up of familiar skin-care botanicals with a long history of use, which is a reassuring starting point for a daily topical product.

Does Revitag work?

It helps to be clear about what the research does and does not cover:

  • Colloidal oatmeal is one of the best-known soothing ingredients in skin care, and a review of the clinical literature supports its use for dry, itchy and irritated skin.[2]
  • Plant oils are widely studied for supporting the skin barrier and calming irritation, which suits a daily-use serum.[4]
  • Research on skin tags themselves shows they are common, benign growths with distinctive collagen and mast cell patterns.[3] That is the territory the manufacturer's explanation builds on.

What this means for you: Revitag combines well-regarded skin-conditioning ingredients in a format that is easy to use every day. The finished serum has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a gentle skin-care routine and give it a few weeks of consistent use. Results vary from person to person.

How to get the most out of Revitag

  1. Apply to clean, dry skin after washing, then massage the drops in until absorbed.
  2. Test a small area first if you have sensitive skin or react to plant oils, and wait a day to check how your skin responds.
  3. Be consistent. The manufacturer suggests using it for at least three months, so build it into a daily routine such as after your evening shower.
  4. Keep the area comfortable: avoid rubbing or pulling at skin tags, and keep clothing and jewelry from irritating them.
  5. Take a photo every couple of weeks in the same light so you can see changes objectively.

Is Revitag safe? Side effects

Revitag is a topical, plant-based serum and contains no stimulants. As with any skin product, the most likely issue is mild irritation or an allergic reaction in someone sensitive to one of the plant ingredients.

  • Patch-test first, and stop using it if you notice redness, stinging or itching.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting a new product, as the manufacturer also advises.
  • Statements about dietary supplements and similar products are not evaluated by the FDA, which is true of every product in this category.[1]
  • See a doctor or dermatologist about any growth that bleeds, grows quickly, changes colour or shape, or hurts. These need a professional look, and a dermatologist can also remove skin tags quickly if you prefer.
