What is Over 40 Keto Solution and how is it meant to work?

Over 40 Keto Solution is a digital handbook created by Shaun and Karen of Get Lean After 40. It takes the standard keto diet, which is very low in carbohydrates and high in fat, and adds planned higher-carb days. The creators call this keto carb-cycling.

Their reasoning is that metabolism and hormones change in our 40s, 50s and 60s, and that strict keto eating every day can be hard to sustain. By timing carbohydrate foods on certain days, the plan aims to keep you in a fat-burning routine while making eating more varied and easier to stick with.

The program page describes a short 7-day handbook with three simple rules, which makes it approachable for beginners who don't want to read a whole book before starting.

The sales page also promises fast results, such as 7 pounds in 7 days. Those figures are the creators' own account and not typical of diets in general, so we judge the program on its approach rather than on that promise.

Do keto and low-carb diets work?

The research on keto and low-carb eating is encouraging, with some caveats:

  • A meta-analysis of randomized trials with at least 12 months of follow-up found that very-low-carbohydrate ketogenic diets led to greater weight loss than low-fat diets over the long term.[2]
  • An earlier meta-analysis found about 3.3 kg more weight loss at 6 months on low-carbohydrate diets than on low-fat diets, along with improvements in triglycerides and HDL cholesterol.[3]
  • Differences between diets are often small once calories are matched, so the best diet is usually the one you can follow consistently.[3]

What this means for you: keto and low-carb eating is a well-supported way to lose weight, and a clear, beginner-friendly plan can make it easier to start. The handbook's carb-cycling twist hasn't had its own trial, so give it a few weeks, track how you feel and keep your expectations realistic.

How to get the most out of Over 40 Keto Solution

  1. Read the whole handbook first and plan your first week of meals.
  2. Drink plenty of water and get enough salt, potassium and magnesium, which can ease the tiredness some people feel when starting keto.
  3. Choose whole foods: vegetables, eggs, fish, meat, nuts and healthy oils.
  4. Add movement. Walking and some strength work help keep muscle as you lose weight.
  5. Track your progress for a few weeks, including energy and sleep, not just the scale.

Is Over 40 Keto Solution safe? Side effects

A keto diet is used by many healthy adults, but it is a big change in eating. The most common complaints are digestive, with constipation the most frequently reported, and temporary tiredness or headache in the first days.[4]

  • If you have diabetes, kidney, liver, gallbladder or pancreas problems, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a history of disordered eating, keto is not a good idea without medical advice.
  • Keto can raise LDL cholesterol in some people, so ask your doctor about a blood test if you have heart risk factors.[3]
  • Dietary advice and supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]

How Over 40 Keto Solution compares with other options

  • Working with a dietitian: the best option if you have medical conditions. A handbook like this works best for healthy adults who want a simple starting plan.
