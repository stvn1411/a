What is All Day Slimming Tea and how is it meant to work?

All Day Slimming Tea is a loose herbal blend built around two classic teas, green and oolong, with ginger, lemongrass, orange peel, dandelion leaf and a few extras for flavour and sweetness. The seller positions it as a daily drink that supports metabolism, digestion and appetite control as part of a healthy lifestyle.

This review covers the free-trial offer at alldayslimmingteafree.com. It is a separate order page from other All Day Slimming Tea listings that may carry similar names, so check the web address before you order.

The idea is simple and easy to follow: replace a sugary drink with a flavourful, low-calorie tea, and enjoy the small effects that tea compounds such as catechins and caffeine can have on how the body uses energy. The seller's own page makes bigger claims than the research supports, and we have kept to what is published below.

Does All Day Slimming Tea work?

The honest picture is encouraging but modest. Research on the main ingredients points to small effects, which is why we see this tea as a helpful companion to good habits, not a shortcut:

  • A meta-analysis of green tea studies found that catechins, often combined with caffeine, produced a small positive effect on weight loss and weight maintenance.[2]
  • In a controlled crossover study, drinking oolong tea for three days raised daily energy expenditure and fat oxidation compared with water, in 12 men.[4]
  • Ginger trials pooled in a meta-analysis showed lower body weight and waist-to-hip ratio in overweight and obese adults.[5]
  • Not every review is as positive: a Cochrane review of longer trials found little weight change for green tea outside Japan, and the Garcinia review found a small effect of uncertain clinical value.[3][6]

What this means for you: tea ingredients like these may give a small boost, and a tasty zero-sugar drink makes healthy habits easier to keep. The finished blend has not had its own clinical trial, so treat the seller's bigger claims, such as results in record time or specific percentage energy gains, as marketing and enjoy the tea for what it is. Our weight-management guidance is simple: pair it with balanced meals and regular movement.

How to get the most out of All Day Slimming Tea

  1. Follow the directions that come with your bag. The sales page only says to drink it daily, so check the packaging for brewing instructions.
  2. Swap it for a sugary drink. Replacing soda or sweetened coffee is where a zero-sugar tea can help most.
  3. Be consistent for a few weeks. Trials on tea ingredients typically lasted 12 weeks or more.
  4. Keep your caffeine total in mind. Green and oolong tea contain caffeine, so avoid drinking it close to bedtime if you are sensitive.
  5. Pair it with the basics: balanced meals, regular walking or exercise, enough sleep and water. The tea works best on top of these.

Is All Day Slimming Tea safe? Side effects

Green tea, ginger and lemongrass are common foods and drinks, and most healthy adults tolerate moderate amounts well. The blend also contains caffeine, ginseng and Garcinia cambogia, so a few points are worth knowing.

  • Caffeine can cause jitteriness, a faster heartbeat or trouble sleeping in sensitive people.
  • Garcinia cambogia supplements have been linked with stomach upset in trials, and the dose in this tea is not listed.[6]
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, have a liver or blood sugar condition, or take prescription medicine (including blood thinners or diabetes medicine), check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Stop and see a doctor if you notice unusual symptoms such as severe stomach pain, yellowing skin or eyes, or a racing heart.
