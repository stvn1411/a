What is Neura and how is it meant to work?

Neura is a brain-support supplement that keeps its formula short. Instead of a long list of botanicals, it uses four ingredients with a traditional or research link to memory, focus and energy: lion's mane, goji berry, Bacopa monnieri and shilajit.

The official page says it is designed to work through several routes: supporting neurotransmitter balance, antioxidant protection and signalling between brain cells. Those are the manufacturer's descriptions; below we look at what published research supports for each ingredient.

Does Neura work?

Memory supplements are a young field, so we looked for human studies on the ingredients that have them:

  • A meta-analysis of 9 placebo-controlled trials of standardized Bacopa monnieri extract (518 people, 12 weeks or longer) found improved results on cognitive tests such as Trail B and choice reaction time.[2]
  • In a double-blind trial of 30 adults with mild cognitive impairment, lion's mane scored higher than placebo on a cognitive scale by weeks 8, 12 and 16, then dropped back after it was stopped, which suggests it works while you keep taking it.[3]
  • For goji berry and shilajit we did not find human trials on memory, so they are best seen as traditional, nutrient-rich companions to the two lead ingredients.

What this means for you: the two lead ingredients have genuine human research behind them, and Neura puts them in one short formula. The finished product has not had its own clinical trial, and amounts per ingredient are not listed, so think of it as a supportive habit alongside sleep, exercise and a healthy diet, and give it a few weeks.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. Contact support before you send anything back, by email or by phone on +1 251-501-2766 (Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern).
  2. Return unopened, undamaged bottles with a note giving your full name and email address, and your order ID if you have it. Keep your proof of purchase and original packaging.
  3. The returns page counts the 90 days from the order date in one place and the delivery date in another, so ask support to confirm, and do not leave it to the last day.
  4. Return postage is paid by you. Refunds are processed within a few days of the bottles arriving, and your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 60, and keep the bottles sealed until you are sure you want to keep the product.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which also offers order support through the link on the official site.

How to get the most out of Neura

  1. Follow the label directions and take it at the same time each day.
  2. Be consistent. Studies of Bacopa measured results after 12 weeks or more of daily use.
  3. Support your brain in other ways: regular sleep, exercise, a balanced diet and staying mentally active all matter.
  4. Keep a simple note of focus and recall over the first weeks so you can judge it fairly.
  5. Check with your doctor first if you take medicines or have a health condition.

Is Neura safe? Side effects

The formula is made of plant and mushroom extracts and a mineral-rich resin. Mild digestive upset is the most commonly reported type of effect with herbal supplements in general.

  • If you take prescription medicine, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Ongoing memory problems deserve a proper medical check-up. A supplement is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment.

How Neura compares with other options

  • Single-ingredient supplements: Bacopa or lion's mane products let you choose one studied ingredient and dose. Neura's advantage is pairing the two in one daily step.
  • Longer formulas: some brain supplements list many more botanicals. Neura's short list makes it easier to understand what you are taking.
  • Lifestyle first: sleep, exercise and diet have the strongest evidence for long-term brain health, and any supplement works best on top of them.

Neura FAQ

Does Neura work?

Neura is built around Bacopa monnieri and lion's mane, two ingredients with human research for cognition. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so results vary from person to person, and most people give it a few weeks of daily use.

Is Neura legit?

Yes. Neura is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with published contact details and a 90-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Neura cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: the Starter package is $59 plus $4.95 shipping, Gold Choice (3 bottles) is $147 and Memory Master (6 bottles) is $234, which is $39 a bottle. The sales page does not state how long a bottle lasts.

How long does Neura take to work?

The manufacturer does not give a timeline. Studies of Bacopa, one of its ingredients, measured changes after 12 weeks or more of daily use.

How do I get a refund on Neura?

Contact support first, then return unopened, undamaged bottles with a note giving your name and email. Return postage is paid by the customer, and refunds are processed within a few days of the bottles arriving.

Does Neura have side effects?

Plant and mushroom extracts are usually well tolerated, with mild digestive upset the most common complaint. People who take prescription medicine, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition should ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is Neura worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a short, easy-to-understand formula to support memory and focus, Neura is a sensible, well-priced choice, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It is built around two ingredients with human research, the larger packages are good value, and the 90-day money-back guarantee leaves little to lose. Pair it with good sleep, exercise and a healthy diet, and give it a few weeks.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-bottle package gives you the best price per bottle and enough time to see how you feel.
