What is Audifort and how is it meant to work?

Audifort is a liquid supplement that you take with a dropper once a day. You can place it under your tongue or stir it into a glass of water or juice, so it takes seconds and is easy to remember with your morning routine.

Our hearing depends on tiny, hard-working cells in the inner ear that need a steady supply of blood, oxygen and nutrients. Research has linked healthy circulation and heart health with better hearing in later life,[5] and scientists are also studying how antioxidants may help protect the delicate structures of the ear.

Audifort is built on those ideas. It combines more than 20 ingredients chosen to support healthy circulation, antioxidant protection, energy and calm focus. The lead ingredients include Panax ginseng, which has been studied for ringing in the ears, green tea and grape seed, two of the best-known plant antioxidants, and GABA, a calming amino acid. It also contains maca, guarana and several amino acids for everyday energy.

Do hearing support ingredients like those in Audifort work?

Hearing supplements are a young field of research, but several findings support the approach behind Audifort:

  • In a pilot trial in 61 people with chronic tinnitus, those taking 3,000 mg a day of Korean red ginseng for 4 weeks reported less day-to-day bother from their tinnitus and better mental wellbeing scores.[2]
  • In a 3-month placebo-controlled trial in 70 people with tinnitus, an antioxidant supplement reduced tinnitus loudness and handicap scores, while the placebo group saw no change.[3]
  • Among 265 adults over 55, regular tea drinkers had better central sound processing than non-drinkers.[4] And in older adults, heart and circulation problems were linked with worse hearing, which is why circulation support makes sense.[5]

What this means for you: Audifort is built around a sensible idea, supporting circulation, antioxidant protection and calm, clear energy, in an easy daily drop. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial yet, the doses aren't listed, and antioxidant supplements haven't been shown to restore hearing that's already been lost,[6] so think of Audifort as everyday support, give it two to three months, and keep up regular hearing checks.

How to get the most out of Audifort

  1. Shake the bottle well and take one full dropper each morning, under your tongue or in a glass of water or juice. Taking it early suits the natural caffeine.
  2. Be consistent. The manufacturer says the best results come after about four months of daily use.
  3. Protect your ears from loud noise with earplugs at concerts, when using power tools or mowing, and keep headphone volume moderate.
  4. Look after your heart and circulation with regular walks, a balanced diet and good sleep, all of which matter for hearing too.
  5. Book a hearing test if you haven't had one recently, so you have a baseline and can see how you're getting on.

Is Audifort safe? Side effects

Audifort's plant ingredients are widely used in foods and supplements and are generally well tolerated. A few points are worth knowing:

  • It contains guarana and green tea, which supply caffeine. If you're sensitive to caffeine, take it in the morning and go easy on coffee.
  • Ingredients such as gymnema, chromium and ginseng can affect blood sugar, so if you have diabetes or take blood-sugar, blood-pressure or blood-thinning medicine, check with your doctor first. The label also advises pregnant and nursing women and under-18s to check with a doctor.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden hearing loss, hearing loss in one ear, ear pain or discharge, or dizziness. These need a professional check, and sudden hearing loss is treated as urgent.

How Audifort compares with other options

  • Single-ingredient supplements: some people take ginkgo or ginseng capsules on their own. Audifort's advantage is that it combines circulation, antioxidant and energy ingredients in one easy daily drop.
  • Capsule hearing formulas: if you'd rather not taste your supplement, a capsule may suit you better. If you dislike swallowing pills, Audifort's liquid is the easier choice.
  • Hearing aids and audiologist care: for hearing loss, a hearing test and, where needed, hearing aids are the proven route. Audifort is everyday support that sits alongside them, not a replacement.
