What is TC24 and how is it meant to work?

TC24, short for Total Control 24, is a once-a-day capsule made by Adem Naturals in the USA. It is built for men who want to look after their prostate and urinary comfort as they get older, and it combines six plant-based ingredients rather than a long list of vitamins.

The formula leans on Pygeum africanum, an extract from the bark of the African cherry tree that has been studied for decades in men with an enlarged prostate. Around it sit pine pollen, boron, jaboticaba (a Brazilian fruit rich in antioxidants), a plant-sterol ingredient called Lipophytol and neem leaf powder. The manufacturer's own explanation is about supporting a healthy inflammatory response in the prostate and promoting better blood flow.

The sales page describes a two-step mechanism involving cholesterol. We could not find independent research on that specific claim, so we have focused our review on what is published about the ingredients themselves.

Does TC24 work? What the research says

The best-studied ingredient in TC24 is Pygeum, and the findings are encouraging:

  • A Cochrane review pooled 18 randomized controlled trials with 1,562 men. Compared with placebo, men using Pygeum were more than twice as likely to report an improvement in overall symptoms.[2]
  • In the same review, night-time urination fell by about 19% and peak urine flow rose by about 23%, and side effects were mild and similar to placebo.[2]
  • The trials were short (about two months on average) and used standardized Pygeum preparations, so results from a different extract or dose may differ.[2]

What this means for you: a plant-based, once-a-day formula built around Pygeum has a reasonable research basis for urinary comfort. TC24 itself has not had a clinical trial, so give it a couple of months and judge by how you feel, ideally alongside a normal check-up.

How to get the most out of TC24

  1. Check the label for the dose. The sales page mentions both one and two capsules a day, so follow the directions printed on your bottle.
  2. Take it in the morning with water or your usual drink, at the same time each day.
  3. Be consistent. The Pygeum trials ran for about two months on average, so give TC24 at least that long before judging it.
  4. Support it with habits such as staying hydrated, limiting caffeine and alcohol in the evening and staying active.
  5. Keep a simple note of night-time trips and daytime urgency so you can see whether things are changing.

Is TC24 safe? Side effects

Pygeum was well tolerated in the research we read, with side effects that were mild and similar to placebo.[2] TC24 contains no stimulants, and the manufacturer says it has not had reports of side effects. As with any supplement, individual responses vary.

  • If you take prescription medicine, have a medical condition, or are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for pain, blood in the urine, difficulty passing urine or a sudden change in symptoms. These need a proper check rather than a supplement.

How TC24 compares with other options

  • Other prostate supplements: Steel Flow Pro and FlowForce Max, both reviewed here, are built around flower pollen extract and saw palmetto. TC24 takes a different route, centring on Pygeum and pine pollen, so it can suit men who want that specific approach.
  • Doctor-led care: a check-up can rule out other causes of urinary changes, and prescribed treatments exist for men who need them. A supplement is best seen as an everyday extra.
  • Lifestyle: regular activity, a healthy weight and managing evening fluids are simple steps that go well with any prostate formula.

TC24 FAQ

TC24 is built around Pygeum africanum, which has shown improvements in urinary symptoms in short clinical trials, along with pine pollen and other plant ingredients. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and results vary from person to person.
