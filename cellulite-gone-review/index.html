What is Cellulite Gone and how is it meant to work?

Cellulite Gone is the name we use for the program sold at BeyondCellulite.com as the NAKED-BEAUTY / SYMULAST Method. It was created by Joey Atlas and is presented as a short video presentation in three steps.

The seller's idea is that cellulite is linked to changes in the muscle and connective tissue beneath the skin, and that slow, gentle movements can stimulate the connective tissue, fascia, ligaments, tendons and muscles so the outer skin layers look firmer and smoother. The video also says any extra body fat can be helped by the same moves.

That fits the way researchers describe cellulite: not one single cause, but a mix of skin structure, circulation and the fat beneath the skin. A program that gives you a clear, repeatable routine is a sensible way to put that into practice.

Does a program like Cellulite Gone work?

Research on cellulite is still limited, but it points in a helpful direction:

  • A peer-reviewed review describes cellulite as very common in post-adolescent women, not limited to people who are overweight, and shaped by skin structure, circulation and the fat beneath the skin.[1]
  • The same review reports that weight loss has been linked to improved cellulite severity on surface measurements, and that massage-type stimulation may influence skin and fat-cell activity.[1]
  • We did not find a published clinical trial of the NAKED-BEAUTY / SYMULAST Method itself, so results should be seen as individual and varying from person to person.

What this means for you: cellulite is common and research supports taking a whole-body approach. Cellulite Gone gives you a clear, gentle routine to follow in the way that suits you. Give it a few weeks of regular practice and judge the change for yourself, knowing the guarantee is there.

Is Cellulite Gone safe?

Cellulite Gone is a movement program, not a supplement, so there are no ingredients to worry about. The usual rule for any exercise routine applies: go at your own pace.

  • If you have joint, back or circulation problems, are pregnant or recently postpartum, check with your doctor before starting any new routine.
  • Stop and seek advice if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual swelling.
  • See a doctor about any skin change that is new, painful or looks different from usual cellulite.
