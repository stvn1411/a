What is the NRL EDGE Building Better Performance webinar?

NRL EDGE: Building Better Performance is a live online webinar from New Regular Lifestyle. It is built on a simple idea: better performance depends on more than finishing the workout. It also depends on how you prepare for training and how well you recover afterwards.

Two speakers share the session, Cristi Bartlett on sleep and Sean Hayes on warm-ups, so you leave with practical strategies for two parts of your routine that are easy to overlook. The session runs live on Zoom, includes a Q&A with both speakers and gives you access to a post-webinar networking session called The Let Out.

This is a digital educational webinar, not a supplement or a device, so there is nothing to ship and no product to take.

Will the webinar help your performance?

The two topics are well chosen, because both are backed by real research:

  • A systematic review of randomised controlled trials leaned towards a benefit of warming up for injury prevention; only three of its five trials found a significant reduction, so the authors called for more trials.[1]
  • A meta-analysis of trials in children and adolescents found that structured warm-up intervention programs cut sports injury rates.[2]
  • In a sleep extension study of collegiate basketball players, aiming for at least 10 hours in bed was associated with faster timed sprints and better reaction time and less daytime sleepiness. It was a small study, but it shows sleep is a real performance lever.[3]

What this means for you: a single webinar can't change your training by itself, but it can give you a clear, practical plan for two things that matter. We have not attended the session, so we can't judge the delivery, but the topics and the live Q&A are a strong fit for athletes and coaches who want practical, simple advice.

How to get the most out of the webinar

  1. Join live if you can, so you can use the Q&A.
  2. Prepare a question or two about your own warm-up or sleep routine.
  3. Take notes and pick one or two changes to start with, rather than changing everything at once.
  4. Try the changes for a few weeks. Sleep and warm-up habits show their effect over time.
  5. Stay for The Let Out networking session to meet other attendees.

Is it safe? Who should be careful

A webinar has no side effects, but the advice on warm-ups and exercise should still match your own body and health.

  • If you have an injury, a medical condition or a long-standing sleep problem, check with your doctor or physiotherapist before changing your training or sleep routine.
  • Introduce new warm-up exercises gradually and stop if something hurts.
  • See a professional for persistent pain, or sleep problems that last weeks.
