What is The Acid Reflux Strategy and how is it meant to work?

The Acid Reflux Strategy is a digital program from Blue Heron Health News. It is delivered as a PDF e-book and is built around two ideas: a handful of everyday diet adjustments, and a simple home remedy made from three ingredients that most kitchens already stock.

The sales pages describe the remedy as quick to prepare, about four to five minutes, and the program as 100% natural and free of drugs and equipment. The three ingredients are named inside the guide after purchase, so we have not listed them here.

The wider idea, that what and when you eat affects reflux, is well supported. Reviews of lifestyle research find that avoiding late evening meals, losing weight if you are carrying extra, and raising the head of the bed can all reduce acid exposure.[1]

Does a diet-first approach to acid reflux work?

We did not find a clinical trial of this particular program, so we looked at the research on the ideas behind it:

  • A systematic review of lifestyle changes for reflux found that weight loss reduced time with acid in the esophagus in two randomized trials, and that avoiding late evening meals reduced acid exposure while lying down.[1]
  • The same review found that raising the head of the bed reduced time with acid exposure when lying down, from 21% to 15% in trials.[1]
  • A later systematic review of five controlled trials (228 patients) found symptom improvement in all four trials that measured it, and called head-of-bed elevation a cheap, safe option, while noting the studies were small and had limitations.[2]

What this means for you: the research supports the general direction of The Acid Reflux Strategy: small, practical changes to how and when you eat can help reflux. The program's remedy has not been trialled on its own, so treat it as a gentle thing to try alongside, not instead of, advice from your doctor.

How to get the most out of The Acid Reflux Strategy

  1. Read the whole guide first, then pick the one or two changes that feel easiest to start with.
  2. Keep meals earlier in the evening. Research found late evening meals increased acid exposure while lying down.[1]
  3. Try a raised head of bed if night-time heartburn is your main issue; wedge pillows or bed blocks were used in the trials.[2]
  4. Keep a short food and symptom diary for a couple of weeks so you can see which changes help you.
  5. Review your progress before day 60 and decide whether to keep the guide.

Is The Acid Reflux Strategy safe? What to know

The program is a diet and home-remedy guide, so it is not a medicine, and the vendor describes it as 100% natural. Heartburn can occasionally signal something that needs medical attention, so use sensible judgement.

  • See a doctor promptly for trouble swallowing, vomiting, black stools, unexplained weight loss or chest pain.
  • If you take medicine for reflux, ask your doctor before changing it, even if you feel better.
  • Check any new home remedy with your doctor if you are pregnant, nursing or managing another condition.
  • Persistent heartburn that returns more than twice a week deserves a check-up whatever you try first.
