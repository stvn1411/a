What is Return to Prime and how is it meant to work?

Return to Prime is a digital strength program that starts from a simple question: why do some people lose size and strength as they age even while still training hard? Author Nick Nilsson's answer is that regular training mainly works the muscles themselves, while the systems that support them, such as blood supply, connective tissue and nerve signals, can be neglected.

The program is divided into four phases, each with its own manual, sets, reps, exercises and training methods: Phase 1 angiogenesis training (aimed at blood supply), Phase 2 connective tissue training, Phase 3 hyperplasia training (aimed at muscle fibre number) and Phase 4 nervous system activation training. A mobile-friendly video library shows every method and workout.

It is delivered as an instant download. The sales page says it is not for beginners and that it suits experienced lifters, including those in their 50s, 60s and beyond. Women can use it too, with different weights and exercise selection.

Does the approach behind Return to Prime work?

Return to Prime is built on resistance training, which has some of the strongest research behind it in exercise science:

  • A meta-analysis of 22 randomized trials found that resistance training significantly improved muscle strength and whole-muscle size in people aged 75 and over, including those aged 80 and over, with few reports of adverse events.[1]
  • A systematic review of 42 randomized trials found that regular static stretching produced small but significant gains in strength and muscle size, with longer durations and higher frequencies working best, which supports the idea of adding stretch-based work to training.[2]
  • The program's more specific claims, such as increasing the number of muscle fibres, rest mostly on animal research, so we see them as interesting rather than proven in people.

What this means for you: the foundation of Return to Prime, progressive resistance training, is among the best-supported approaches for keeping muscle and strength. The four-phase structure is the author's own design and has not been tested as a package, so treat it as a fresh, structured plan and judge it by how you respond over a few months.

How to get the most out of Return to Prime

  1. Start with the main manual so you understand how the four phases fit together.
  2. Follow the phases in order and use the videos to check your technique.
  3. Warm up well and use sensible weights. The methods can feel different from normal training.
  4. Track your workouts so you can see progress over weeks.
  5. Prioritise sleep and protein, which matter for muscle regardless of the program.

Is Return to Prime safe? Things to know

Resistance training is generally safe for healthy adults, and the research in older adults reports few adverse events.[1] Still, this is a demanding program.

  • Check with your doctor first if you have joint pain, heart or blood pressure concerns, or a recent injury, or take regular medicine.
  • The author says it is not for beginners, so build a base of regular weight training first.
  • If you plan to use any supplements from the guide, check with your doctor, especially if you take prescription medicine.
  • Stop and seek advice for chest pain, dizziness, or sharp joint pain during training.

Does Return to Prime work?

Return to Prime is a structured, four-phase strength program built on resistance training, which research shows builds strength and muscle even in people well into their 70s and 80s.
