What is ProstaClear and how is it meant to work?

ProstaClear is a capsule supplement taken as two capsules at night. The official page presents it alongside five practical habits for prostate health: a balanced diet with plenty of leafy vegetables, some sensible sun exposure, staying active, limiting inflammatory foods such as refined sugar, and supporting healthy hormone levels. That framing is a good sign, because supplements work best on top of healthy basics.

The formula itself is divided into three labelled blends: EstroControl, TestSupport and LivSupport. Together they bring plant compounds such as resveratrol, fenugreek, Tongkat Ali and milk thistle into one routine, and the label says they are made in capsules with rice flour.

The label's advice to use the product daily for 8 weeks and then pause for 4 weeks is unusual and, in our view, a thoughtful way to use any herbal formula.

Does ProstaClear work?

The research behind the headline ingredients is encouraging, especially for fenugreek and Tongkat Ali:

  • A placebo-controlled trial of 120 men aged 43 to 70 found that a specialised fenugreek seed extract raised total and free testosterone compared with placebo after 12 weeks.[2]
  • A multicentre, placebo-controlled trial in 105 men aged 50 to 70 found that a standardised Tongkat Ali extract improved total testosterone.[3]
  • A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials concluded that Tongkat Ali improves serum total testosterone in men.[4]
  • These studies used different extracts from the ones on the label and looked at hormones rather than the prostate itself, so they support the ingredients but not the finished formula.

What this means for you: ProstaClear combines ingredients that have human research behind them with plant compounds that are still being studied, in a routine that includes built-in rest periods. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the label gives blend totals only, so use it as directed, for the 8 weeks the label suggests, and judge by how you feel.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, a full six months, which makes ProstaClear easy to try. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. Ask for the refund within 180 days. The sales page counts from the date you ordered and the refund page from when you received the order, so counting from the order date is the safe choice.
  2. Email support@getprostaclear.com, or call +1 (917) 675-3052 between 5am and 1am EST. Support will give you the US return address; send the return only there.
  3. Return the unopened bottles, including any bonus bottles. Opened bottles can be kept, but any bottles you are claiming for that are not returned must match the time the product was used. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Once the return is received, your refund is issued and can take 3 to 7 business days, or up to two weeks, to appear depending on your bank.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150 from the order date. That leaves time to see how you are getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably, and keep your order details handy, because the policy needs them to match your return.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and ClickBank order support can also help with any order question.

How to get the most out of ProstaClear

  1. Take two capsules at night, as the label directs.
  2. Follow the schedule on the label: use daily for 8 weeks, then stop for 4 weeks, and check with your doctor before starting again.
  3. Be consistent during the 8 weeks. The studies behind the main ingredients ran for around 12 weeks.
  4. Keep a simple diary of night-time trips and how comfortable you feel, so you can see any change.
  5. Keep up the basics: the five habits on the official page (balanced diet, some sun, staying active, less refined sugar and healthy hormone levels) plus routine check-ups with your doctor.

Is ProstaClear safe? Side effects

ProstaClear's label carries clear warnings, which we think is a good thing, and it is worth reading them before you order.

  • The label says to consult a licensed physician before use, and not to take it if you have prostate hypertrophy, liver disease, kidney or heart disease.
  • The official page also advises against use if you are pregnant, nursing, under 18 or taking prescription or over-the-counter medication, so check with your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for blood in urine, pain, fever, difficulty passing urine or a sudden change in symptoms; these need a professional check, not a supplement.

How ProstaClear compares with other options

  • Powder and liquid formulas: the ProstaCleanse review covers a powder drink whose label shows the dose of each main ingredient, and the PotentStream review covers a liquid taken by dropper. ProstaClear's advantages are its on-and-off schedule and price per day.
  • Single-ingredient products: fenugreek or milk thistle on their own let you control the dose, but you give up the combined formula.
  • Professional care: a urologist or doctor is the right place for symptoms, and a supplement works best alongside regular check-ups.

ProstaClear FAQ

Does ProstaClear work?

ProstaClear combines ingredients such as fenugreek and Tongkat Ali, which have placebo-controlled human research behind them, although mostly with different extracts. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and results vary from person to person. The label suggests using it for 8 weeks, then pausing for 4.

Is ProstaClear legit?

Yes. ProstaClear is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with a published label, contact details and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does ProstaClear cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $59 plus $19.95 shipping, 3 bottles cost $147 and 6 bottles cost $234, with free US shipping on the larger packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.30 to $2.63 per day.

How long does ProstaClear take to work?

The official page says it usually takes 60 to 90 days to see the best results, while the label suggests 8 weeks of daily use followed by a 4-week break.

How do I get a refund on ProstaClear?

Within 180 days, email support or call the number on the refund page, and use the US return address you are given. Unopened bottles, including bonus bottles, need to be returned, return postage is paid by the customer, and refunds can take up to two weeks to show on your statement.

Does ProstaClear have side effects?

The label advises consulting a physician before use and not taking it with prostate hypertrophy, liver disease, kidney or heart disease. People who take medication, or who are pregnant or nursing, should also ask a doctor first.

Final verdict: is ProstaClear worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a simple, well-priced nightly routine with a clear label, ProstaClear is an easy and low-risk choice, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It names every ingredient, builds in rest periods, includes fenugreek and Tongkat Ali, and comes with a six-month money-back guarantee. Keep up your check-ups and a healthy routine, and let ProstaClear do its part on top.

If you decide to go ahead, the 6-bottle package gives you the best price per day and a full six months to decide.
