What is Ageless Knees and how is it meant to work?

Ageless Knees is a knee-comfort program built around a short seated routine. You sit with a towel under your knee, use a small handheld massage wand for about a minute, then work through gentle movements that are designed to wake up and strengthen the muscles at the front of the thigh.

The idea is simple and sensible: strong thigh muscles help support the knee, and many people with achy knees have weaker quadriceps. The creator, an athletic trainer, adds a second element, a massage wand that gives a mild electrical tingle, which he pairs with the exercises. Research on both ideas is encouraging, and we cover it below.

The program arrives in two forms: a physical DVD with the wand, and a digital version you can open on a phone, tablet or computer the moment you order.

Do strengthening exercise and gentle stimulation help knees?

Ageless Knees rests on two approaches, and both have published research:

  • In a randomized clinical trial of 100 people with knee osteoarthritis, a group doing quadriceps strengthening for 8 weeks improved significantly on pain, stiffness, function and a timed up-and-go test compared with a group that only received an information booklet.[1]
  • A meta-analysis of randomized trials (14 included) found that TENS significantly reduced knee pain compared with control groups. It did not find a significant difference on the WOMAC knee score, and the authors called for larger, longer trials.[2]
  • The program's own explanation, that waking up a leg nerve is the key, is the creator's framework. We did not find a published trial of the finished program, so we rate the routine on the strength of the exercise research behind it.

What this means for you: a short, regular strengthening routine is one of the best-supported things you can do for knee comfort, and a gentle wand is a pleasant extra. Ageless Knees puts both into an easy follow-along format. Give it at least the 30 days the creator recommends, and keep your doctor in the loop.

How to get the most out of Ageless Knees

  1. Start with the digital version the day you order. You can begin the same day while the DVD and wand ship.
  2. Try the wand on its lowest setting first. The creator notes it should feel like a light tickle.
  3. Follow the 7-minute routine for each knee that bothers you, ideally every day for at least 30 days.
  4. Use the 3-minute morning flow before you get out of bed if stiffness is worst in the morning.
  5. Keep moving through the day with gentle walking, and note how stairs, kneeling and getting out of a chair feel from week to week.

Is Ageless Knees safe? Side effects

The routine is low-impact and designed to be beginner-friendly. The wand is described as using a gentle current, with a lowest setting.

  • Mild muscle soreness after starting a new exercise is common and usually settles quickly.
  • If you have a pacemaker or other implanted electrical device, are pregnant, or have a skin condition on the area, check with your doctor before using any electrical stimulation device.
  • Stop and see a professional for sharp pain, swelling, redness or warmth, locking or giving way, or an injury.
  • If you are due for, or recovering from, knee surgery, follow the plan your surgeon or physical therapist gave you.
