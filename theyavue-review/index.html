What is TheyaVue and how is it meant to work?

TheyaVue is a daily vision-support supplement. The official page describes it as a healthy-aging formula for vision and wellness that contains 24 ingredients, and it names eight of them: lutein, zeaxanthin, vitamin C, calcium, vitamin E, bilberry extract, zinc and rutin.

The thinking behind it is well grounded. Lutein and zeaxanthin are carotenoids that build up in the macula, the part of the eye responsible for sharp central vision, and they have been the focus of major eye-health research. Bilberry has a long history in eye nutrition, and vitamins C and E and zinc are antioxidants often included in eye formulas.

TheyaVue is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for an eye condition. It works best alongside regular eye exams, UV protection and a diet rich in leafy greens.

Does TheyaVue work?

The research behind TheyaVue's main ingredients is among the stronger bodies of work in supplements:

  • The AREDS2 trial, a double-masked, placebo-controlled study of 4,203 people aged 50 to 85, tested lutein and zeaxanthin for age-related macular degeneration.[2]
  • In a placebo-controlled trial of screen workers, bilberry extract improved some measures of eye fatigue after eight weeks.[3]
  • Reviews of nutrients such as vitamins C and E and zinc have looked at their role in slowing macular degeneration.[4]

What this means for you: TheyaVue combines nutrients that have been studied for eye health, and it is a sensible daily addition. The amounts of each ingredient are not stated and the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a couple of months of daily use alongside regular eye exams.

How to get the most out of TheyaVue

  1. Take two capsules once a day, 20 to 30 minutes before a meal with an 8 oz glass of water, as directed.
  2. Be consistent. The bilberry studies we found ran for 8 to 12 weeks.
  3. Take screen breaks. Look at something 20 feet away every 20 minutes.
  4. Eat leafy greens and wear UV-blocking sunglasses. These are the most reliable habits for eye health.
  5. Keep up your eye exams. A supplement does not replace them.

Is TheyaVue safe? Side effects

The ingredients in TheyaVue are generally well tolerated. The official page says it has had no reports of side effects; as with any supplement, mild stomach upset is possible, and zinc is best taken with water before a meal as directed.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take blood thinners or other prescription medicine, or have a diagnosed eye condition, check with your doctor first. Bilberry may affect blood clotting at high doses.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See an eye doctor right away for sudden vision changes, eye pain, flashes or new floaters.
