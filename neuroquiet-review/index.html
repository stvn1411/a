What is NeuroQuiet and how is it meant to work?

NeuroQuiet is a liquid supplement that you spray under the tongue. The brand positions it as support for ear health, a calmer mind and everyday energy, and it pairs ingredients associated with focus and relaxation with ones associated with healthy circulation.

The format is a real advantage for some buyers. A spray needs no water and no pills, and the directions are simple: two sprays in the morning and four before bedtime, held under the tongue for 20 seconds before swallowing. If you find large capsules a chore, this is a friendlier routine.

It helps to be clear about what the product is. It is a dietary supplement, not a hearing aid or a medical treatment, and it works best as one part of a routine that includes hearing check-ups, protecting your ears from loud noise and good sleep.

Does NeuroQuiet work?

The honest picture is that NeuroQuiet is built from ingredients with real research in other settings, and the finished spray has not had its own trial:

  • The alpha-GPC evidence is the strongest of the group: a 12-week randomized, double-blind trial found a clear improvement in cognitive test scores compared with placebo.[2]
  • Tyrosine has encouraging results for mental performance under stress or demanding conditions.[5]
  • Oral GABA has early evidence for relaxation, though reviews describe the overall picture as mixed, so we treat the calm-and-focus benefit as a pleasant possibility rather than a promise.[3]
  • For hearing itself, we did not find trials on these ingredients, so we rate NeuroQuiet mainly as a focus-and-calm formula that the brand positions alongside ear health.

What this means for you: NeuroQuiet is a thoughtful blend of familiar ingredients in an easy format. Give it a few weeks of consistent use, keep up with hearing check-ups and ear protection, and use the 90-day guarantee as your safety net.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee, which makes NeuroQuiet low-risk to try. Here is how it works, based on the return policy:

  1. The 90 days count from your date of purchase, and the return must be requested and approved within that window.
  2. Email support (support@neuroquiet.com) or call +1-877-861-0092 to request a return authorization (RMA) number.
  3. Send the product back with the RMA number and your proof of purchase to the return address in the policy. Return postage is paid by you, and the policy asks you to ship it within 14 days of receiving the RMA.
  4. Once the return is received, the sales page says the refund is issued within 48 hours, minus shipping and handling fees.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 75. The request has to be made inside the 90 days, so that leaves you time to see how you are getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, and the page notes that ClickBank is the retailer. The support line is open every day from 10 AM to 1 AM Eastern, according to the contact page.

How to get the most out of NeuroQuiet

  1. Follow the directions. Shake well, spray under the tongue (2 sprays in the morning, 4 before bedtime) and hold for 20 seconds before swallowing.
  2. Keep eating and drinking away from it. The directions suggest avoiding food and drink for 30 minutes before and after.
  3. Be consistent. The brand suggests about three months of daily use for the full picture.
  4. Protect your ears as well. Turn the volume down, use ear protection around loud noise and keep up with hearing check-ups.
  5. Keep notes. A short weekly note on focus, mood and how you feel makes it easy to decide before the guarantee ends.

Is NeuroQuiet safe? Side effects

NeuroQuiet is sold as a dietary supplement, and the sales page does not list doses, so we cannot judge how strong it is. Some of its ingredients deserve a little care.

  • If you take prescription medicine, particularly for blood pressure, Parkinson's disease or mood, check with your doctor first. L-arginine and L-dopa bean can interact with some medicines.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or have a medical condition, ask a doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for sudden hearing loss, new ringing, dizziness or ear pain. These need a professional check and should not wait for a supplement.

How NeuroQuiet compares with other options
