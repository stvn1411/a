What is Cholibrium and how is it meant to work?

Cholibrium is a daily capsule supplement that combines ten mushroom extracts in one formula. Mushrooms such as reishi, cordyceps and shiitake have a long history in traditional wellness routines, and they are now widely studied for their nutrients, fibre-like compounds and plant chemicals.

The manufacturer positions Cholibrium as a way to add this variety to a heart-healthy lifestyle: take two capsules a day, ideally 20–30 minutes before a meal, with a glass of water. It is described as allergen-free, gluten-free, non-GMO and made in a cGMP-certified facility.

Each package also comes with three guides: 21 heart-healthy dessert recipes, 10 smoothie and juice recipes and a guide to reading food labels for blood pressure. These pair naturally with the capsules.

Does Cholibrium work? What the research says

Cholibrium has not been through its own clinical trial, so the research below is about its ingredients. Mushroom research is a growing field, and the honest summary is that it is promising rather than settled:

  • For reishi, the best-studied mushroom in the blend, a 2025 review pooled 17 randomized trials with 971 participants. Overall effects on blood pressure and lipids were not significant, but lower doses and younger participants showed some benefit for total cholesterol.[2]
  • A randomized, double-blind trial of shiitake bars in people with borderline high cholesterol shows researchers are actively testing culinary mushrooms for heart-health markers.[4]
  • Mushrooms are also a nutritious part of a heart-healthy diet, which is the setting Cholibrium is designed for.

What this means for you: Cholibrium is best seen as an easy, low-risk way to add a variety of mushroom extracts to a heart-healthy lifestyle. It does not replace diet, exercise or medical care, and the 365-day guarantee lets you try it for a full year.

How to get the most out of Cholibrium

  1. Take two capsules daily, ideally 20–30 minutes before a meal with a glass of water, as the manufacturer suggests.
  2. Be consistent. Mushroom research typically runs for many weeks or months.
  3. Build the habit around a meal you eat every day so you do not forget.
  4. Use the bonus guides. The recipes and label-reading guide fit neatly into a heart-healthy way of eating.
  5. Keep up the basics: regular exercise, plenty of vegetables and fibre, not smoking and regular check-ups.

Is Cholibrium safe? Side effects

Mushroom extracts are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and Cholibrium is free of gluten and common allergens. As with any supplement, mild digestive changes are the most commonly reported effect.

  • If you take blood pressure, cholesterol or blood-thinning medicine, check with your doctor before starting, and do not stop any prescribed medicine.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing, have an autoimmune condition or a mushroom allergy, ask your doctor first.
  • Seek urgent medical help for chest pain, shortness of breath or sudden weakness; these need immediate care, not a supplement.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
