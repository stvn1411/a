What is ProstaLite and how is it meant to work?

ProstaLite is a once-a-day capsule made for men who want to look after their prostate and urinary comfort as they get older. It brings together plant extracts, a plant pigment (lycopene) and a milk thistle compound in a single, simple capsule, with no stimulants.

The maker explains the idea in terms of fat deposits around the prostate, which it says the formula targets. That is the maker's own theory, and we haven't seen it tested on the finished product. What we can say is that several of the ingredients are well known in men's health research, and that the formula is easy to take and backed by a 60-day refund.

The label lists seven ingredients as a proprietary blend: Uvaria rufa Blume extract, bamboo (Phyllostachys edulis) extract, jaboticaba skin extract, silymarin, neem leaf powder, pumpkin seed extract and lycopene.

Does ProstaLite work?

The honest picture is encouraging but not conclusive, which is typical for plant-based men's health formulas:

  • Pumpkin seed is one of the most studied plant ingredients for men's urinary comfort. In the large 12-month GRANU study, the main response rate was similar to placebo, but the researchers saw a descriptive difference favouring pumpkin seed and recommended further confirmatory trials.[3]
  • A small 12-month Korean trial (47 men) of pumpkin seed oil and saw palmetto oil found both oils clinically safe, though differences on the measured outcomes were not statistically significant in a study that size.[4]
  • Lycopene has been studied in several small trials. A review of eight trials concluded there were too few good-quality studies to support or refute its use for prostate conditions, so we treat it as a promising extra, not a proven one.[2]
  • The other ingredients are backed mainly by laboratory and animal research, which is how most plant ingredients start out.[5]

What this means for you: ProstaLite is a sensible, simple way to add well-known men's health ingredients to your daily routine. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial, so go in with realistic expectations, use it consistently for a few months as the maker suggests, and keep up with regular check-ups.

Is ProstaLite safe? Side effects

The formula is made of plant ingredients and contains no stimulants, and the maker reports no notable side effects. As with any supplement, a few people may notice mild stomach upset.

  • If you take prescription medicine (especially for blood pressure, prostate or urinary issues, or blood thinners) or have a medical condition, show the label to your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for blood in the urine, pain when urinating, difficulty emptying your bladder, or a sudden change in urinary symptoms. These need a professional check, not a supplement.
