What is Kerassentials and how is it meant to work?

Kerassentials is a topical oil blend that you apply to the nail bed once a day, after your shower, when nails and skin are clean and soft. It is designed as a daily care step rather than a quick fix, and the manufacturer suggests using it consistently.

The formula brings together plant oils with a long history of use in skin care, plus aloe vera, a stable form of vitamin E and undecylenic acid, a fatty acid found in many over-the-counter foot products. Several of these ingredients, including tea tree oil and undecylenic acid, have been looked at in published studies on skin and nail microbes.

Because it is a topical product, there is nothing to swallow and no pill routine to remember. If you like simple habits, this is an easy one to add.

Does Kerassentials work? What the research says

Kerassentials has not been put through its own clinical trial, so the research below is about its key ingredients. It is encouraging, and the picture is consistent:

  • In a randomized, double-blind trial of 117 people with toenail problems, tea tree oil applied twice daily for six months gave results comparable to a standard clotrimazole solution, with about 60% of both groups showing clinical improvement.[2]
  • Undecylenic acid performed on a par with tolnaftate, a well-known antifungal, in a 103-person double-blind trial, and both beat placebo.[4]
  • Lavender oil showed antifungal activity in laboratory tests, which fits its traditional use in skin care.[5]

What this means for you: Kerassentials combines ingredients that each have a reasonable research story, in an easy daily format. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and nails grow slowly, so plan on using it consistently for several months and keep up good foot hygiene alongside it.

How to get the most out of Kerassentials

  1. Apply it after your shower, when nails and skin are clean and dry, as the official directions suggest.
  2. Be consistent. Nails grow slowly, so judge the routine over months, not days.
  3. Keep feet clean and dry, change socks daily and let shoes air out between wears.
  4. Wear breathable shoes and flip-flops in shared showers or pool areas.
  5. Take a photo of your nails now and again each month so you can see gradual changes.

Is Kerassentials safe? Side effects

Kerassentials is a topical plant-oil product with no stimulants, and it is applied to the nail bed rather than swallowed. As with any essential-oil product, skin sensitivity is the main thing to watch for.

  • Do a small patch test first if you have sensitive or allergy-prone skin; stop using it if you notice irritation.
  • If you have diabetes or poor circulation, are pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • See a doctor or podiatrist for nails that are painful, swollen, discharging or spreading.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
