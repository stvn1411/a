What is KeraFirm Pro and how is it meant to work?

KeraFirm Pro is a liquid you brush onto the nail and work gently into the cuticle. The manufacturer describes it as an applicator solution for people dealing with stubborn toenail problems and the daily frustration of products that do not seem to last. It lists 13 ingredients, mostly well-known plant oils.

The formula combines oils with long traditions in foot and skin care (tea tree, clove, lavender, manuka, camphor, menthol) with nourishing carrier oils (flaxseed, sweet almond, walnut, chia seed), aloe vera for comfort and vitamin E. Undecylenic acid is also included, an ingredient found in many over-the-counter foot-care products.

Because it is a topical, it works where you put it, and there are no capsules to swallow. The routine is four short applications a day, which is the main commitment.

Does KeraFirm Pro work? What the research says

Research on topical plant oils for nails is limited, but the best-known ingredient has been tested in people:

  • In a double-blind, multicenter trial, 117 people with toenail problems applied either 1% clotrimazole solution or 100% tea tree oil twice daily for six months. Partial or full clinical improvement was seen in about 60% of both groups, and about 55% of each group were still improving or resolved three months after the treatment ended.[2]
  • In the same trial, laboratory cultures were cleared in 18% of people using tea tree oil versus 11% with clotrimazole. These are modest numbers, which tells us nail care takes patience.[2]
  • The study tested pure tea tree oil, not KeraFirm Pro, and was published in 1994. We found no published trial of this formula.

What this means for you: tea tree oil has real human research behind it, and KeraFirm Pro surrounds it with conditioning oils and aloe for comfort. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and nails grow slowly, so expect to give it many weeks of steady use. The manufacturer itself suggests at least 3 months before judging.

How to get the most out of KeraFirm Pro

  1. Apply four times a day: twice in the morning and twice in the afternoon, using the brush applicator to coat the nail.
  2. Work it into the cuticle with a cotton swab, as the manufacturer directs.
  3. Keep feet clean and dry and change socks daily; breathable shoes help nails and skin.
  4. Be patient. Nails grow slowly, and the manufacturer suggests at least 3 months before judging.
  5. Do a small patch test first if you have sensitive skin, since essential oils can irritate some people.

Is KeraFirm Pro safe? Side effects

KeraFirm Pro is a topical product with no stimulants. The most common issue with essential-oil products is mild skin irritation in people who are sensitive to them.

  • Tea tree oil can cause skin irritation or allergic contact dermatitis in some people. Stop using it if you notice redness, itching or swelling.
  • Nail changes can have several causes. If your nail is painful, swollen, draining or changing quickly, see a doctor or podiatrist for a diagnosis.
  • If you have diabetes or poor circulation, check with your doctor before using any new foot product.
  • Dietary supplements and wellness products are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]
