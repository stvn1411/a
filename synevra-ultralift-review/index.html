What is Synevra UltraLift and how is it meant to work?

Synevra Lift & Lock is a two-part skincare routine. The first part, UltraLift, is a serum you apply to the skin. The second, VitaLock, is a set of beauty nutrients you take by mouth. The idea is straightforward: give skin hydration and smoothing ingredients on the outside, and the vitamins and minerals it uses on the inside.

The serum centres on a dipeptide complex called Syn-Ake, which the company describes as a peptide that helps smooth the look of expression lines, together with sodium hyaluronate for hydration, vitamin C for a brighter look, vitamin E as an antioxidant and glycerin to hold in moisture. VitaLock adds biotin, niacinamide, vitamin B6, zinc and horsetail extract.

It is a cosmetic and nutrition routine, not a medical treatment: it aims to help skin look and feel its best alongside sunscreen, sleep and a balanced diet.

Do ingredients like those in Synevra work?

The research is strongest for hydration, which is where the serum puts most of its effort:

  • In a randomized trial, creams with 0.1% hyaluronan applied around the eyes for 60 days improved skin hydration and elasticity compared with placebo.[2]
  • A 12-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of adults aged 35 to 64 found that oral hyaluronan was associated with fewer visible wrinkles and better skin condition.[3]
  • A 16-week randomized trial of collagen plus vitamin C, with or without hyaluronic acid, found improvements in skin density, texture and wrinkle severity, supporting the idea that nutrition and skin go hand in hand.[4]

What this means for you: hydrating ingredients and skin-friendly nutrients are well studied, and Synevra combines several of them in a simple routine. The finished system has not had its own clinical trial, and the Syn-Ake peptide has limited independent research, so expect a smoother, more hydrated look rather than a dramatic change.

How to get the most out of Synevra UltraLift

  1. Use the serum as directed on the label, on clean skin, as part of a simple routine.
  2. Add sunscreen every morning. Sun protection does more for the look of skin than any serum.
  3. Be consistent. Skin changes gradually; give any routine at least 4 to 8 weeks before judging it.
  4. Patch-test first on a small area, especially if your skin is sensitive.
  5. Look after the basics: sleep, water, a balanced diet and not smoking all show on your skin.

Is Synevra UltraLift safe? Side effects

Topical serums with hyaluronic acid, vitamin C and vitamin E are generally well tolerated. The most common reaction to any new skincare product is mild, temporary irritation or redness; vitamin C can sting on sensitive skin. VitaLock's vitamins and minerals are ordinary nutrients, but they are still best taken at sensible amounts.

  • If your skin is sensitive or reactive, patch-test the serum first and stop if irritation continues.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing, or take prescription medicine, or you have a medical condition, ask your doctor before taking the VitaLock supplement.
  • Very high doses of biotin can interfere with some lab tests, so tell your doctor you take it before blood tests.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor or dermatologist for a changing mole, a rash that does not clear, or painful skin. These need a professional check.

How Synevra UltraLift compares with other options

  • Standalone hyaluronic acid serums: inexpensive and simple. Synevra's advantage is pairing a serum with a supplement and offering a 60-day money-back guarantee.
  • Collagen powders and beauty supplements: some have randomized trials behind them, and they suit people who prefer taking something rather than applying it. Synevra offers both approaches in one system.
  • Dermatologist care: prescription treatments are stronger. Synevra is best seen as a gentle everyday routine on top of sunscreen and professional advice where needed.
