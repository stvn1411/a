What is ProstaVive and how is it meant to work?

ProstaVive is a powder that you stir into a glass of water once a day. It is flavoured with natural flavours, citric acid and stevia, so there are no capsules to swallow and no strong herbal taste to push through.

Rather than relying on one herb, it combines stinging nettle root, a traditional prostate-support ingredient, with herbs that men often take for general vitality, including tongkat ali, ashwagandha, panax ginseng, maca and fenugreek, plus vitamin D3, zinc, magnesium, vitamin B6 and boron.

That makes it a good match for men who want one simple product that covers prostate support and everyday wellness. It is a dietary supplement, so it works best alongside a balanced diet, regular activity and routine check-ups.

Does ProstaVive work?

ProstaVive is a blend of ingredients, so the research is about the parts rather than the whole:

  • Stinging nettle root is the ingredient most closely tied to prostate comfort. In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 620 men, 81% of the nettle group reported improved symptoms at six months compared with 16% on placebo, and the International Prostate Symptom Score fell from 19.8 to 11.8.[2]
  • That is an encouraging result, but it came from a single-author trial, the author asked for further confirmation, and the nettle preparation and dose were not the same as in ProstaVive.
  • The remaining herbs and nutrients are well known in men's wellness, but they have not been tested specifically for prostate health.
  • We did not find a published trial of the finished ProstaVive formula.

What this means for you: ProstaVive is a thoughtfully dosed, transparent blend with one prostate-relevant ingredient that has encouraging evidence and several broader wellness ingredients. Give it a couple of months of daily use and judge by how you feel, with the 180-day guarantee as your safety net.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest windows we see in this category. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days start from the day you placed your order, so note the date.
  2. Email support with "Refund Request" in the subject line, with your full name, order number and the email address used for the order.
  3. Wait for the support team to confirm before sending anything back. The policy asks for the bottles to be returned, and its wording about opened and empty bottles is not entirely clear, so ask support to confirm what to send.
  4. Return postage is paid by you; email the tracking ID of your return to support. Your refund is confirmed by email, and it usually takes 5-10 days to appear on your statement.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150. That leaves plenty of time to contact support and sort out the return details while you are still inside the window.

How to get the most out of ProstaVive

  1. Mix one scoop into a glass of water and drink it, ideally with a meal, at the same time each day.
  2. Stay consistent. A daily routine is the easiest way to judge any supplement fairly; plan to review after two to three months.
  3. Count your other supplements. ProstaVive already contains vitamin D3, zinc and magnesium, so tell your doctor about other multivitamins you take.
  4. Keep up the basics: a balanced diet, regular activity, enough water and routine check-ups.
  5. Track how you feel in a simple note, such as night-time trips to the bathroom, stream strength, energy and sleep.

Is ProstaVive safe? Side effects

ProstaVive is made of herbs, minerals and vitamins that most healthy adults tolerate well. Because it contains several active herbs, it is worth a quick word with your doctor first.

  • Herbs such as ashwagandha, ginseng and tongkat ali can interact with some medicines, so check with your doctor first if you take prescription medicine, especially for blood pressure, diabetes or thyroid conditions.
  • A serving provides 50 mcg (2,000 IU) of vitamin D3 and 12 mg of zinc, so check with your doctor before adding other supplements with the same nutrients.
  • If you are scheduled for surgery or have any ongoing health condition, ask your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for difficulty urinating, a weak or interrupted stream, pain, or blood in your urine. These need a proper check.
