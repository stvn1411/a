What is NeuroZoom and how is it meant to work?

NeuroZoom is a supplement for people who want to support memory and mental sharpness as part of a healthy routine. Rather than relying on one herb, it combines 35 ingredients that the manufacturer describes as memory essentials: antioxidants, minerals, vitamins and herbs chosen to support several parts of normal brain function at once.

The official page groups the ingredients by role. B vitamins, choline and DMAE are included for their link with the neurotransmitter acetylcholine, Bacopa and Huperzine A are traditional herbal ingredients for memory, and antioxidants such as vitamins C and E, selenium and green tea extract are there for general cellular support. The page does not state a serving size or capsule count, so follow the directions printed on the bottle.

Does NeuroZoom work?

We looked for human studies on the ingredients that have them. The picture is encouraging, with honest limits:

  • A meta-analysis of placebo-controlled trials of standardized Bacopa monnieri extract (12 weeks or longer) found improved results on cognitive tests, particularly speed of attention.[2]
  • In a 24-week trial, DHA at 900 mg a day improved learning and memory scores compared with placebo in older adults with age-related cognitive decline, and it was well tolerated.[3]
  • In a 2-year trial of 271 people over 70 with mild cognitive impairment, B vitamins slowed the rate of brain atrophy.[4] It was a different dose and population, so we treat it as supporting evidence.
  • A trial of phosphatidylserine with omega-3 reported better memory scores in older adults with memory complaints, although other phosphatidylserine studies have been mixed.[5]

What this means for you: a good number of the ingredients have genuine research behind them, and NeuroZoom brings them together in one formula. The finished product has not had its own clinical trial, and the amount of each ingredient is not listed, so think of it as a supportive habit alongside sleep, exercise and a healthy diet, and give it a few weeks.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works in practice, based on the refund policy page:

  1. You have 60 days after you receive your order to decide. If it is not for you, contact support by email before you ship anything back.
  2. Send all the items you received to the returns address given in the policy (in Largo, Florida). Return postage is at your own cost.
  3. The refund is the purchase price minus any shipping fees. A package sent on day 59 still counts, even if it arrives a few days after day 60.
  4. Once your return is received and inspected, the refund is processed to your original payment method within a few days.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar a few days before the 60 days end, and keep your tracking number when you post the bottles back.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. Keep your order confirmation handy and check your card statement for the ClickBank charge.

Is NeuroZoom safe? Side effects

The ingredients are vitamins, minerals, amino acids and plant extracts, and the manufacturer states the formula contains no stimulants. Mild digestive upset is the most commonly reported type of effect with supplements in general. The official FAQ also recommends checking with a medical professional first, especially when taking other medications.

  • If you take prescription medicine, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first. A formula with 35 ingredients is worth running past a pharmacist or doctor if you take other medicines.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Ongoing memory problems deserve a proper medical check-up. A supplement is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment.
