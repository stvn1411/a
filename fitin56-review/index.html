What is FITin56 and how is it meant to work?

FITin56 is a 56-day online fitness program led by trainer Daniel Pastor. The maker's starting point is that most plans ask for your hardest effort on day one, so one sore week or busy stretch turns into a missed month. FITin56 reverses the order: two weeks of Foundation workouts to build the habit, then a Build phase with one muscle focus a day, then an Intensity phase with bigger combinations and more cardio.

You choose one of three tracks, and every membership includes all of them: the Fitness Track for stamina and routine, the Lean Track for conditioning and fat loss, and the Muscle Track for strength and definition. Each is laid out day by day for all 56 days, and the maker suggests the Fitness Track if you are unsure.

The nutrition side is plant-forward, with five to six smaller meals a day, hand-sized portions instead of calorie counting, and one cheat meal a week. The maker says plant-forward is a recommendation, not a requirement. One transparency note from the sales page: the introduction video uses an AI-generated likeness of Daniel (with his permission), while the workout videos inside the program are real footage of him.

We did not take the program ourselves, so we judge it by what the official page describes and by the research behind its main building blocks.

Does FITin56 work?

We'd put it this way: FITin56 is built from ingredients that research supports, namely regular strength training, some cardio and a sensible, mostly plant-based way of eating, and it packages them in an order that is easy to follow.

  • In healthy young women, resistance-based programs gave a meaningful improvement in strength and lean mass and a small drop in body fat percentage, and higher training volume was linked to better results.[1]
  • In postmenopausal women, exercise training reduced fat mass, body fat percentage and waist circumference and increased muscle mass, which supports offering both strength and cardio sessions.[2]
  • Vegetarian diets were linked to about 2 kg more weight loss than non-vegetarian diets across 12 trials, with vegan diets showing the largest difference.[3]

What this means for you: the most useful thing a plan can do is help you keep going. FITin56's gentle Foundation start, half-rep option and day-by-day calendar are designed for exactly that. The program itself has not had a clinical trial, and the maker states that it does not promise a specific amount of weight loss, so treat it as a well-organised way to apply approaches that research supports.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every purchase is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how it works, based on the sales page:

  1. Request your refund within 60 days of purchase. The maker says this is longer than the 56-day program itself.
  2. Refunds are processed by ClickBank, the retailer, through the order support link on the sales page or at clkbank.com.
  3. The page does not ask you to return anything, since the program is digital. It says to contact the maker or ClickBank for a full refund.
  4. To stop the Quarterly Membership from renewing, cancel through ClickBank or the support team. Access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar around day 45. That leaves you time to finish the Foundation and Build phases, decide whether the plan suits you, and still ask for a refund comfortably inside the 60 days.

How to get the most out of FITin56

  1. Start on the Fitness Track, as the maker suggests, and cut the reps in half whenever you need to until you can keep the full pace.
  2. Schedule your 30 minutes at the same time each day. The program is designed around showing up consistently.
  3. Do the Foundation phase properly. It is there to build the habit, and rushing it defeats the point.
  4. Use the demo videos to check your form on any move you have not done before, and start with light weights.
  5. Follow the meal plans as written, or adapt them to the way you eat now. The principles work either way.
  6. Plan day 57. Repeat a phase, switch tracks or push harder. The 1-year pass makes this easy.

Is FITin56 safe? Side effects

FITin56 is an exercise and nutrition program rather than a supplement, so there are no ingredients to worry about. The main safety points are the ordinary ones for any new training plan.

  • The maker advises checking with your physician before starting, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication.
  • The workouts are described as intense. Start with light weights and use the half-reps option until you can keep up.
  • Stop exercising and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, as the maker's page also says.
  • The program is intended for adults 18 and older.
  • Mostly plant-based eating is suitable for many people, but if you have a medical condition or special dietary needs, ask your doctor or a registered dietitian first.

FITin56 FAQ

Does FITin56 work?

FITin56 is built on strength training, cardio and plant-forward eating, all of which have good research behind them, and it is designed to help people stick with a routine. The program itself has not had its own clinical trial and the maker does not promise a specific result, so outcomes depend on consistency, nutrition and your starting point.

Is FITin56 legit?

Yes. FITin56 is a real program sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with clear pricing, a members area and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does FITin56 cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: $57 every 3 months for the Quarterly Membership, $85 one time for 6 months, or $114 one time for 1 year, which works out to about $9.50 a month.

How long does FITin56 take?

The program runs 56 days across three phases. Most workouts are about 30 minutes, and Core & Abs and Stretch sessions are about 10 minutes.

How do I get a refund on FITin56?

Ask for a refund within 60 days of purchase through ClickBank, the retailer. The Quarterly Membership can also be cancelled at any time so it does not renew.

Do I need equipment for FITin56?

At a gym you will find everything you need. At home you will need dumbbells, a bench or exercise ball, and a pull-up bar or exercise bands. A yoga mat is recommended for the yoga workout.

Final verdict: is FITin56 worth it?

Yes. For anyone who wants a clear, beginner-friendly plan, FITin56 is a well-organised, good-value program, and it earns our score of 4.6 out of 5. It starts at a level you can manage, builds in stages, includes workouts, nutrition guidance and meal plans in one membership, and rests on training and eating principles that research supports. Check with your doctor if you have a health condition, begin with light weights, and be consistent.

If you decide to go ahead, the 1 Year Lifestyle Pass gives you the best price per day, no renewal and time to repeat phases or try another track, all backed by the 60-day money-back guarantee.
