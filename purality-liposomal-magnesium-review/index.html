What is Purality Liposomal Magnesium and how is it meant to work?

Purality Liposomal Magnesium (sold on the page under the PureDose and Vitality Mineral names) is a ready-to-drink magnesium supplement. Instead of tablets or powder, each serving comes in a small pouch that you can squeeze straight into your mouth, a glass of water or a spoon. The flavor is a mild vanilla and almond.

Magnesium takes part in hundreds of reactions in the body, and many people do not get as much as they would like from food. The brand's idea is to pair a gentle form of magnesium, magnesium bisglycinate, with lipid carriers (sunflower lecithin and oil) that form micelles and liposomes, tiny fat-based bubbles used in many supplements to help nutrients reach the gut wall.

It is a short, simple formula, and everything in it is listed on the sales page, which is a welcome change from long proprietary blends.

Does liquid magnesium bisglycinate work?

Magnesium is one of the best-known essential minerals, and the research on supplementing it is encouraging in several areas, with sleep among the most studied:

  • In a 2025 randomized, double-blind trial, 155 adults with poor sleep took 250 mg of elemental magnesium as bisglycinate or a placebo. After 4 weeks the magnesium group had a greater improvement in insomnia scores (-3.9 points versus -2.3), a small but real benefit, and people with lower dietary magnesium seemed to benefit most.[2]
  • A systematic review of 9 studies and more than 7,500 people found that magnesium status was linked with sleep quality in observational studies, while the randomized trials gave mixed results.[3]
  • The brand also points to research on mood, heart, bone and exercise. We focused on the sleep studies above because they used the same form of magnesium as this product.

What this means for you: magnesium bisglycinate is a sensible, well-studied form, and a liquid pouch is an easy way to take it every day. The finished product has not had its own clinical trial, and the brand's claim of higher absorption comes from its delivery technology rather than a published study of this formula. Give it a few weeks of daily use and see how you feel.

How to get the most out of Purality Liposomal Magnesium

  1. Follow the label: one pouch a day, ideally on an empty stomach or about 20 minutes before a meal, and do not exceed the recommended dose.
  2. Pick a time you will remember, such as with your first glass of water in the morning or as part of an evening routine.
  3. Be consistent. The sleep trials above ran for about 4 weeks of daily use.
  4. Keep eating magnesium-rich foods such as leafy greens, nuts, seeds and whole grains. A supplement works best on top of a good diet.
  5. Notice the changes: how you sleep, how your muscles feel after exercise and how you feel overall.

Is Purality Liposomal Magnesium safe? Side effects

Magnesium is an essential mineral and is generally well tolerated at sensible doses. The most common issue with magnesium supplements is loose stools, which is more likely if you take more than the recommended serving.

  • The formula contains almonds, so avoid it if you have a tree nut allergy.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, have kidney problems or any known medical condition, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting. This matches the caution printed on the sales page.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for persistent muscle cramps, irregular heartbeat or long-lasting sleep problems rather than relying on a supplement alone.

How Purality Liposomal Magnesium compares with other options

  • Magnesium capsules and powders: these are usually cheaper per serving, and bisglycinate capsules use the same form of magnesium. Purality's advantages are its single-serving liquid format and its long guarantee.
  • Food first: nuts, seeds, beans, leafy greens and whole grains all supply magnesium, and are a good foundation whether or not you supplement.
