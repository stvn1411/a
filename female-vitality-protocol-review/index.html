What is Female Vitality Protocol and how is it meant to work?

Female Vitality Protocol is a short, discreet digital program for women. The sales page opens with a video that introduces what it calls an upper-body alignment technique, and it supports its ideas with a list of 23 scientific references on pelvic floor strength and female sexual function.

The idea is simple and has a real research basis. The pelvic floor is a group of muscles that supports the bladder and core and takes part in sexual response. Studies in women have repeatedly linked stronger, better-coordinated pelvic floor muscles with better sexual function, and pelvic floor muscle training is one of the most widely used non-drug approaches in women's health.[2]

The program's details sit inside the video, so we describe the approach rather than each movement. It comes from the same publisher family as Pelvic Floor Strong, which our team has also reviewed; the privacy contact on the page uses the same Pelvic Floor Strong address.

Does pelvic floor training like Female Vitality Protocol work?

The research on pelvic floor training is encouraging, particularly where women want to work on strength and sexual wellbeing:

  • A 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of pelvic floor muscle training in postmenopausal women found a significant improvement in total Female Sexual Function Index scores compared with control groups, with the clearest gains in arousal, orgasm and satisfaction.[2]
  • The same authors point out that the trials were small and differed from one another, so the size of the benefit is uncertain, and results will vary from person to person.[2]
  • Women who want to understand the idea better will find the sales page's own reference list a useful reading list; it cites studies on pelvic floor strength, incontinence and sexual function.

What this means for you: the idea behind Female Vitality Protocol, strengthening and coordinating the pelvic floor, is one of the better-supported non-drug approaches in women's health. The program's own alignment technique has not been tested separately, so think of it as a guided, private way to practise pelvic floor work and judge it by how you feel after a few weeks.

How to get the most out of Female Vitality Protocol

  1. Choose a private, comfortable time when you will not be interrupted, and follow the video from the beginning.
  2. Be consistent. Pelvic floor training studies typically measured results after several weeks of regular practice.[2]
  3. Go gently. You should feel muscles engage, never sharp pain.
  4. Keep notes on bladder comfort, strength and intimate wellbeing so you can judge progress honestly.
  5. Combine it with healthy habits: regular activity, enough sleep and time to relax all support pelvic and sexual wellbeing.

Is Female Vitality Protocol safe? What to know first

Pelvic floor exercise is generally considered low risk for most healthy adults, and Female Vitality Protocol is a digital exercise program rather than a supplement, so there are no ingredients to take.

  • If you have pelvic pain, pain during intimacy, prolapse or bladder problems, see a doctor or pelvic health physiotherapist first, because some conditions need a tailored plan.
  • If you are pregnant, postpartum or recovering from surgery, ask your health professional before starting.
  • Stop and seek advice if an exercise causes pain, bleeding or a feeling of pressure.
  • The program does not replace medical care for sexual health or hormonal concerns.
