What is YU SLEEP and how is it meant to work?

YU SLEEP is a bedtime liquid you take with a dropper about half an hour before you go to sleep. It combines a modest dose of melatonin with ingredients that are popular in sleep routines: L-theanine from tea, magnesium, lemon balm, apigenin (a compound found in chamomile), 5-HTP, GABA and red tart cherry.

The idea is simple and sensible: give the body the building blocks and calming ingredients it uses at the end of the day, then pair them with a regular, relaxing routine. The sellers also include a bedtime-story audio bonus on the larger packages to help you wind down.

Because it is a liquid, there are no pills to swallow, and the 0.9 mg melatonin dose is on the lower side of what many sleep products use.

Does YU SLEEP work? What the research says

Two of the main ingredients have human research behind them:

  • A meta-analysis of 19 placebo-controlled trials with 1,683 participants found melatonin reduced the time to fall asleep and increased total sleep time compared with placebo. Longer trials and higher doses showed larger effects, and the benefit did not seem to wear off with continued use.[2]
  • In a randomized, double-blind crossover trial of 30 healthy adults, 200 mg a day of L-theanine for four weeks improved sleep-quality scores compared with placebo, including the sleep-latency and sleep-disturbance sections. The supplier of the tablets also funded the study.[3]
  • The other ingredients are popular in sleep formulas, but we did not find trials of this exact combination.

What this means for you: melatonin and L-theanine both have encouraging human research, and YU SLEEP combines them in an easy liquid. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet, and the effect of melatonin in studies is modest, so pair it with a calming, regular bedtime routine and give it a couple of weeks.

How to get the most out of YU SLEEP

  1. Take 2 droppers about 30 minutes before bed, as the manufacturer directs.
  2. Keep a regular schedule: going to bed and getting up at similar times helps any sleep product work better.
  3. Wind down: dim the lights and put screens away, or play the bonus audio stories.
  4. Watch caffeine and late meals, which are common causes of restless nights.
  5. Track how you feel over a couple of weeks and decide before the 60-day window ends.

Is YU SLEEP safe? Side effects

Melatonin at 0.9 mg is a modest dose and is generally well tolerated by healthy adults. The most common effects of melatonin are drowsiness, headache or vivid dreams.

  • Take it only when you can go to bed; do not drive or operate machinery after taking it.
  • 5-HTP acts on the serotonin pathway, so check with your doctor first if you take antidepressants or other prescription medicine.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor before using any sleep supplement.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor if you snore loudly, stop breathing at night, or have trouble sleeping for weeks; these can have causes that need a proper check.
