What is VENOPLUS 8 and how is it meant to work?

VENOPLUS 8 is a powder you stir into a glass of water once a day. Instead of one headline ingredient, it combines several that research has linked to nitric oxide, a molecule your blood vessels use to relax and widen, which supports healthy blood flow.

The formula works from two directions. Beetroot supplies dietary nitrate, while L-citrulline and L-arginine are amino acids your body uses to make nitric oxide. Vitamin K2 (as MenaQ7), magnesium, vitamin C, hesperidin, pomegranate and grape seed extracts round it out with nutrients and plant compounds associated with vascular and general wellbeing.

The official page states that it is designed to support blood pressure and cholesterol levels that are already in the normal range. It is a dietary supplement, not a medicine, and is not meant to replace advice or treatment from your doctor.

Does VENOPLUS 8 work?

We could not find a trial of VENOPLUS 8 itself, but the research on several of its key ingredients is encouraging:

  • A meta-analysis of 16 crossover trials found that dietary nitrate and beetroot juice lowered systolic blood pressure by about 4.4 mm Hg on average.[2]
  • A meta-analysis of 8 trials found that L-citrulline supplementation may lower systolic blood pressure by about 4.1 mm Hg, and the review's authors noted a diastolic effect at doses of 6 g a day or more.[3]
  • In a one-year randomized trial of vitamin K2 as MK-7 (180 µg a day), post-menopausal women showed a smaller increase in arterial stiffness than those on placebo, although this came from a post hoc analysis.[4]

What this means for you: VENOPLUS 8 puts several well-studied nitric oxide ingredients into one easy scoop. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial, and doses aren't listed, so we can't promise the same results as in the studies. If you have high or low blood pressure, or take medicine for your heart or blood pressure, talk to your doctor before starting and keep taking your medicine as prescribed.

How to get the most out of VENOPLUS 8

  1. Follow the directions: one scoop (5.4 g) in 10 fl. oz. of water daily, preferably with a meal.
  2. Take it at the same time each day so it becomes an easy habit.
  3. Give it time. Studies on its key ingredients typically ran for several weeks, so judge it after a month or two rather than a few days.
  4. Keep up the basics: a vegetable-rich diet, regular movement, good sleep and routine check-ups. The powder works best on top of these.
  5. Keep your doctor in the loop, especially if you monitor your blood pressure at home or take any medicine.

Is VENOPLUS 8 safe? Side effects

The ingredients in VENOPLUS 8 are common nutrients and plant extracts, generally well tolerated by healthy adults. The most common complaint with supplements like this is mild stomach upset, particularly with amino acids taken on an empty stomach, which is why the directions suggest taking it with a meal.

  • If you take blood pressure medicine, blood thinners or other prescription medicine, or have a heart condition, check with your doctor before starting. Ingredients such as beetroot and L-arginine may affect blood pressure, and vitamin K can interact with some blood thinners.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or planning surgery, ask your doctor first.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness or fainting, and for any symptoms that worry you. These need a professional check, not a supplement.
