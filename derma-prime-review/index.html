What is Derma Prime and how is it meant to work?

On the ClickBank marketplace the product is listed as Derma Prime, and the official site brands it as Derma Prime Plus. Either way it is a capsule you swallow, made by a US company that says its capsules are produced in a GMP-certified facility.

The sales page presents the formula as a plant-based way to support skin, and its help pages connect skin health with the liver. We could not verify that mechanism for this formula, so we have not scored it on that claim. Instead we looked at what is actually published for the ingredients it names.

The page gives no capsule count or directions, so check the bottle label on arrival and follow it. Because it works from the inside, it fits best next to the basics: enough sleep, plenty of water, sunscreen and a gentle cleanser.

Does Derma Prime work?

Here is the honest picture. No trial on Derma Prime itself is published, and the official page cites none in its own text, so we looked at its ingredients one by one:

  • Ginger supplements have a sizeable trial literature: across 29 randomized trials, supplementation was linked with lower CRP, TNF-alpha, IL-6 and oxidative-stress markers, particularly in people with existing health conditions.[3]
  • Artichoke leaf extract produced a modest, statistically significant drop in total cholesterol in a placebo-controlled trial, suggesting the herb is biologically active at the dose studied.[4]
  • Chanca piedra was tolerated well over a full year at a high dose, with no major adverse events reported.[2]

What this means for you: the herbs in Derma Prime have a reasonable track record for general wellness markers and tolerability, but none of these studies tested skin appearance. If you try it, treat it as one supportive habit, give it a few weeks and judge by how your skin feels to you. The guarantee exists for exactly that purpose.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the official page says a refund is available even if you used the whole bottle. Here is how it works:

  1. The 60 days start from the day your order shipped. The date is in your shipping email and on the package.
  2. Contact the support team by email, using the address on the refund page, and include the Return and Refund Form that came with your order.
  3. Send back all the bottles (opened or not) to the return address on the refund page. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Bottles posted within the 60 days still qualify if they arrive a little later, and refunds are processed within a few days of the package arriving. You will get an email when it is done.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 45 and keep your tracking receipt. Because the clock starts at shipping, not delivery, that leaves you comfortable time to post the bottles back if you need to.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer. ClickBank, as the retailer, can also help with any order question.

How to get the most out of Derma Prime

  1. Read the bottle label when it arrives and follow the directions printed on it, since the sales page gives none.
  2. Take it at the same time each day so it becomes a habit.
  3. Give it a few weeks. The guarantee window is about two months, so use it to judge fairly.
  4. Keep up the basics: sleep, water, a daily sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher and lukewarm showers, all tips the official page itself recommends.
  5. Take a photo in the same light once a week so you can compare changes honestly.

Is Derma Prime safe? Side effects

The official site lists no side effects and says the capsules are non-GMO, free from stimulants and not habit forming. Herbal supplements are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, but individual reactions can differ.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medicine or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting. The official page gives the same advice.
  • Ingredients such as ginger and artichoke are plants that can interact with some medicines, so mention it to your pharmacist.
  • Dietary supplements are not approved by the FDA before they are sold, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for skin changes that are painful, spreading, bleeding or not healing.
