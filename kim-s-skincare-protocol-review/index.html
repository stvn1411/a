What is Kim's SkinCare Protocol and how is it meant to work?

Kim's SkinCare Protocol is a digital program rather than a cream or supplement. Instead of selling a product, it teaches a routine: what to do in the morning and at bedtime, how to match steps to your skin type and age, and which ingredients and habits to favour or avoid.

The sales page describes seven steps for rebuilding the skin barrier, rebalancing hydration and resetting renewal, followed by a 21-day plan. You can watch the video presentation or read the text version on the official site, and the purchase is delivered through a download area.

The method is presented as coming from a retired South Korean dermatologist, written up by an author who uses a pen name. We could not verify those credentials, so we judge the program on what it teaches and how it is sold.

Does a routine-based program like this work?

A routine guide is not a treatment, so the fair question is whether the habits it builds on are sound. A few are well supported:

  • The American Academy of Dermatology advises daily broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher, with reapplication about every two hours or after swimming or sweating.[1] Any routine you follow is stronger with this habit in place.
  • Green tea polyphenols, the one ingredient the program names, have human trial data on UV-induced redness and skin quality in a small 12-week study.[2]
  • Researchers describe green tea polyphenols as promising to use alongside traditional sunscreens, which fits the idea of a layered routine.[3]

What this means for you: the program's value is mostly in giving you structure and consistency. The finished program has not had its own clinical trial, so judge it by how your skin looks and feels after several weeks of steady use, and keep sunscreen in your routine.

How the 60-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The refund page covers the first 60 days after purchase.
  2. Email the vendor at support@skincareprotocol.com and ask for a full refund. Explaining why is optional.
  3. Allow 2 to 10 business days for the refund to appear in your account.
  4. Because the product is digital, nothing is shipped, so there is no return postage. The page does not mention anything that must be sent back.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 50. That gives you time to judge your progress and still use the guarantee comfortably.

How to get the most out of the program

  1. Start with the video or text presentation, then work through the seven steps in order.
  2. Identify your skin type first, so you pick the right playbook.
  3. Patch-test new products on a small area before using them on your face.
  4. Be consistent. The plan is built around a 21-day routine, and skin changes gradually.
  5. Keep daily sunscreen in your routine, SPF 30 or higher and broad-spectrum.
  6. Take photos in the same light at the start and after three weeks, so you can judge progress honestly.

Is Kim's SkinCare Protocol safe? Side effects

The program is a guide to routines and habits, not a medicine or supplement, so there is nothing to swallow. Any new skincare product can irritate sensitive skin, so go gradually.

  • If you have a skin condition, allergies or very sensitive skin, check with a dermatologist before changing your routine.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, ask your doctor before using new active ingredients.
  • See a doctor for a changing mole, a sore that does not heal, or persistent redness, pain or swelling.

How it compares with other options

  • Free online routines: plenty of general advice exists for free. This program's advantage is having everything organised in one plan by skin type and age.
  • Skincare products and serums: these cost more over time and give no guidance on routine. This guide is a lower-cost starting point.
  • A dermatologist visit: nothing replaces personal advice for a skin condition, and a program like this works best as a complement.
