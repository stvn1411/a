What is Acne No More and how is it meant to work?

Acne No More is a downloadable guide written by Mike Walden, who says he lived with severe acne for more than a decade before building the approach he now teaches. The sales page presents it as a five-step system that you work through in order.

Instead of focusing on creams or cleansers, the guide takes a whole-body view: what you eat, how you live, and how you support your body's balance. It is delivered as a PDF you can read on a computer or print out, with diagrams and step-by-step instructions.

That food-first focus matches a real area of research. Several controlled trials have looked at how eating lower-glycemic-load foods affects acne, with encouraging results (more on that below).

Does a food-first approach to acne work?

Research on diet and acne is growing, and it supports the core idea behind guides like this one:

  • In a 12-week randomized trial of 43 young men with acne, a low-glycemic-load diet reduced total lesion counts by 23.5 on average, compared with 12.0 in the control group.[2]
  • In a separate 10-week randomized trial of 32 people with mild to moderate acne, those on a low-glycemic-load diet had significant improvement in both inflamed and non-inflamed lesions.[3]
  • Both trials were small and short, so they suggest rather than prove a link. We did not find a trial of the Acne No More system itself.

What this means for you: eating lower-glycemic-load foods is a research-backed lifestyle step for acne, and Acne No More puts a food-first plan into an easy, low-cost guide. Give it several weeks, keep any treatment your dermatologist has prescribed, and see how your skin responds.

How to get the most out of Acne No More

  1. Read the whole guide before you start, then follow the steps in the order they appear.
  2. Make food changes gradually so they are easy to keep up.
  3. Give it 8 to 12 weeks. The diet trials above measured results over 10 to 12 weeks.
  4. Keep a simple photo diary in the same light every two weeks.
  5. Keep your usual skin care and any dermatologist-prescribed treatment going while you try it.

Is Acne No More safe? Things to check

The guide is a book, so there are no capsules to swallow. The main thing to be careful about is changing treatments or taking supplements without advice.

  • Do not stop prescription acne medicine because of anything you read in any guide; talk to your dermatologist first.
  • If you plan to try a supplement the guide suggests, check with your doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you are pregnant, nursing or on medication.[1]
  • See a dermatologist for severe, painful or scarring acne; the American Academy of Dermatology publishes evidence-based guidelines for acne care.[4]
