What is Pulmo Balance and how is it meant to work?

Pulmo Balance is a capsule you take with a meal each day. It is made for adults who want steady, everyday support for their lungs and airways using a blend of plant extracts rather than a stimulant or a quick fix.

The idea is to combine several ingredients with a long track record in respiratory and antioxidant research. Mullein is a traditional herb for the respiratory tract, bromelain is an enzyme from pineapple, maritime pine bark is a well-studied antioxidant extract, and quercetin is a plant flavonoid found in many fruits and vegetables.

According to the order page the formula contains 13 ingredients; it names six of them, and the references list also mentions BioPerine (black pepper extract).

Do respiratory formulas like Pulmo Balance work?

The research on the individual ingredients is a mix of promising and early-stage:

  • A 2024 review of 39 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials of one maritime pine bark extract reported that small trials in asthma and seasonal allergies showed symptom improvements compared with placebo.[3]
  • In a randomized safety trial, people with COPD tolerated quercetin up to 2,000 mg a day for a week without serious side effects.[2]
  • Mullein is one of the best-known traditional herbs for the chest, although reviews note the evidence is mostly traditional use rather than human trials.[4]

What this means for you: Pulmo Balance puts several recognizable, generally well-tolerated ingredients into an easy daily capsule, and one of them (pine bark) has real clinical research behind it. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and doses are not disclosed, so treat it as a supportive habit and give it a couple of months alongside healthy routines.

How the 90-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 90-day money-back guarantee, which makes Pulmo Balance easy to try. Here is how the return policy describes it:

  1. The window is 90 days from the purchase date, and the return authorization (RMA) must be requested and approved within that time.
  2. Email support with your order details to request an RMA number.
  3. Send the product back within 14 days of receiving the RMA, with the RMA number and proof of purchase, and email the tracking number. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. After the return is inspected you receive an email, and an approved refund goes back to your original payment method. Shipping and handling fees are not refunded.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 60, and request the RMA early. That gives you time to see how you are getting on and still use the guarantee comfortably.

Product support is at support@pulmobalance.com, and ClickBank order support can help with any billing question through clkbank.com.

How to get the most out of Pulmo Balance

  1. Take two capsules with a meal each day, as the directions say, and do not exceed the recommended amount.
  2. Be consistent. Give it a couple of months of regular use before judging.
  3. Keep the basics in place: avoid smoke, stay active, keep up with check-ups and stay hydrated.
  4. Note how you feel in a short diary: breathing during activity, morning comfort and energy.
  5. Keep your order confirmation and RMA steps handy for the 90-day window.

Is Pulmo Balance safe? Side effects

Plant extracts like these are generally well tolerated by healthy adults. Mild stomach upset is the most common complaint with supplements of this kind.

  • The directions say the product is not for anyone under 18, or for pregnant or nursing mothers.
  • Bromelain comes from pineapple and may not suit people with pineapple allergy or those taking blood thinners, and quercetin and other extracts can interact with some medicines, so check with your doctor if you take prescriptions.
  • Anyone with asthma, COPD or another diagnosed lung condition should talk to their doctor first and should not use a supplement in place of prescribed treatment.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for shortness of breath, chest pain, coughing blood or a cough lasting more than a few weeks.
