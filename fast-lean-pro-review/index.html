What is Fast Lean Pro and how is it meant to work?

Fast Lean Pro is a powder that you mix into a drink once a day. The official page says it has no taste, so it can go into water, or into tea or coffee if you prefer. There are no capsules to swallow and no schedule of meals to follow, which is a big part of its appeal.

The idea behind it comes from intermittent fasting. The sales page explains that going without food for long stretches switches on processes in the body linked to using stored fat and recycling old cells, a process often called autophagy. Many people find fasting hard to keep up, so Fast Lean Pro is designed to support those same pathways with a blend of nutrients and a fiber while you keep eating normally.

That explanation is the maker's own concept, and the finished formula has not had a clinical trial of its own, so in this review we look at what each named ingredient shows in published research. The page mentions six named ingredients in one section and a larger number in its FAQ; we cover the six that are named.

Do the ingredients in Fast Lean Pro work?

Fast Lean Pro has not been tested as a finished product, so the fair question is whether its main ingredients have human research behind them. Two of them do:

  • A Cochrane systematic review pooled six placebo-controlled trials of chromium picolinate and found about 1.1 kg more weight lost than with placebo after 12 to 16 weeks. The authors rated the evidence as low quality and the size of the effect as of debatable clinical relevance, so it is best seen as a small, supportive contribution.[2]
  • In a randomized, double-blind crossover study, 10 g of Fibersol-2 taken with a meal delayed hunger for around 1.5 to 2 hours and raised the satiety hormones PYY and GLP-1, while 5 g did not. The study was small (19 healthy adults) and short, and the amount of Fibersol-2 in each Fast Lean Pro serving is not disclosed.[3]
  • Niacin and vitamin B12 support normal energy metabolism, which fits a formula built for people who want to keep their energy up while managing their weight.

What this means for you: Fast Lean Pro pairs a satiety-focused fiber with chromium and two B vitamins in an easy daily drink. The research behind chromium and the fiber is encouraging but modest, so the best way to use it is as extra support for balanced meals and regular movement. The 180-day guarantee gives you time to decide how it works for you.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, which is much longer than the usual 30 or 60 days. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days start from the day your order is delivered; check the carrier's tracking page for the date.
  2. Email the support team with "Refund Request" in the subject line.
  3. Send all the jars back, empty or not, with the packing slip. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Your refund is processed within about 5 to 10 days of the package arriving.

Helpful tip: the maker suggests using Fast Lean Pro for at least 3 months before deciding. Put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150 so you have plenty of time to use the guarantee comfortably if you need it.

How to get the most out of Fast Lean Pro

  1. Follow the directions on the jar for how much to use each day.
  2. Mix it into a drink you enjoy. The maker says it is tasteless and suggests tea or coffee as well as water.
  3. Be consistent. The maker advises at least 3 months, and the chromium trials measured results after 12 to 16 weeks.
  4. Build on the basics: balanced meals with protein and vegetables, plenty of water and a daily walk or other activity you enjoy.
  5. Track simple measures such as waist size, energy and how full you feel between meals, so you can judge how it works for you.

Is Fast Lean Pro safe? Side effects

The sales page describes the formula as stimulant-free and made from non-GMO, plant-based ingredients. The most common effect of added fiber in general is mild, temporary bloating or a change in digestion as your body adjusts.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, under 18 or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting any supplement.
  • If you manage blood sugar or diabetes, ask your doctor first, because chromium and fiber can influence glucose levels.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor if you have unexplained weight changes, ongoing digestive symptoms or a reaction after taking it.

Fast Lean Pro FAQ
