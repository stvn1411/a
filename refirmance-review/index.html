What is ReFirmance and how is it meant to work?

ReFirmance is a lightweight serum that you apply to your face, jawline and neck twice a day. It is made for women who want to support firmer, smoother-looking skin as they age, and it is a skin-care product that you apply, not a capsule that you swallow.

The maker explains it in terms of collagen and hyaluronic acid: when collagen breaks down, its fragments can interfere with a signal that tells skin to take up hyaluronic acid, and the formula is meant to support the skin's natural collagen process. That is the maker's own explanation and we haven't seen it tested on the finished serum. What we can say is that the formula includes a mix of peptides and soothing plant ingredients that are common in modern skin care, and the routine takes under a minute.

The label lists 17 ingredients, including Juvinity (a micrococcus lysate), aloe, glycerin, cucumber extract, chrysin and several palmitoyl peptides.

Does ReFirmance work?

Peptide-based skin care has real research behind it, and ReFirmance builds on that:

  • Palmitoyl pentapeptide-3 improved the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles in a 12-week double-blind study of 93 women, and the skin tolerated it well.[1]
  • Humectants such as glycerin and soothing extracts such as aloe and cucumber help skin feel hydrated and smoother, which is often the first change people notice from a serum.[2]
  • The research on peptides used a different formula and concentration, and no trial has tested ReFirmance itself, so the effect on sagging skin or jowls is not proven. Skin care generally works gradually and best with daily use.

What this means for you: ReFirmance is a pleasant, well-priced serum that uses ingredients with a track record in skin care. Use it twice a day for several weeks, protect your skin from the sun, and expect a gradual change in how your skin looks and feels rather than a dramatic lift.

How to get the most out of ReFirmance

  1. Patch test first. Try a small amount on your inner forearm or behind the ear for a day before using it on your face.
  2. Apply twice a day, morning and evening, a nickel-sized amount to the face, jawline and neck.
  3. Massage gently in small circular motions, or tap it in until absorbed, and keep it away from your eyes.
  4. Use sunscreen every morning. Sun protection matters more for firmness than any serum.
  5. Be consistent. The peptide study ran for 12 weeks, so give it a few months and take photos in the same light to track changes.

Is ReFirmance safe? Side effects

ReFirmance is a topical serum that uses ingredients common in skin care, and the maker reports no notable side effects. Even so, any skin product can occasionally cause redness, stinging or irritation in sensitive skin.

  • Do a patch test first, and stop using it if you notice irritation, rash or swelling.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, have allergies or a skin condition such as eczema or rosacea, or use prescription skin treatments, check with your doctor or dermatologist first.
  • See a doctor for a mole or spot that changes, or any skin change that worries you.

How ReFirmance compares with other options

  • Prescription retinoids: these have the strongest evidence for aging skin but can be irritating. ReFirmance is a gentler, easier daily routine and can be discussed alongside any dermatologist's advice.
  • Other peptide serums: many list concentrations or percentages. ReFirmance's strengths are its price per day, free US shipping and money-back guarantee.
  • Professional treatments: a dermatologist can offer in-office options for firmness; a serum is a daily, low-cost complement to those.
