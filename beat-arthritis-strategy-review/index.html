What is Beat Arthritis Strategy and how is it meant to work?

Beat Arthritis Strategy is a digital programme, not a pill. It breaks a lifestyle plan into 21 short daily steps: on each day you add one small change, such as removing a food ingredient, adding a supplement or doing a few minutes of movement.

The idea is to make change manageable. Instead of overhauling your routine in one go, you build up gradually, which many people find easier to stick with. The programme is delivered online as a video with a text transcript, so you can start straight away.

The sales page presents it as a way to relieve stiffness and support joint comfort for many types of arthritis. We would describe it more modestly: a structured, supermarket-ingredient lifestyle plan that fits alongside the care your doctor recommends.

A note on naming: the original listing for this offer was called "Cure Arthritis Naturally", but that title does not appear on the sales page we reached, so we review it under the name the page itself uses.

Does Beat Arthritis Strategy work?

The most useful way to judge a programme like this is to look at the research on its parts:

  • A meta-analysis of seven trials with 797 participants found that curcuminoids gave better pain relief and quality of life than placebo in knee osteoarthritis, although they were less effective than ibuprofen and the authors asked for more trials.[1]
  • A meta-analysis of curcuminoids alone found improvements in pain and function compared with placebo, and results that were not inferior to anti-inflammatory drugs on pain and function.[2]
  • A 2025 network meta-analysis found that turmeric products significantly reduced knee osteoarthritis pain compared with placebo.[3]
  • Gentle exercise such as yoga has been pooled in a review of randomized trials in knee osteoarthritis.[4]

What this means for you: the supplement and movement elements of the plan are the ones with real research behind them, and they are low-cost to try. The sales page's strongest promises, such as complete freedom from arthritis in 21 days, go beyond what research shows, so treat the programme as support for comfort and routine, not as a replacement for medical care.

How to get the most out of Beat Arthritis Strategy

  1. Talk to your doctor first so you know which type of arthritis you have and which changes are right for you.
  2. Follow the daily steps in order and give each new change a day or two before judging it.
  3. Keep moving gently. Short, regular sessions beat occasional long ones for stiff joints.
  4. Track how you feel. A simple daily note on pain, stiffness and sleep shows what is helping.
  5. Keep your usual care. Carry on with prescribed treatment and appointments unless your doctor says otherwise.

Is Beat Arthritis Strategy safe? Side effects

The programme uses everyday foods, simple exercises and supplements, so it is low risk for many people, but there are a few points to check first.

  • If you take blood-thinning or other prescription medicine, check with your doctor before adding supplements such as turmeric or omega-3.
  • Arthritis comes in many forms, including inflammatory types and gout, and a correct diagnosis matters for treatment.
  • Do not stop prescribed arthritis medicine without medical advice.
  • See a doctor promptly for hot, swollen joints, fever, sudden severe pain or new numbness.

How Beat Arthritis Strategy compares with other options

  • Joint supplements: a capsule is simpler, but this plan adds structure, movement and diet changes in one place.
  • Physiotherapy: a therapist can tailor exercises to your joints, which a general programme cannot.
  • Medical treatment: your doctor can diagnose the type of arthritis and offer proven treatment; a lifestyle programme sits alongside that.
