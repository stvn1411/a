What is Synaptigen and how is it meant to work?

Synaptigen is a small tablet you either let dissolve in your mouth or chew slowly, once a day. Its formula centres on three well-known probiotic species, plus a prebiotic fibre (inulin) that probiotics feed on, a calcium source, and strawberry and peppermint extracts for a pleasant taste.

The thinking behind the product is the gut-brain connection: researchers have been studying whether the community of bacteria in the gut can influence mood, memory and thinking through the nerves, immune system and chemicals that link the two. That is an active field, and several clinical trials have tested probiotics for cognitive outcomes (more on that below).

The sales page describes a specific mechanism involving sugar build-up on neurons. We could not verify that mechanism in published research, so in this review we judge Synaptigen on the ingredients it lists and the research on probiotics and cognition more broadly.

Do probiotics like Synaptigen support memory and focus?

Research on probiotics and thinking skills is still young, but it is encouraging:

  • A 2026 meta-analysis of 21 randomized controlled trials found that probiotics improved cognition in older adults: overall cognition, processing speed, memory and spatial ability all improved compared with placebo.[2]
  • The memory result for older adults was among the strongest in that analysis, and the best results came after about 12 weeks of daily use.[2]
  • Smaller benefits were reported in children (executive function) and young adults (verbal ability), so the effect appears to be strongest in older age groups.[2]

What this means for you: probiotics are a research-backed area for supporting thinking skills, and Synaptigen puts three well-known species into an easy daily tablet. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and the strains and doses used in studies differ from product to product, so give it about three months of daily use to judge for yourself.

How to get the most out of Synaptigen

  1. Take one tablet at the same time each day. Chew it slowly or let it dissolve, as directed.
  2. Be consistent. Probiotic studies saw their best results after about 12 weeks of daily use.
  3. Feed your gut bacteria: vegetables, beans, oats and other high-fibre foods work well alongside a probiotic.
  4. Support your brain in other ways: regular sleep, physical activity and mentally engaging hobbies all matter for memory.
  5. Keep a simple note of focus and recall every few weeks, so you can judge the difference.

Is Synaptigen safe? Side effects

Probiotics are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, and Synaptigen contains no stimulants. The most common side effect of probiotics in general is mild, temporary bloating or a change in digestion as the body adjusts.

  • If you are immunocompromised, pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting any probiotic. The manufacturer says the same.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Memory changes can have many causes. If you or someone close to you notices ongoing memory loss or confusion, see a doctor rather than relying on a supplement.
