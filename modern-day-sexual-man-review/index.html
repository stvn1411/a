What is Modern Day Sexual Man and how is it meant to work?

Modern Day Sexual Man is a private, on-demand video training program for adult men. There is nothing to swallow and nothing to wear: you log in to a student area and work through short modules at your own pace.

It was created by Eric Von Sydow, a dating and confidence coach who also goes by "Hypnotica". The program is built around what he calls the Hypnotica Sex Protocol, an approach based on the idea that dopamine, the brain chemical linked to motivation and reward, plays a central role in sexual desire and arousal. The training begins with a Fast Start Session and then moves through separate modules, including one on stamina and performance, one on overcoming performance anxiety, and others on foreplay, communication and technique.

The program is explicit and written for adults. We keep our description of the content brief and factual, and we focus on what matters to a buyer: how it is organized, what it costs and how the guarantee works.

Does Modern Day Sexual Man work?

It helps to be clear about what kind of product this is. It is an instructional program, so there is no ingredient to test. What we can check is whether its main idea is grounded in real science, and it is:

  • A review in the International Journal of Impotence Research describes how dopamine in the brain's reward and hypothalamic pathways is involved in sexual motivation and arousal, drawing mostly on animal research.[1]
  • A review of the human and animal literature identifies brain dopamine systems as the core of the excitatory system for sexual desire, balanced by inhibitory systems involving serotonin and others.[2]
  • A further review concludes that dopamine has facilitative effects on sexual motivation and genital reflexes, while serotonin is mainly inhibitory.[3]

These are the same references listed on the official page. They support the general role of dopamine, but they do not test the author's protocol itself, and terms such as "dopamine hijacking" are the author's own framing rather than standard clinical terms.

What this means for you: Modern Day Sexual Man rests on a sensible, research-based starting point and delivers it as structured, easy-to-follow video lessons. Treat it as a way to learn skills and build habits, give it a few weeks of practice, and see a doctor if problems with erections or desire continue.

How to get the most out of Modern Day Sexual Man

  1. Start with the Fast Start Session, as the program recommends, before moving to the other modules.
  2. Go at your own pace. Short sessions that you actually practice are better than watching everything at once.
  3. Practice and keep notes. Skills and habits build over weeks, so a simple note on confidence and enjoyment helps you judge progress.
  4. Talk with your partner. Good communication and consent are the foundation of everything in this area.
  5. Support the basics. Sleep, exercise, a balanced diet and less alcohol all help sexual health.

Is Modern Day Sexual Man safe?

Because it is a video course, Modern Day Sexual Man has no ingestible side effects. The main things to know are about content and health:

  • It contains explicit adult material and is intended only for adults aged 18 and over.
  • It includes a section on non-monogamous relationships. That section is optional, and any approach should involve open, honest and consenting partners.
  • Ongoing difficulty with erections or sexual desire can be linked to heart health, blood sugar, medicine side effects or mood, so it is worth discussing with a doctor.
  • Low mood, anxiety or relationship stress may benefit from a conversation with a qualified therapist or counsellor.
