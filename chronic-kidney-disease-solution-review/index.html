What is the Chronic Kidney Disease Solution and how is it meant to work?

The Chronic Kidney Disease Solution is a downloadable guide, not a pill or a device. The official page describes a three-phase routine that starts with a day-by-day food framework and gut-supporting habits, moves on to food choices and optional natural compounds, and finishes with a longer-term phase.

The thinking behind it is that everyday food, blood-sugar balance and gut health all influence how the body feels, and that following a clear plan is easier than piecing advice together from many websites. The guide also includes lab-tracking sheets so readers can note their own results over time.

Because it is a guide to eating and lifestyle, its biggest value is in giving you something concrete to discuss with the people managing your kidney care.

Does the Chronic Kidney Disease Solution work?

There is no published trial of this guide's protocol, so here is what mainstream sources say about the area it covers:

  • The US National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases says medical nutrition therapy, nutrition counseling with a registered dietitian, can help slow kidney disease progression and improve quality of life.[2]
  • Reviews of plant-dominant, lower-protein diets describe them as a promising strategy in kidney disease, while noting that more high-quality trials are needed.[3][4]
  • NIDDK also stresses that sodium, protein and potassium limits are personal and are set by your health care team.[2]

What this means for you: a food-first guide matches the direction of mainstream advice, which makes it a reasonable thing to read and discuss with your kidney team. Treat the guide's results as unproven for you personally, share your plan with your clinic and let your own lab results guide you.

How to get the most out of the Chronic Kidney Disease Solution

  1. Read it through first before changing anything, so you see the whole plan.
  2. Share it with your kidney doctor or a registered dietitian and ask which parts suit your stage, labs and medicines.
  3. Keep your medicines, appointments and blood tests exactly as your care team advises.
  4. Change one thing at a time and use the lab-tracking sheets to note how you feel and what your results show.
  5. Ask before adding any supplement, as some can affect potassium, phosphorus or how medicines work.

Is the Chronic Kidney Disease Solution safe? What to know

A guide is low-risk in itself, but kidney disease is serious and individual, so how you use it matters.

  • Never stop or change prescribed medicine, skip appointments or skip lab tests because of any guide. The sales page's own disclaimer says the content is not medical advice.
  • Protein, potassium, phosphorus and sodium needs differ from person to person with kidney disease. Your care team sets your personal limits.[2]
  • Check any supplement or herbal product with your care team, since some are not suitable for people with reduced kidney function.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor promptly for swelling, much less urine, shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion or severe tiredness.

How Chronic Kidney Disease Solution compares with other options
