What is Synadentix and how is it meant to work?

Synadentix is a chewable tablet that you let melt under your tongue or chew slowly just before bed. Taking it at night is deliberate: saliva flow naturally drops while you sleep, so the evening is a good moment to give your mouth some extra support.

The formula is built on two ideas. The first is a mineral: microcrystalline hydroxyapatite is the form of calcium phosphate that tooth enamel is largely made of. The second is a set of enzymes, among them lysozyme, lactoferrin and lactoperoxidase, that also occur naturally in saliva as part of the mouth's own defences. Guava fruit powder rounds out the list.

Does Synadentix work? What the research says

Synadentix has not had its own clinical trial, so the research is about its ingredients, and it is honest to say it is still developing:

  • Lysozyme, lactoferrin and lactoperoxidase are natural parts of human saliva, which is why they are such a popular choice for oral-care products.
  • Hydroxyapatite is the mineral that gives enamel its structure, which makes it a logical ingredient for a tooth-care product.
  • In a randomized 12-week trial of tablets containing lactoferrin and lactoperoxidase, gum and bacteria measures were comparable between the groups, so we see these as gentle, supportive ingredients rather than a stand-alone solution.[1]

What this means for you: Synadentix is a thoughtful, sensibly built nighttime chewable, and its ingredients mirror what saliva already does. It is best seen as a gentle bedtime addition on top of brushing, flossing and dental visits, rather than as a replacement for any of them.

  1. Take it at bedtime, after brushing: let it melt under your tongue or chew it slowly.
