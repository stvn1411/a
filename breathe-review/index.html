What is Breathe and how is it meant to work?

Breathe is a herbal liquid you take as drops. It is built for people who want a gentle, everyday way to support their lungs, and it is aimed at adults who like the idea of a plant-based routine rather than another pill.

The sales page links to published research on several herbs. Mullein is a traditional favourite for the respiratory tract, elderberry is one of the most studied herbal ingredients for upper-respiratory comfort, and mint is a familiar fresh-tasting herb. The page presents the formula as year-round support rather than a treatment.

It is sold through ClickBank with free shipping on the 3- and 6-bottle packages and bonus guides on those larger orders.

Do herbal lung-support drops like Breathe work?

The research behind the herbs in Breathe is encouraging in places and still developing in others:

  • A meta-analysis of randomized, controlled trials found that black elderberry supplements reduced upper-respiratory symptoms, though it pooled only a small number of participants.[2]
  • In a randomized, double-blind trial of 312 long-haul air travellers, people taking an elderberry extract had shorter and milder colds than those on placebo.[3]
  • Mullein has a long history of use for the respiratory tract, but reviews note the evidence comes mostly from traditional use and laboratory studies rather than human trials.[4]

What this means for you: Breathe draws on herbs people have trusted for generations, and elderberry in particular has real clinical research behind it. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a supportive daily habit and give it a few weeks alongside your usual healthy routine.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Breathe comes with a generous refund window, one of the longest we see on supplements. Here is how the returns page describes it:

  1. The window is 6 months (180 days) from the shipment date, shown on your shipping email or order slip.
  2. Email support with "Refund Request" in the subject line, adding your full name, order number and the email used for the order.
  3. Wait for an agent to confirm before you ship anything back, then send the bottles (empty or unopened) to the returns address and email the tracking number. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Once the return is registered you receive an email, and the refund usually reaches your statement within about 5–10 days.

Helpful tip: the order page and the returns page describe the refund window in slightly different words, so save your shipping confirmation, note the date, and get the terms confirmed by email when you order. A reminder in your calendar for about day 150 keeps you comfortably inside the window.

How to get the most out of Breathe

  1. Follow the label. Use the serving printed on your bottle; the sales page FAQ mentions a few drops two to three times a day.
  2. Be consistent. Herbal supports are best judged after a few weeks of regular use.
  3. Keep the basics in place: fresh air, regular movement, plenty of water and not smoking do far more for your lungs than any supplement.
  4. Keep track of how you feel in a simple note: breathing during activity, morning comfort and sleep.
  5. Store it as directed and keep your shipping confirmation for the refund window.

Is Breathe safe? Side effects

Herbal products are usually well tolerated by healthy adults, but they are not right for everyone. Mild stomach upset is the most common complaint with herbal supplements in general.

  • If you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medicine, have an immune-related condition or are about to have surgery, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Anyone with a diagnosed lung condition such as asthma or COPD should talk to their doctor first and should not use any supplement in place of prescribed treatment.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for shortness of breath, chest pain, coughing blood or a cough lasting more than a few weeks, as these need a professional check.
