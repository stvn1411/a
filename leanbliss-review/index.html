What is LeanBliss and how is it meant to work?

LeanBliss is a soft, chocolate-flavored chew meant to be eaten each day, either as a snack or as a treat at the end of breakfast. The manufacturer explains its idea like this: swings in blood sugar can influence cravings, energy and weight, so a formula built to support steadier blood sugar may make healthy eating easier.

Rather than one headline ingredient, it combines ten: a mix of plant extracts such as Ceylon cinnamon, banaba leaf, kudzu flower, olive leaf and berberine, with xylitol as the sweetener. The official page describes the mix as a proprietary blend, so amounts for each one are not listed.

Of those ingredients, several have been studied in people, and we cover that research below.

Do ingredients like those in LeanBliss work?

The research is encouraging for several of the ingredients, though none of it tests the finished chew:

  • A meta-analysis of 12 randomized trials found that berberine modestly but significantly lowered body weight, BMI and waist circumference.[2]
  • An 8-week placebo-controlled trial found that olive leaf extract added to a calorie-reduced diet produced greater loss of weight and fat mass than diet alone, along with improvements in fasting blood sugar and insulin.[3]
  • In a 12-week trial of 81 obese adults, a kudzu flower extract lowered BMI and visceral fat area compared with placebo.[4]
  • Banaba and corosolic acid have been studied for blood sugar, and a published review reports no adverse effects in controlled human trials.[5]

What this means for you: several of the ingredients in LeanBliss have real human research behind them, and the chew format makes a daily routine easy. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial and the per-ingredient amounts are not listed, so give it a few months alongside healthy eating and regular movement before you judge it.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, which is one of the longest windows we see. Here is how it works:

  1. The 180 days start from the day your order is delivered, so check the carrier's tracking for the date.
  2. Email the support team with "Refund Request" in the subject line.
  3. Send all the bottles back, opened, empty or unopened, with the packing slip. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. Your refund is processed after the package arrives, typically in 5 to 10 days.

Helpful tip: the manufacturer asks you to use LeanBliss for at least 3 months before deciding. Put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150, so you can judge fairly and still use the guarantee comfortably.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and ClickBank's customer support (1-800-390-6035 at the time of writing) can help with any order question.

How to get the most out of LeanBliss

  1. Eat one chew daily, either as a snack or at the end of breakfast, as the manufacturer suggests.
  2. Be consistent. The manufacturer advises at least 3 months before judging results.
  3. Keep the basics: balanced meals, protein and fibre, regular walking or exercise and good sleep.
  4. Track the simple things such as cravings, energy and weight, every couple of weeks.
  5. Tell your doctor if you take diabetes medicine, so they can watch your blood sugar.

Is LeanBliss safe? Side effects and who should be careful

The manufacturer says it has not seen notable side effects, and the ingredients are plant extracts that are commonly sold in supplements. As with any supplement with blood sugar-supporting ingredients, a few points are worth knowing.

  • If you take diabetes medicine or insulin, check with your doctor first, because berberine and banaba can affect blood sugar.[2]
  • If you are pregnant, nursing, or take any prescription medicine, check with your doctor first. The manufacturer says the same.
  • Berberine can cause mild stomach upset in some people.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for unexplained weight loss or gain, constant thirst or tiredness.

LeanBliss FAQ

LeanBliss contains ingredients such as berberine, olive leaf and kudzu flower that have human research behind them for weight and body composition.
