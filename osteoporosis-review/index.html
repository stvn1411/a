What is The Bone Density Solution and how is it meant to work?

The Bone Density Solution is a digital program written by Shelly Manning. It explains, in plain language, which everyday habits affect bone strength and what to do about them. The contents list on the order page covers diet, the nutrients bones need, exercise, and gut and inflammation topics.

Rather than one big change, it breaks the plan into 14 small steps, which you can take on one a day or one every couple of days. The sections include the best diet for building strong bones, which foods supply vitamin D, vitamin K and magnesium, which proteins to choose, and three types of exercise that work together to support bone.

The exercise side stands on firm ground: reviews of trials in postmenopausal women show that resistance and impact exercise can modestly improve bone density.[2] The guide is also notable for including gentle options for people who are recovering from a fracture, which many general programs leave out.

Does a diet and exercise program like this work?

Exercise is where the evidence for bone health is strongest, and it is a core part of this program:

  • The Cochrane review of exercise for postmenopausal women found a modest but statistically significant benefit to bone density compared with control groups, with the clearest results for progressive resistance training of the legs.[2]
  • In the LIFTMOR randomized trial, postmenopausal women with low bone mass who did supervised high-intensity resistance and impact training twice a week for eight months improved bone density and physical function compared with a low-intensity home program.[3]
  • That trial was supervised and used heavy lifting, so it should not be copied at home without guidance. The Bone Density Solution describes gentler movements, which suit people starting out.

What this means for you: the strongest part of this program is the one with the best research support: regular exercise for bone. Combine it with the bone-friendly eating plan, keep up your medical check-ups, and see how you feel over a few months. It works best as a complement to your doctor's advice.

How to get the most out of The Bone Density Solution

  1. Start with the exercise steps, since exercise has the best evidence for bone, and build up slowly.[2]
  2. Take one step at a time. The guide is designed for one step a day or every couple of days.
  3. Use the bonus recipes to make the bone-friendly eating plan automatic.
  4. Ask your doctor before adding supplements, especially vitamin K or D, and keep taking any prescribed treatment.
  5. Keep up bone density scans and check-ups so you can see real progress over time.

Is The Bone Density Solution safe? What to know first

The program is a diet and exercise guide rather than a supplement, so there are no capsules to take. The main safety questions are about exercise intensity and the nutrients it discusses.

  • If you have been diagnosed with osteoporosis or have had a fracture, ask your doctor which exercises are safe for you before you start; some movements, such as deep forward bending, are usually avoided.
  • Do not stop or change prescribed osteoporosis treatment without your doctor's advice; the guide itself says it does not replace medical advice.
  • If you take blood thinners or other medicines, check with your doctor before increasing vitamin K foods or supplements.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]

How The Bone Density Solution compares with other options

  • Prescribed treatment: doctors can prescribe medicines for diagnosed osteoporosis. The guide suits people who want to add lifestyle habits alongside that care.
  • Supervised exercise programs: a physiotherapist can design a program for your bone density. This costs more, and the guide is a good lower-cost way to learn the basics.
  • Calcium and vitamin D supplements: these address only part of the picture, whereas this program also covers exercise, protein and everyday habits.
