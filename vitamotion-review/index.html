What is VitaMotion and how is it meant to work?

VitaMotion is a two-part system. The first part is a drink mix: each morning you stir one scoop into a glass of water. The second is a guided movement program of about 10 minutes a day, created by kinesiologist Rick Kaselj, with routines for the neck, mid-back, lower back, hips and pelvis.

The drink mix combines minerals, vitamins and plant extracts that are commonly chosen for muscle, nerve and joint comfort. The movement routines start with gentle, low-impact moves, include a full seated program for anyone who finds the floor difficult, and offer an advanced program when you are ready for more.

Together the idea is simple: nourish the body in the morning, then move gently in a structured order. Exercise is one of the best-supported approaches for back comfort, and the program makes it easy to start.

Do turmeric, Boswellia and exercise help the back and joints?

VitaMotion pairs plant extracts with exercise, and both have published research:

  • A Cochrane review of 249 trials found moderate-certainty evidence that exercise reduces pain in chronic low back pain compared with no treatment, usual care or placebo, by an amount considered clinically important.[1]
  • The same review found exercise probably works better than education alone and than non-exercise physical therapy for pain, and that reported side effects were mostly minor, such as muscle soreness.[1]
  • A meta-analysis of randomized trials found that turmeric extract improved knee pain and function compared with placebo, and the authors noted that larger, high-quality trials are still needed.[2]
  • A small randomized pilot trial of a Boswellia extract in knee osteoarthritis reported improved pain and function versus placebo.[3]

What this means for you: the movement program is the best-supported half of VitaMotion, and the drink mix adds a clearly dosed set of nutrients and plant extracts that people use for joint comfort. The finished product has not had its own trial, so give the routine and the drink a few weeks of daily use and keep your doctor involved if pain is significant.

How to get the most out of VitaMotion

  1. Mix one scoop with water each morning, as the seller directs.
  2. Do the 10-minute routine daily for the first month, then every other day if you like, as the FAQ suggests.
  3. Use the seated program on stiff days or if getting down to the floor is difficult.
  4. Move gently through the day: short walks and breaks from sitting add to the routine.
  5. Check the label against any other supplements you take, since the seller advises against doubling up on similar ingredients.

Is VitaMotion safe? Side effects

Most of the ingredients are common nutrients and plant extracts that people tolerate well, and VitaMotion contains no stimulants. Supplements can still affect people differently.

  • White willow bark contains salicin, a salicylate. If you are sensitive to aspirin, take blood thinners or other prescription medicine, are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor first.
  • The B12 and B6 amounts are well above daily values, so mention VitaMotion to your doctor if you take other vitamins.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.
  • See a doctor for back pain after an injury, numbness, leg weakness, fever, or loss of bladder or bowel control, and check with your doctor before starting any new exercise program if you have a medical condition.

How VitaMotion compares with other options

  • Physical therapy: a therapist can tailor exercises to you in person. VitaMotion is a lower-cost, at-home option, and many people use both.
  • Separate supplements: you can buy magnesium, turmeric and vitamin D on their own. VitaMotion's advantage is having them in one tasty scoop with the doses listed.
  • Free exercise videos: many exist online. VitaMotion stands out for its structured order, seated program and exercise swaps.
