What is Venus Factor and how is it meant to work?

Venus Factor is a once-a-day capsule made for women. The maker describes it as a "female fat-burning hormone formula" and says it is designed to support the body's natural weight-management signals. Those are the maker's own claims about how it works; we haven't seen a study of the finished formula, so we judge it by what is inside.

What is inside is refreshingly short: four plant ingredients, all familiar from other supplements and foods. Two of them, turmeric and green tea, have been studied in many human trials for body weight and waist size, which gives the formula a sensible foundation.

Every order also includes free access to the Venus Factor Body Sculpting Program, which the maker values at more than $300. We didn't review the program itself, but it is a welcome extra for anyone who wants guidance on top of the capsules.

Does Venus Factor work?

We'd put it this way: the ingredients have a respectable track record, and the findings are encouraging when they are combined with healthy habits.

  • Across 60 trials, curcumin or turmeric supplements were linked to a small average drop of about 0.8 kg in body weight and 1.3 cm in waist size.[2]
  • When green tea catechins were added to exercise training, people had a small additional reduction in weight, BMI and fat mass compared with exercise alone.[3]
  • The catechin benefits in an earlier review were seen mainly when catechins were taken with caffeine, and the authors called them modest.[4]

What this means for you: Venus Factor puts two of the better-studied weight-management botanicals into one easy capsule. Think of it as a supportive extra on top of balanced meals and regular movement, not a substitute for them. The finished formula hasn't had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of consistent use alongside your usual routine.

How to get the most out of Venus Factor

  1. Take it once a day with a glass of water, as the maker directs, and pick a time you'll remember.
  2. Be consistent. The maker suggests three to six months, and the studies on its ingredients usually run weeks to months.
  3. Build on the basics: balanced meals with enough protein and fibre, regular movement and good sleep. The green tea research showed its benefit mostly alongside exercise.[3]
  4. Use the bonus program that comes with your order for structure.
  5. Track more than the scale: waist measurement, energy and how your clothes fit.

Is Venus Factor safe? Side effects

The ingredients are familiar plant extracts, and the maker says the capsules are made in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility in the USA. Healthy adults generally tolerate turmeric and green tea well, though green tea extracts can occasionally upset the stomach.

  • If you take prescription medicine, including GLP-1 medicines, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting. The maker gives the same advice.
  • Check the label if you're sensitive to soy or caffeine-containing plants.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor if you have unexplained weight changes, or symptoms such as stomach pain, a rash or yellowing of the skin or eyes after starting any supplement.

Venus Factor FAQ

Does Venus Factor work?

Venus Factor combines four plant ingredients, and research on turmeric and green tea catechins shows small, helpful changes in body weight and waist size, especially alongside healthy eating and exercise. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial yet and doses are not listed, so results vary from person to person.

Is Venus Factor legit?

Yes. Venus Factor is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, as a one-time payment with published contact details and a 60-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Venus Factor cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 2 bottles cost $158 plus shipping, 3 bottles cost $207 plus shipping and 6 bottles cost $294 with free shipping. Each bottle is a 30-day supply, so the 6-bottle package works out to about $1.63 per day.

How long does Venus Factor take to work?

The maker recommends taking it for at least three to six months. Studies on turmeric and green tea typically run several weeks to a few months.

How do I get a refund on Venus Factor?

Ask for a refund within 60 days of your order date by contacting support, then return the product undamaged with your name, email, ClickBank invoice and order number. Return postage is paid by the customer and shipping and handling charges are not refunded.

Does Venus Factor have side effects?

The ingredients are familiar plant extracts and are generally well tolerated by healthy adults, but green tea extracts can affect some people's stomach. If you take prescription medicine, including GLP-1 medicines, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first.

Final verdict: is Venus Factor worth it?

Yes. For women who want a simple daily addition to a healthy routine, Venus Factor is an easy, sensibly built and low-risk choice, and it earns our score of 4.5 out of 5. It uses four familiar plant ingredients, two of which have solid research behind them, comes with a free program, and is a one-time payment. Pair it with balanced meals and regular movement, and check in with your doctor if you take medication. With a 60-day money-back guarantee, there's very little to lose by giving it a try.
