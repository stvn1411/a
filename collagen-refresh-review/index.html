What is Collagen Refresh and how is it meant to work?

Collagen Refresh is a drink powder from WellMe that you stir into a glass of water each day. It pairs three collagen peptide sources with the nutrients the body uses to build collagen, plus hyaluronic acid, which sits alongside collagen in skin and joints.

Collagen peptides are the best-researched part of the formula. Placebo-controlled trials have measured better skin hydration and elasticity in people taking them, and there is early research on joint comfort too (more on that below). The vitamins and minerals in the mix, such as vitamin C and copper, are part of the body's own collagen-making process.

The maker describes the formula as supporting the body's own collagen as well as providing collagen peptides directly. Each jar holds 30 servings, with a naturally sweetened berry lemonade flavor.

Does collagen like Collagen Refresh work?

Collagen peptides have a solid research base for skin, and a smaller one for joints:

  • A meta-analysis of 26 randomized controlled trials with 1,721 participants found that oral hydrolyzed collagen improved skin hydration and elasticity compared with placebo.[3]
  • In a placebo-controlled trial of 69 women, specific collagen peptides were linked with improved skin elasticity compared with placebo.[2]
  • A randomized trial of 250 people with knee osteoarthritis who took 10 g of collagen hydrolysate daily for 6 months reported better knee joint comfort.[5] A 2023 meta-analysis of four trials also found less pain with collagen peptides, though the trials were small and the authors called for better studies.[6]

What this means for you: collagen peptides are one of the better-studied supplement ingredients for skin, and Collagen Refresh gives 5,000 mg of them in a daily drink. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, and trial doses for joints were larger than one scoop, so think of it as steady daily support, best judged after a few months.

How to get the most out of Collagen Refresh

  1. Stir one scoop into 6 to 8 ounces of water and drink it, as the label directs.
  2. Choose a time you will remember, such as with breakfast, so it becomes a daily habit.
  3. Give it time. Skin studies on collagen typically measured results after 8 to 12 weeks of daily use.
  4. Add sun protection and protein-rich meals. Daily sunscreen protects the collagen you already have.
  5. For joints, keep moving. Gentle regular exercise and a healthy weight matter as much as any supplement.

Is Collagen Refresh safe? Side effects

Collagen is generally well tolerated. The most common complaints with collagen supplements are mild, such as a feeling of fullness or mild digestive upset. Collagen Refresh contains no stimulants.

  • The label lists fish and eggs as allergens, and the collagen comes from beef, fish and eggshell membrane. Avoid it if you are allergic to any of them.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or taking prescription medicine, check with your doctor first, as the official site advises.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for joint pain that is severe, sudden, with swelling or redness, or that keeps getting worse.
