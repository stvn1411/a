What is Ennora and how is it meant to work?

Ennora is a small online shop for binaural beats meditation music, created, according to the site, by a professional sound engineer who has been producing recordings since 2009. Each track plays a slightly different tone in each ear. When you listen on stereo headphones, your brain perceives the difference between the two as a slow, pulsing beat. If the left ear hears 200 Hz and the right ear 205 Hz, for example, the beat you perceive is 5 Hz, which falls in the range linked with deep relaxation.

The idea is that your brainwaves tend to follow rhythmic sound, a process the site calls the frequency following response. The beats sit underneath gentle pads, piano and natural sounds, so the tracks work as ambient music as well as relaxation tools. The packs group tracks by goal: Sleep for winding down, Mind Power for focus, and Meditation, Mystic and Balance for stillness and reflection.

It is an approachable, low-effort way to add calm to your day. For a book-style routine that works well alongside evening listening, our Health Protocols review looks at a practical sleep and energy manual.

Do binaural beats like Ennora's work?

Binaural beats are a real, well-studied technique, and the findings are encouraging while still mixed:

  • A meta-analysis of 22 studies concluded that binaural beats can affect cognition, anxiety and pain perception, with the size and direction of the effect depending on the frequency used, how long people listened and when.[1]
  • The same analysis suggested that masking the beats with white or pink noise was not necessary for them to be effective, which suits Ennora's approach of layering beats under soft music.[1]
  • Not every experiment agrees: a later study found evidence against beta-frequency beats improving sustained attention, so it is worth treating focus claims with some caution.[2]

What this means for you: binaural beats are a reasonable, low-risk way to support relaxation and wind down, and Ennora presents them in a pleasant, easy format. The recordings themselves have not had a clinical trial, so use the free sample and the 60-day guarantee to find out how they suit you.

How to get the most out of Ennora

  1. Use stereo headphones. Each ear needs its own tone for the beat to form. You can try the free sample first.
  2. Match the track to the moment. Use focus tracks while working, and keep sleep tracks for lying down. The site says not to exercise to the sleep tracks or to drive while listening.
  3. Start with one or two sessions a day for the first week, as the seller suggests, using the same track at the same time.
  4. Listen up to an hour before bed with sleep tracks, in a dark, quiet room.
  5. Keep the basics in place: a regular bedtime, less caffeine late in the day and a calm room help any relaxation tool work better.
  6. Notice the change in how settled you feel after a week of regular listening.

Is Ennora safe? Side effects

Binaural beats are gentle audio, and most people use them without any problem. The site itself lists a few groups who should check first.

  • Check with a healthcare provider first if you are pregnant or nursing, have a heart condition or pacemaker, have a history of seizures or epilepsy, or are under 18.
  • Do not listen while driving or operating machinery, and keep volume at a comfortable level.
  • Meditation music is not a treatment for insomnia, anxiety or any other condition. See a professional for ongoing sleep problems, persistent anxiety or low mood.
