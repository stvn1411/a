What is Cardio Shield and how is it meant to work?

Cardio Shield is a capsule supplement sold with a short, personal guide to six everyday habits for healthy blood pressure: daily exercise, more potassium-rich food, stress management, less added sugar and refined carbs, and more berries. The formula is presented as the supplement step that sits on top of those habits.

The sales page says it combines hawthorn leaf, olive leaf extract, green tea extract and garlic with other ingredients. All four are familiar plant ingredients that researchers have looked at for blood pressure and circulation, which is a sensible foundation for a formula like this.

The full ingredient list and the amount of each are not shown on the sales page, so check the bottle label before buying if you have allergies or take medicine.

Do ingredients like those in Cardio Shield work?

The research on the named herbs is genuinely encouraging, especially for garlic, olive leaf and green tea:

  • A meta-analysis of 20 trials found garlic supplements lowered blood pressure compared with placebo, with a larger effect in people who started with high blood pressure.[2]
  • A meta-analysis of 12 studies found olive leaf extract lowered systolic blood pressure and triglycerides, with larger changes in people with hypertension.[3]
  • A meta-analysis of 13 trials found green tea lowered both systolic and diastolic blood pressure by a small but significant amount.[4]
  • For hawthorn, one randomized trial in people with type 2 diabetes saw a greater fall in diastolic pressure than placebo, while systolic pressure showed no group difference.[5]
  • The effects in these papers are modest and the studies vary in dose and quality, so these herbs are best seen as supportive, not as a substitute for medicine.[2]

What this means for you: Cardio Shield puts several well-known circulation herbs into one daily formula, and each named herb has published human research behind it. The finished formula has not published its own clinical trial, and the amounts are not listed on the sales page, so use it as a supportive extra alongside activity, diet and your doctor's advice.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest we see on supplements. Here is how the official terms work:

  1. The 180 days run from the date you originally ordered.
  2. Email support@getcardioshield.com with your order details written clearly so the team can match your return.
  3. Return unopened bottles, including any bonus bottles, to the fulfillment address you are given. The policy lets you keep opened bottles. Return postage is paid by you.
  4. After the fulfillment centre receives the package, your refund is issued and can take 3 to 7 business days to appear on your statement.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150. That leaves plenty of time to decide and to post any unopened bottles. Read the full refund policy on the site first, particularly the part on opened and unopened bottles.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, which also offers order support if you have a payment question.

How to get the most out of Cardio Shield

  1. Check the label. The directions and the full ingredient list are on the bottle; the sales page does not give them.
  2. Be consistent. The page itself suggests 60 to 90 days for the best effect, and many of the studies we cited ran for several weeks.
  3. Build the habits the page recommends: daily movement, whole foods with more potassium and less processed food, less added sugar and ways to manage stress.
  4. Keep checking your numbers. If you measure your blood pressure at home, keep doing so and share the readings with your doctor.
  5. Keep your check-ups and tell your doctor you are taking it.

Is Cardio Shield safe? Side effects

Cardio Shield is made from plant ingredients that are widely sold as supplements. Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1] The official page also advises that you should not take it if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription or over-the-counter medication, under 18, or have or suspect a medical condition.

  • If you take blood pressure, blood-thinning or other medicine, check with your doctor first. Herbs that support blood pressure may add to the effect of medicine.
  • Garlic can cause mild stomach upset or breath odour in some people.
  • Stop and speak to a professional if you feel dizzy, unwell or notice anything unusual.
  • See a doctor for chest pain, breathlessness, fainting or very high readings; these need urgent professional care, not a supplement.

Cardio Shield FAQ

Does Cardio Shield work?

Cardio Shield is built around hawthorn, olive leaf, green tea and garlic, which have published research for healthy blood pressure and circulation. The effects in studies are modest, the finished formula has not published its own clinical trial, and results vary from person to person.

Is Cardio Shield legit?

Yes. Cardio Shield is a real product sold through ClickBank, a large US online retailer, with published contact details and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Cardio Shield cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $59 plus $19.95 shipping, 3 bottles cost $147 and 6 bottles cost $234, with free US shipping on the larger packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.30 to $2.63 per day.

How long does Cardio Shield take to work?

The sales page says it usually takes 60 to 90 days to see the best effect and suggests at least 180 days. Many studies on the herbs it contains ran for several weeks.

How do I get a refund on Cardio Shield?

Within 180 days of ordering, email support@getcardioshield.com with your order details and return unopened bottles, including any bonus bottles. You may keep opened bottles, return postage is paid by you, and refunds can take 3 to 7 business days to show after the package is received.

Does Cardio Shield have side effects?
