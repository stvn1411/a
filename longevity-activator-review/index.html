What is Longevity Activator and how is it meant to work?

Longevity Activator is a multi-ingredient capsule from Zenith Labs, a Colorado-based supplement company. It is formulated by Dr. Ryan Shelton, N.D., and is built around the idea of supporting the body's natural housekeeping as we age.

The manufacturer's reasoning starts with cellular senescence: over time some cells stop dividing but linger in the body, and researchers are actively studying plant compounds that may influence them. Longevity Activator uses three of those compounds (EGCG, fisetin and resveratrol) as its first blend, then adds four ingredients traditionally linked with healthy ageing (the telomere support blend), a nine-ingredient booster blend and three absorption helpers.

It's worth being clear that senescence research is still young and much of it is done in the lab and in animals. We see Longevity Activator as a sensible, nutrition-based addition to a healthy lifestyle rather than a shortcut.

Does Longevity Activator work?

The finished capsule hasn't been tested on its own, but several of its ingredients have encouraging research behind them:

  • In lab and animal work, fisetin was the most potent of 10 flavonoids at reducing senescent cells, and it extended lifespan in aged mice.[2]
  • A 12-month, placebo-controlled trial in older adults found a curcumin formulation was associated with steadier cognitive scores than placebo on one test.[3]
  • A randomized trial found ashwagandha improved several measures of memory, attention and mood in healthy adults after 30 days.[4]
  • Panax ginseng showed dose-related benefits for mood and mental arithmetic in healthy volunteers.[5]

What this means for you: Longevity Activator gathers a strong cast of well-studied botanicals into one convenient capsule. Several of the human trials were small or ran in younger adults, and the fisetin findings are mainly from mice, so think of it as a supportive daily habit and give it a few months, as the manufacturer suggests.

Every order is covered by a 180-day guarantee, which is much longer than the usual 30 or 60 days. Here's how to use it if you need to:

Is Longevity Activator safe? Side effects

The ingredients are botanicals and nutrients that are commonly used in supplements and generally well tolerated by healthy adults. Mild stomach upset is the most common complaint with herbal capsules.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing or take prescription medicine, check with your doctor first. Ginseng, turmeric and ashwagandha can interact with some medicines, including blood thinners and diabetes or thyroid medication.
  • Dietary supplements aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • See a doctor for new or worsening symptoms, such as chest pain, memory problems or unexplained fatigue.
