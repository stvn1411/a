What is Aquaburn and how is it meant to work?

Aquaburn is a powdered drink mix that you stir into water each morning. It is built around the idea of thermogenesis, the body's natural process of producing heat and burning energy, and it combines a set of plant ingredients that the maker says support it.

The official page explains the idea through brown fat, a type of body fat that burns energy to make heat. Researchers have studied whether some plant compounds can nudge this process, and the maker lists the studies it relies on in its references. We read the human research on the best-known ingredients below; for several others the research is still mostly in the lab or in animals.

Alongside the metabolism ingredients you get a small detox-support blend (dandelion and milk thistle), a blend of seven fruit extracts for color, taste and antioxidants, and, on the bigger packages, three bonus guides covering meal timing, smoothies and healthy-ageing foods.

Do the ingredients in Aquaburn work?

The research behind Aquaburn's best-known ingredients is encouraging, if modest:

  • A meta-analysis of 15 trials with 1,243 people found green tea catechins taken with caffeine were linked to a small but statistically significant drop in body weight (about 1.4 kg versus caffeine alone) and waist size.[2]
  • A meta-analysis of 14 trials found ginger supplements reduced body weight and waist-to-hip ratio in overweight and obese adults, and also improved fasting glucose.[4]
  • In a small randomized crossover study of 10 men, a catechin and caffeine drink taken for five weeks raised cold-induced energy burning, which the authors linked to brown fat.[3]

What this means for you: Aquaburn puts several studied ingredients into one easy morning drink, and the research suggests small, supportive effects rather than dramatic ones. The finished formula has not had its own clinical trial, so think of it as a helper for a healthy eating and activity plan, and give it a few months.

How the 180-day money-back guarantee works

Every order is covered by a 180-day money-back guarantee, one of the longest we have seen, which makes Aquaburn easy to try properly. Here is how to use it if you need to:

  1. The 180 days start from the day your order ships.
  2. Email support@aquaburn.org with "Refund Request" in the subject line, and wait for confirmation from an agent before you return anything.
  3. Send all your bottles, empty or unopened, to the returns address on the official refund page, with a note giving your name, order number and order email. Return postage is paid by you; emailing the tracking ID speeds things up.
  4. Once the return is registered you are refunded in full, usually within 5 to 10 days.

Helpful tip: put a reminder in your calendar for around day 150. That leaves time to review your progress and still use the guarantee comfortably.

Orders are processed by ClickBank, a long-established US online retailer, and order questions can also go through ClickBank's customer support.

How to get the most out of Aquaburn

  1. Take it in the morning. Stir one scoop into a glass of water or a favorite drink, as the maker suggests.
  2. Be consistent. The maker suggests ideally 3 to 6 months of daily use, and the research on similar ingredients typically ran for weeks to months.
  3. Build the basics around it: balanced meals, regular activity and good sleep do most of the work, and Aquaburn is there to support them.
  4. Use the bonus guides that come with the 3- and 6-bottle packages for meal timing and smoothie ideas.
  5. Track simple measures such as energy, waist size and how your clothes fit, every week or two, so you can judge progress honestly.
  6. Avoid doubling up with other supplements that contain similar ingredients, as the maker advises.

Is Aquaburn safe? Side effects

Aquaburn is described by the maker as a non-stimulant, non-habit-forming formula with no known allergens. It is made of plant extracts, and these are generally well tolerated in food-like amounts, but the amounts in this formula are not listed.

  • If you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medicine or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before starting, as the maker also advises.
  • If you are sensitive to green tea, ginger, black pepper extract or ginseng, check first. The caffeine content is not stated.
  • Dietary supplements are not reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale, which is true of every supplement.[1]
  • Stop and speak to a professional if you notice stomach upset that does not settle, a rash or any other symptom that worries you.

How Aquaburn compares with other options

  • Green tea or green tea extract on its own: the best-studied single ingredient in Aquaburn, usually cheaper, but one ingredient rather than a blend.
  • Other metabolism powders and capsules: many list exact doses for every ingredient, which suits buyers who want to match a product to specific studies. Aquaburn's strengths are its drink-mix format, price per day on the larger packages and the 180-day guarantee.
  • Food and lifestyle changes: nothing replaces balanced meals, activity and sleep, and supplements work best on top of them.

Aquaburn FAQ

Does Aquaburn work?

Aquaburn combines ingredients such as green tea, ginger, fucoxanthin and piperine that have been studied in connection with energy use and body weight. Research on green tea with caffeine and on ginger shows modest effects, and the finished formula has not had its own clinical trial. Most people do best using it daily for a few months alongside balanced eating and regular activity, and results vary from person to person.

Is Aquaburn legit?

Yes. Aquaburn is a real product sold through ClickBank, with published support and refund policies, a stated US manufacturing facility and a 180-day money-back guarantee.

How much does Aquaburn cost?

On the official site at the time of our review: 1 bottle costs $79 plus $9.95 shipping, 3 bottles cost $59 each ($177) and 6 bottles cost $39 each ($234), with free US shipping on the larger packages. Each bottle lasts 30 days, so the cost is roughly $1.30 to $2.97 per day.

How do I get a refund on Aquaburn?

Within 180 days of your order shipping, email support@aquaburn.org with 'Refund Request' in the subject line and wait for confirmation. Then send back all bottles, empty or unopened, with a note giving your name, order number and email. Does Aquaburn have side effects?

Final verdict: is Aquaburn worth it?
