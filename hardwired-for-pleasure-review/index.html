What is Hardwired For Pleasure and how is it meant to work?

Hardwired For Pleasure is a program of two guided audio sessions. You put on headphones, relax and follow the voice, in a style of guided relaxation usually called hypnosis. Nothing is swallowed, applied or worn.

The creator, Lloyd Lester, explains the idea through the picture of a "control tower" in the brain that sends the signals involved in arousal, and the audios aim to calm performance worry and strengthen a confident, relaxed mindset. We want to be clear about what is established and what is the seller's own explanation: it is well documented that hypnosis changes patterns of brain activity,[1] and that psychological support can help men whose erection difficulties are linked to stress and confidence.[2] The specific "control tower" explanation on the sales page is the seller's own framing.

The program is made of two audios, Identity (confidence and self-image) and Mastery (relaxation, focus and sensitivity), plus three guides that cover couple exercises, recovery habits and a size-focused method.

Does a hypnosis audio program like Hardwired For Pleasure work?

Two kinds of research are relevant, and both are encouraging for the mind-side approach:

  • In a functional MRI study of 57 people, hypnosis was linked to reduced activity in a brain region involved in worry and self-monitoring and to stronger links between areas that support focus and body awareness.[1] This supports hypnosis as a real, measurable state of focused relaxation.
  • In a randomized trial of 30 men with mild to moderate psychogenic erection difficulties, men who had group psychotherapy, alone or with sildenafil, reported higher satisfaction, confidence and a more natural feel in sexual activity after six months than men who took sildenafil alone.[2]
  • Neither study tested Hardwired For Pleasure, and the second involved therapy rather than hypnosis, so it supports the principle rather than this exact program.

What this means for you: if stress, worry or low confidence play a part in your experience, working on the mind side is a sensible, research-supported direction, and a guided audio is a gentle, private way to do it. The program has not had its own clinical trial, so give it a few weeks of regular listening and use the 60-day guarantee to judge for yourself.

Is Hardwired For Pleasure safe? Side effects

  • Erection difficulties can sometimes be linked to heart health, diabetes, blood pressure, hormones or medication, so a check-up is worthwhile, especially if they began suddenly.
  • Do not listen while driving or doing anything that needs attention, as the audios are meant to relax you.
  • Do not stop or change prescribed medicine on your own. The seller says the program can be used alongside medication, and your doctor can advise you.
  • See a doctor for chest pain, numbness, painful or prolonged erections or any new symptom.
