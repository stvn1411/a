What is Balmorex and how is it meant to work?

Balmorex is a cream you apply directly to the areas that feel sore or stiff, such as the lower back, knees, shoulders or hands. Because it is used on the skin, it is a different kind of product from the pills and capsules that fill most of the joint-support shelf.

The formula combines a sulfur compound (MSM), two plant extracts with a long history of traditional use for sore joints and muscles (arnica and Indian frankincense), warming ginger, soothing aloe vera and moisturising oils and butters, plus Epsom salt. The maker describes it as quick-acting and non-greasy, so you can apply it and get on with your day.

The sales page lists no application directions, so we suggest following the label on the jar you receive and applying a small amount to clean skin.

Does a cream like Balmorex work?

Research on individual ingredients gives us good reasons to be positive:

  • Topical arnica gel matched ibuprofen gel for pain and hand function over 21 days in a randomized, double-blind trial of 204 people with hand osteoarthritis.[2]
  • MSM taken by mouth for 12 weeks improved physical function and total WOMAC scores compared with placebo in a trial of 49 adults with knee osteoarthritis.[3]
  • A Boswellia serrata extract taken by mouth reduced knee pain and swelling compared with placebo in a crossover trial of 30 patients.[4]

What this means for you: Balmorex brings together several ingredients with research behind them in one easy cream. The finished product has not had its own clinical trial, and some studies used oral forms, so treat it as a comfort aid and give it a few weeks of regular use to see how it suits you.

How to get the most out of Balmorex

  1. Follow the directions on the jar you receive; the sales page doesn't list them.
  2. Apply to clean, dry skin and massage in gently until absorbed.
  3. Be consistent for a few weeks before judging, as the maker suggests giving the cream a fair chance.
  4. Patch-test first: try a small area of skin before wider use, and avoid broken skin or the eyes.
  5. Keep moving: gentle stretching, regular activity and good posture work well alongside any cream.

Is Balmorex safe? Side effects

Balmorex is a topical cream made from plant-based ingredients, and the maker describes it as safe to use alongside other pain relievers. We could not find a published safety study on the finished product, so we suggest sensible precautions.

  • The most common issue with any topical product is skin irritation. Stop use if redness, itching or a rash appears.
  • If you are allergic to plants in the daisy family, check the ingredient list carefully, as arnica belongs to that family.
  • If you are pregnant, nursing or taking prescription medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
  • Dietary and wellness products aren't reviewed by the FDA for effectiveness before sale.[1]
  • See a doctor for severe, sudden or persistent pain, swelling, numbness or any pain after an injury.
